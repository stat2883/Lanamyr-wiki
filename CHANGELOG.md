# Lanamyr World Bible — Changelog

A record of how the world itself has changed, version by version, newest first.

This tracks **lore and story**: what was decided, what was revised, what was
resolved, and why. It is not a file-by-file record of edits — git history
already covers that. Workflow and structural changes are noted briefly at the
end of an entry when relevant, not at the top.

---

## v002

### Revised — the origin of the Marauders

Previously the Marauders were an elite unit that Mya Li hand-picked and
purpose-built for the final incursion into Dark Elf territory. That has been
replaced.

The unit is now the product of a **council of war** held after Mya's crushing
first defeat and the death of Kharis Ailwin. Mya returned to Faelyn accompanied
by Adomorn, and the council — Aer'Raenal, Adomorn, Mya, and Faelyn's tacticians
and strategists — concluded that Faelyn could not keep absorbing attacks and
had to go on the offensive. Mya volunteered to lead it.

She did not choose the ten. She told her peers and fellow soldiers plainly what
the mission was: dangerous, very likely fatal, and in her heart the best chance
of saving Faelyn. She was prepared to fight to the death. Anyone who wished to
come was welcome, and anyone who stayed behind would carry no blame and no ill
will from her. Ten came forward, all understanding it was likely a suicide
mission, their love of kingdom, family, and friends outweighing their fear.

The change shifts the emotional weight of the unit considerably. They are no
longer soldiers selected for a job; they are people who were asked and said yes
knowing the cost.

### Added — the turn to the offensive

Established as confirmed lore that Diquyk's two-front campaign kept Faelyn
entirely on the defensive from 387 to 392 AW, and that the council of war is the
moment that posture breaks. This reaches back into the early war years and gives
those four years a shape they previously lacked.

### Added — Adomorn at the council

Adomorn returned to Faelyn with Mya and sat the council of war. He did not
propose or select the unit.

### Resolved — who proposed the Marauders

The open question of whether Mya advocated for the unit herself or Adomorn
suggested it is now answered: neither. The offensive was a council decision, and
Mya volunteered to lead it.

### Corrected — three long-standing inconsistencies

- **Grimmon Herringer's age at death.** His character entry said "would have
  been 60," contradicting the timeline. Born 372 AW, died 429 AW — he was 57.
  Corrected to match the timeline.
- **Scavenger Kobold lifespan.** Described as "second shortest of all Kobold
  subtypes" at 35 years, but with Mystics at 50 and both Tunnelers and
  Hardbacks at 40, 35 is the shortest. Wording corrected.
- **Cobalt Dragonborne origin.** `Races` placed the Desolate Tops in eastern
  Utoa while `Geography` placed them in central Utoa. Central is correct;
  `Races` corrected. Eastern Utoa holds the Evergreen Strand, origin of the
  Hardback Kobolds.

### Still open

- The identities of the ten. Names, backgrounds, and whether any had served
  under Mya before or knew Kharis.
- How the council scene plays — whether Mya's volunteering is expected, and how
  Aer'Raenal responds to his daughter volunteering for something the room knows
  is likely fatal.
- Faelyn is described in `Kingdoms_History` as occupying the "Eastern and lower
  western arm of Alios," which sits oddly against everything else placing the
  Wood Elves in the western arm. Possibly a typo for "southern," possibly
  deliberate. Unresolved.

- Added this changelog.
