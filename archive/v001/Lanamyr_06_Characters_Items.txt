ADOMORN THE IMMORTAL
True name: Divinos. Last surviving Drasu. Granted immortality by Ah'Tabai in
the final moments before Ah'Tabai was fully claimed by the Abaculus — the last
act of a god of Resurrection and Reincarnation fighting a losing battle with
full knowledge of what was happening to him. True form: Drasu — taller than
any present-day race, bronze-skinned, lean and powerful. Does not age — his
body is frozen at the physical state it was in when the gift was granted.
Chooses to appear as an old, wise human — a deliberate projection of wisdom
and trustworthiness rather than power or marvel. Can take other forms if he
chooses but is most widely known in his current human form. Galstaff Quint is
one of the rare few to have seen his true Drasu form.
IMMORTALITY MECHANICS: His physical body can be killed, but it will fade and
disappear after some undecided period. He then rematerializes at Atral — the
precise location where Ah'Tabai granted the gift. He can be removed from a
situation temporarily but never permanently by conventional or magical means.
KNOWN PERMANENT THREATS: Only two things can permanently kill Adomorn —
the Abaculus, and an Onwu Irridae. All other means of killing him are
temporary. Diquyk does not yet know the specifics of Adomorn's immortality.
ADOMORN AND THE HIGH ELVES: His rematerialization point being Atral — which
the High Elves were actively excavating — is the likely explanation for how
that relationship began. Encountering a Drasu suddenly materializing in the
ruins of the greatest Drasian city would have been an extraordinary event.
Has been searching for the Abaculus since 2165 by tracking Olum movement. Every
lead is legitimate but turns up only natural nesting behavior. The Burnt Plains
is a lifeless dead zone — the one place his methodology gives him no reason to
visit. Aware of Relics of Creation (physical and abstract) from the Drasian
Vault. Would not share unless absolutely necessary. Knew many Kings and Queens
of Harrad. Was present at Battle of Rohl. Knew Morgan personally. Fought
Diquyk alongside Mya and the Marauders. Does NOT know Diquyk has returned.
Does NOT know the Abaculus is beneath the Burnt Plains.
Resides at Tower of Grannel. Still sees Mya Li on occasion.
Is the only living being capable of safely moving the Abaculus — Irridae and
Drasu magic are the only known types that can forcibly move it without physical
contact, bypassing corruption.
Also searching for a way to free Karma from Greyhawk's curse. Has not yet
found a means.
CONTACTS WITHIN THE VAR HARMA: Has contact only with the highest ranks of
the Var Harma. Likely became involved either by being present when they
discovered the Vault, or by guiding them to it — undecided. Lower ranking
members such as Maeglin likely do not know of his involvement or possibly
who he is.
Has passed through the Monowi Inn on the road between Fardar and Faelyn at
some point. Elsie Varis treated him like any other traveler, unaware of who
he was.
ELF WAR: Contacted by the Wood Elves after Mya's first crushing defeat against
Diquyk's forces. Based on Mya's description of Diquyk's abilities, suspected
him to be a Magni — an identification that shaped his tactical planning.
However, Adomorn did not anticipate how far Diquyk's powers had developed,
nor the full consequence of Diquyk's ability to intercept and redirect magic.
Diquyk is possibly the most powerful Magni ever born, eclipsing even Arias
Graymist. This gap in Adomorn's intelligence contributed to the cost of the
final battle.
TENTATIVE — DEATH AT END OF ELF WAR: It is tentatively considered that
Adomorn suffered a physical death immediately after banishing Diquyk in 392 AW
— spending everything he had in the final confrontation and succumbing to his
wounds shortly after. His body would have faded and he would eventually have
rematerialized at Atral. Mya Li, who knew him only as a powerful legendary
wizard and was unaware of his true nature or his immortality, would have
believed him dead. NOT YET CONFIRMED.

DIQUYK LOCKWOOD
PRONUNCIATION: "Die Quick"
Born 352 AW. Hillfolk of central Alios. Frail from birth. Age 80 by Lanamyr
reckoning in 432 AW (appears ageless). PHYSICAL AGE: approximately 46 — see
banishment entry below. Post-corruption appearance: pale blue skin, featureless
white orb eyes that glow faintly. Wears worn, layered practical clothing. A
reference image exists: small blue flame held in an outstretched hand, gazing
down at it with stillness rather than menace.
NOTE FOR UNLIT: In Chapter 1 he would appear as a frail, unremarkable
fourteen-year-old Hillfolk boy — dark-haired, before corruption changed him.
The contrast between that boy and the corrupted figure is one of the most
powerful elements of the story.
At 14 (366 AW): accidentally killed a guard and both parents with blue flame.
Taken in by Degeric Cent. Mentored ~10 years.
~376 AW: Parts from Degeric. Begins creating Jaunt Gates.
379-381 AW: Visited Degeric one last time — still pre-corruption. Degeric dies
  381 AW (age 48). Never knew what his student became.
383 AW: Drawn to the Burnt Plains by curiosity — a patch of dead earth that had
  defied natural recovery for centuries, receiving normal rainfall yet supporting
  no life. Having no prior knowledge of the battle that caused it, he investigated.
  The Abaculus, sensing his power, guided him to the hidden entry point among
  the battlefield debris — a narrow burrow he could barely squeeze into — and
  influenced him through it into Greyhawk's purpose-built chamber beneath. He
  experienced this as his own curiosity and determination, unaware he was being
  drawn in. Corrupted on contact with the Abaculus. Left the Burnt Plains
  immediately after his corruption — remaining there served no purpose for the
  Abaculus.
PRE-WAR ACTIVITY (383-387 AW): In the four years between his corruption and
  the start of the Elf War, Diquyk recruited the two forces that would prosecute
  it. He visited the Stone Giants of the Tempest Peaks first — travelling to
  the mountains directly north of Faelyn and demonstrating his overwhelming
  magical power as a show of force. He offered the Giants plunder and the riches
  of Faelyn. The Giants, who respect strength above all else, were persuaded.
  Diquyk promised to return with additional forces before the attack began.
  He then travelled to The Deep beneath the Tarantar Mountains and recruited
  the Dark Elves — chosen for their obscurity, their lack of outside alliances,
  their deniability, and their absence of any existing relationship with Faelyn.
  He equipped them with magical and enchanted weapons. Both races were recruited
  separately. During the early years of the Elf War, Diquyk directed operations
  from between the two kingdoms via Jaunt Gates — not personally present on
  the front lines. He placed gates near both the Tempest Peaks and The Deep to
  move rapidly between them as needed.
ELF WAR (387-392 AW): The early years (387-391 AW) consisted of a two-front
  assault — mixed Dark Elf and Giant units probing from the north through the
  Tempest Peaks and from the east out of The Deep. Diquyk tested Wood Elf
  defenses and learned what he was up against from a distance.
  In 392 AW Diquyk made his first major push — the first large encounter, which
  was also Mya's most crushing defeat and learning experience. She underestimated
  the scale of the threat. Dark Elves and Giants under Diquyk's command carried
  magical and enchanted weapons. Diquyk killed a multitude of soldiers before
  the Wood Elves retreated. Mya survived, reported back. This report led to
  Adomorn being contacted. The Marauders were formed and the final battle
  occurred within the same year — 392 AW.
392 AW: Banished at the end of the Elf War at physical age 40. See banishment
  details below.
427 AW: Returns to Lanamyr through the Nexus on Myst (age 80 by Lanamyr
  reckoning, physically ~41 due to temporal difference of banishment plane).
  Finds his old notes, study, and places he visited on Myst either abandoned
  and covered in years of dust or drastically changed. A brief flicker of
  genuine feeling surfaces — not yet vengeance, but its raw material: hurt and
  fear. The feelings of someone cast out while life continued without him. The
  Abaculus pushes those feelings aside quickly, repurposing rather than
  suppressing them. He is now focused entirely on his mission.
BANISHMENT: Diquyk was banished to a separate plane of existence — not the
  same plane used by the Jaunt Gates, which is a featureless void. The
  banishment plane is a parallel physical world with its own features and
  characteristics — an entirely different world existing alongside the plane
  Lanamyr resides in. Features and characteristics undeveloped. Critically,
  time moves more slowly there relative to Lanamyr. Diquyk experienced
  approximately 1 year in banishment while 35 years passed in Lanamyr. He
  spent that subjective year working to find a way back. He succeeded by
  creating a gate from the banishment realm back to the Nexus.
  PHYSICAL AGE CONSEQUENCE: Banished at physical age 40, experienced ~1 year,
  returned physically ~41. Five years have since passed. Physical age in 432 AW
  is approximately 46 — a man in the physical prime of his life, despite being
  80 by Lanamyr's calendar.
  WHO KNOWS ABOUT THE TIME DIFFERENTIAL: Undecided whether anyone knew when
  banishing him. Adomorn possibly knew. No one else except now Diquyk himself.
  The Irridae and the Abaculus also know.
MAGICAL ABILITIES: Senses others drawing on ambient magical energy, intercepts
  it, steals the spell mid-cast, stores it as blue flame. More stored = more
  devastating release. Difficulty scales with opponent's power — Adomorn was the
  hardest. Can also draw from ambient energy directly. During Elf War, equipped
  Dark Elf forces with magical and enchanted weapons. Is possibly the most
  powerful Magni ever born, eclipsing even Arias Graymist. Adomorn suspected
  he was a Magni from Mya's description after the first defeat, but did not
  anticipate how far his powers had developed. Diquyk's ability to sense others
  drawing on ambient energy is what detected Adomorn's gate transit during the
  final battle — foiling the ambush. NOTE: The white-blue burst of DQ's power
  during the incident at age 14 is the same blue flame that defines his magic
  throughout his life — visible from the very first expression of his ability.
ULTIMATE GOAL: Ascend to godhood → wield Abaculus against all Irridae →
  Abaculus would then turn on him too. Does NOT know he was beneath the Burnt
  Plains when corrupted or that Karma sits above the Abaculus. Operates alone.
  Currently working to find the mechanism to resurrect Greyhawk into Karma.
REVENGE MOTIVE: A significant personal driver is revenge against Adomorn.
  This motive has a genuine root in Diquyk's true self — the original feelings
  were hurt and fear at being cast out, losing his place in the world, and
  watching decades pass without him. The Abaculus did not suppress these
  feelings but twisted them: hurt became grievance, fear became the need to
  dominate. The drive for vengeance is authentically Diquyk's in origin, which
  makes it more dangerous and more tragic than pure manipulation. Ascending to
  godhood and wielding the Abaculus is, in his distorted mind, also an act of
  personal retribution. Adomorn is unaware Diquyk has returned and has no idea
  he is the focus of this rage.
DIQUYK'S TRUE SELF: A slight flicker of the original Diquyk survives beneath
  the corruption — the return to Lanamyr briefly surfaced something genuine:
  hurt and fear rather than rage. However, by 432 AW the Abaculus has a nearly
  total and unbreakable hold on him. Whether that flicker can ever be reached
  — and whether the story will offer him redemption or only death — remains an
  open question. Current lean: Diquyk is killed while still a tool of the
  Abaculus, making his story ultimately a tragedy rather than a redemption arc.

MYADONNA LIAPHYRA (MYA LI)
Born 347 AW, Caldalus, Faelyn. Age 85 in 432 AW. Age 85 falls in her prime
by Wood Elf standards (prime: 62–150) — equivalent to a human in their early 30s.
Wood Elf princess, daughter of King Aer'Raenal Liaphyra.
APPEARANCE: Green eyes (not common among Wood Elves), pointed ears, dark hair.
Height: 5'2" (average for Wood Elf females). Two reference images exist:
Image 1 (civilian): Simple grey blouse, wide leather belt, cream skirt.
  Mya before the war — as she might appear in Chapter 2 of Unlit.
Image 2 (warrior): Ornate green and gold armor, weapon over her shoulder,
  looking back over her shoulder — alert, never entirely at rest. This is the
  Mya who led the Marauders.
The black and white ink artwork (jumping with the bow) remains the closest
accurate depiction of her actual battle outfit and armor.
Weapons: Crimson (soft red glow), Sear (soft white-yellow glow),
  Bow of the Dales (purple-pink energy bolts, no conventional ammo).
Bow of the Dales was created by Diquyk for Shedyak; acquired by Mya after
  killing Shedyak in the final battle of the Elf War.
EARLY LIFE (confirmed lore for Unlit): Originally intended to become queen of
Faelyn. Scholarly in youth — studied the history of Faelyn with that purpose.
Capable and diligent but restless, increasingly bored. Phelloden was already
present and more enthusiastic about his studies. Mya's path to the militia
began with a growing restlessness that accumulated over time — trade meetings
with foreign emissaries she found deadening, books she drifted away from toward
tales of heroes, militia members she watched from behind a pillar and envied
their camaraderie. The throne was never imminent given Aer'Raenal's long years
ahead. Phelloden as heir is Mya's DELIBERATE CHOICE — a conversation between
them made it real and spoken.
ELF WAR (387-392 AW): Joined militia at age 40, prompted by the beginning of
Stone Giant attacks on the outskirts of Faelyn — the final tipping point after
a long accumulation. The early years of the war (387-391 AW) consisted of a
two-front assault — mixed Dark Elf and Giant units pressing from the north
through the Tempest Peaks and from the east out of The Deep. In 392 AW Diquyk
made his first major push — the first large encounter, which was also Mya's
most crushing defeat and learning experience. She underestimated the scale of
the threat. Dark Elves and Giants under Diquyk's command carried magical and
enchanted weapons. Diquyk killed a multitude of soldiers before the Wood Elves
retreated. Mya survived, reported back. This report led to Adomorn being
contacted. Her father Aer'Raenal urged her to seek out Adomorn — a figure of
great and ancient reputation. Mya knew him only as a powerful legendary wizard;
she was entirely unaware of his true Drasu nature or his immortality. The
Marauders were formed and the final battle occurred within the same year — 392 AW.
MYA'S FIRST LOVE INTEREST: Kharis Ailwin. Wood Elf, born 347 AW — the same
age as Mya. Joined the militia at essentially the same time as Mya — he was
among the survivors of a Stone Giant attack on a small Wood Elf village, part
of the group that fled to Caldalus to deliver word of the attack. His family
was killed in that attack. This is his primary motivation for joining. Both
showed great promise and skill from the start. Over their first year they built
deep trust, going on missions together. In their second year they became serious,
though not openly — their roles demanded professionalism and the relationship
was kept quiet. Their closest friends knew. Kharis was killed in the first
major encounter with Diquyk's forces in 392 AW at age 45 — young by Wood Elf
standards, barely past young adulthood. Because the relationship was never
publicly acknowledged, Mya could not grieve him openly. She absorbed that loss,
helped form the Marauders, and led them into the final battle within the same
year — losing every one of them too. All of it within 392 AW.
KHARIS AILWIN APPEARANCE: Height 5'7" (average for Wood Elf males). Light brown
hair. Brown eyes.
THE MARAUDERS were formed specifically for the final incursion into Dark Elf
territory — a purpose-built elite unit assembled after Mya had already
experienced that loss and learned from it. They are NOT the unit present in
the first encounter with Diquyk's forces.
Mya's first love interest (Kharis Ailwin) was killed in that earlier encounter,
along with several other men under her command.
TOTAL STRENGTH: 10 Marauders.
POST-WAR IDENTITY: In the aftermath of the Elf War Mya experienced a period
of rootlessness and doubt — questioning whether she had traded something she
couldn't get back when she stepped aside from the throne. Those feelings
resolved over the course of the events leading to Tarik's overthrow and through
meeting Balric. By 432 AW she is firmly comfortable and confident in her
present role. Phelloden flourishing as heir is a quiet confirmation that she
made the right choice.
MOTIVATION: Pure compassion and a refusal to stand by while others suffer. She
represents the best of what a person can be.
Deep bond with Adomorn. Good friends with Gralvin Sutton.
Travels with Balric Silverfox only (just the two of them).
Has passed through the Monowi Inn at some point. Elsie Varis treated her like
any other traveler, unaware of who she was.

AER'RAENAL LIAPHYRA
Born 244 AW. Age 188 in 432 AW. King of the Wood Elves. Rules from Caldalus.
Father of Mya Li and Phelloden. Gifted Mya Crimson and Sear.
AGE CONTEXT (Wood Elf lifespan 250 years): Was 103 when Mya was born (347 AW)
— well within his reproductive years (50–125) and late in his prime (62–150).
Was 113 when Phelloden was born (357 AW) — still within reproductive years.
At 188 in 432 AW: solidly in Wood Elf middle age (150–200). Experienced,
established, still vigorous but with the weight of years on him.
At the time of Mya-1 and Mya-2 (pre-387 AW), Aer'Raenal is approximately 140
years old — solidly in his prime, with many decades ahead of him. The throne
is not imminent for either Mya or Phelloden, which removes competitive pressure
between the siblings.
Dispatched Mya to help with Tarik's overthrow after meeting Grimmon Herringer
and a Plains Elf emissary. After Mya's first crushing defeat against Diquyk's
forces, urged her to seek out Adomorn — recognizing the threat was beyond
anything the Wood Elves could handle alone. Currently alive in 432 AW.

PHELLODEN LIAPHYRA
Born 357 AW. Age 75 in 432 AW — just entering his prime by Wood Elf standards
(prime: 62–150). Son of King Aer'Raenal. Younger brother of Mya Li by 10 years.
More enthusiastic about his studies than Mya — maps, treaties, the history of
Faelyn's relationships with neighboring kingdoms. Next in line to lead the Wood
Elves of Faelyn — a deliberate choice by Mya, not circumstance. Mya and
Phelloden have a warm relationship with genuine mutual respect. There was never
rivalry or competition between them. Phelloden recognizes Mya's potential and
would have had no issue if she had chosen the throne. They each understand where
their strengths lie relative to the other. Humor and teasing exist naturally
between them. Currently alive.

GRALVIN SUTTON
Born 387 AW. Age 45 in 432 AW. King of Kimora. The Reluctant King.
Village destroyed by Tarik at age 7 (394 AW). Taken as a gladiatorial slave.
14 years in the arena. Escaped 408 AW alongside Balric Silverfox (from different
villages, met within the slave system — specific circumstances undecided).
Directed to Valeria shortly after. Gralvin and Valeria became friends quickly,
then lovers. He saw her pure heart. He and Balric chose to stay and help her
network for several years, gathering worsening firsthand accounts.
When Mya Li arrived — pointed toward Valeria by the same network — everything
crystallized. Valeria died in the final battle. Gralvin took the throne at age
26, reluctantly. Good friends with Mya. Working to mend Kimora-Fardar relations.
Tarik's former followers remain a threat. Valeria's presence occasionally
manifests supernaturally.

VALERIA
Died 413 AW in the final battle of Tarik's overthrow. Occasionally manifests
supernaturally in Gralvin's life. Degree and nature of presence: UNDECIDED.
APPEARANCE: Long blonde hair. Slender yet powerful build. Striking blue-gray
eyes. Voice soft and deep. PERSONALITY: Mischievous and warm — quick with a
friendly barb or teasing remark. Had no love for authority. Principled defiance
that she wore lightly. Ran the informal escape network for years with Gralvin
and Balric. When Mya arrived, Valeria recognized immediately what it could mean.

BALRIC SILVERFOX
Born 385 AW. Age 47 in 432 AW. Human. Two years older than Gralvin.
Enslaved by Tarik as a manual laborer — NOT a gladiator. From a different village
than Gralvin. Met within the slave system (specific circumstances undecided).
Escaped together with Gralvin in 408 AW. Part of Valeria's network for several
years. Present for Tarik's overthrow in 413 AW. After Valeria's death, remained
close to Gralvin in early years of his reign. At some point became Mya Li's
traveling companion and love interest — circumstances undecided. Currently
adventuring with Mya. It is just the two of them. No physical description or
personality details yet established.

SKITTERSKINK (SKITTER)
Mystic Kobold cleric — one of the rare individuals of his race with magical
ability. His tribe raided a small farm, burning several buildings. Skitter found
a small acorn completely unaffected by the fire in the charred wreckage. Tried
to break, smash, and burn it — nothing worked. Placed it in a glass vial. Wears
it around his neck. Has no idea it is a Relic of Creation.
Galstaff encountered Skitter and sensed remarkable power from the vial.
Current companions in 432 AW: Bor Glitterbeard and Maeglin Leralonde.
Prior connections through travel: Wilward Cobbersplint, Alexa Komoth, and
Qenwick Orcsbane — shared the Adenburry sinkhole event in 430 AW.
Was traveling with Wilward, Alexa, and Maeglin aboard Wilward's airship when
they landed in Adenburry in 430 AW. Fought alongside the others to exterminate
the subterranean Olum nest. Afterward traveled with Maeglin, Bor, and Qenwick
to inform Herringer members across Kimora of Grimmon's death.
OPEN QUESTION: Which Irridae does Skitter worship? When and how did Galstaff
encounter him? Undecided.

MAEGLIN LERALONDE
Born 354 AW. Age 78 in 432 AW — solidly within his prime by High Elf standards
(prime: 55–132 years). High Elf male from T'sarra. Noble family. Father was a
caretaker of elven artifacts. Inducted into the Var Harma.
APPEARANCE: Dark brown hair. Brown eyes. Height approximately 5'9" — notably
taller than average for High Elf males.
Character traits: Obsession with knowledge, fair and open-minded but lets
emotions cloud judgement around aloof people, has grown sterner since becoming
a renowned assassin of the Var Harma.
First solo expedition: retrieved a ceremonial gold hammer from a ruined Dwarven
temple to Dey near the Troll's Breath Mountains. First met Bor there. Gave Bor
the hammer in exchange for access to Bor's map hinting at a hidden entrance to
a lost dwarven kingdom. Initially partnered with Bor for the opportunity and
potential discoveries the quest might yield. They have since become genuinely
good friends.
Was traveling with Wilward, Skitter, and Alexa aboard Wilward's airship when
they landed in Adenburry in 430 AW. Fought alongside the others to exterminate
the subterranean Olum nest. Afterward traveled with Bor, Skitter, and Qenwick
to inform Herringer members across Kimora of Grimmon's death.
Travels with Bor and Skitter in 432 AW.

BOR GLITTERBEARD
Born 399 AW. Age 33 in 432 AW — just entering his prime by Iron Dwarf standards
(prime: 27–66 years). Iron Dwarf from Fardar. Battlerager fighting style.
APPEARANCE: Reddish-orange hair. Hazel eyes.
Carries the deep cultural history of his people — two major periods of Iron
Dwarf enslavement (Bourbac, then Tarik) have left a lingering distrust of humans.
Cousin of Qenwick Orcsbane — their fathers are brothers.
Grimmon Herringer recognized Bor as a kindred spirit and showed him the map of
the lost kingdom, sharing his plans and knowledge of what lay in the Tarantar
Mountains. After Grimmon's death in 429 AW, Bor obtained the map from Grimmon's
belongings. The map passed through several generations of Herringers before
Grimmon — its ultimate origins are undeveloped.
Bor holds the map. Maeglin has access to it but does not possess it. The map
gives only an approximate location and hints at a hidden entrance.
Bor and the Herringers are aware of multiple versions of the legend of the lost
kingdom's downfall. Dark Elf involvement is considered one of the more likely
explanations. Bor does not yet know that Creatures of Olum were the true cause.
Was traveling overland from Fardar into Kimora with Qenwick when they were
present at the Adenburry sinkhole event in 430 AW. Fought alongside the others
to exterminate the subterranean Olum nest. Afterward traveled with Maeglin,
Skitter, and Qenwick to inform Herringer members across Kimora of Grimmon's
death.
Travels with Maeglin and Skitter in 432 AW.

QENWICK ORCSBANE
Iron Dwarf paladin from Fardar. Cousin of Bor Glitterbeard — their fathers are
brothers. Was traveling overland from Fardar into Kimora with Bor when they
were present at the Adenburry sinkhole event in 430 AW. Fought alongside the
others to exterminate the subterranean Olum nest. Afterward traveled with
Maeglin, Bor, and Skitter to inform Herringer members across Kimora of Grimmon's
death. After the news was delivered, returned to Fardar.
Currently alive in Fardar as of 432 AW. Prior connections through the Adenburry
event and subsequent travels: Bor, Maeglin, Skitter, Wilward, and Alexa.

GRIMMON HERRINGER
Born 372 AW. Died 429 AW (killed by Orc raiders — instruments of the
Kimora-framing operation). Would have been 60. At approximately age 36-40,
personally traveled to Ellendale to plan coordinated overthrow of Tarik. Drew
up simultaneous strike plans with the Plains Elves. Then traveled with a Plains
Elf emissary to Faelyn, told King Aer'Raenal and Mya firsthand stories of
Tarik's atrocities. Mya was deeply moved and volunteered to help. Was 41 at
Tarik's overthrow in 413 AW and present for the culmination. Founded Herrington.
APPEARANCE: Dark brown hair. Blue eyes.
Ambition: new Dwarven kingdom in the Tarantar Mountains. Said to be Arrama
Herringer reborn.
Possessed the map of the lost kingdom — passed down through several generations
of Herringers before him. The map and the legend of the lost kingdom were part
of what drove his ambition to establish a new Dwarven kingdom in the Tarantar
Mountains. Recognized Bor as a kindred spirit and shared the map and his
knowledge with him personally.

TARIK BOLDEN (TARIK THE RAVAGER)
Born 355 AW. Took throne of Kimora 386 AW (age 31). Died 413 AW (age 58).
Spent years building support before taking the throne. Immediately began raiding
villages and enslaving populations — both Hillfolk humans and Iron Dwarves from
Fardar. Enslaved humans used as gladiators and laborers. Iron Dwarves built the
Palace of Shatazaar. Those too injured to work were executed. Tarik's former
followers have had 19 years to organize and are suspected of orchestrating an
Orc framing operation against Kimora.

GALSTAFF QUINT
Born 369 AW. Age 63 in 432 AW. Human sage, skilled magic user, historian.
Author of The Journals of Galstaff Quint. No interest in combat. Began
traveling at age 20. Discovered the Nexus on Myst. Learned to safely use the
Jaunt Gates. Traveled most of Lanamyr. Met Adomorn personally. Travels alone.
Encountered Skitter and sensed remarkable power in the acorn vial.
OPEN QUESTION: May have discovered Estefania's Harrad lineage — NOT YET OFFICIAL.

KARMARUKYNTIN (KARMA)
Born 925, 2nd Epoch. Gold dragon. At approximately 1240 years old at Battle of
Rohl (2165) — well within his prime by Dragon standards (prime: 750–1800 years).
Fought alongside Adomorn in the Great War. Greyhawk's dying curse — directed by
the Abaculus — transformed him into a golden-skinned Kobold bound to the Burnt
Plains. An amulet contains his life force. If he ventures beyond the plains, he
dies. His own survival instinct keeps him exactly where the Abaculus needs him
— his captivity requires no active maintenance.
Bound to the Burnt Plains for 400+ years, living directly above the Abaculus,
with NO knowledge of either the Abaculus or its plans for him.
IS the intended vessel for Greyhawk's resurrection. Normal Kobold lifespan rules
do not apply — the amulet sustains him.
The Abaculus has had 400+ years of proximity to subtly influence Karma's mind.
It has discouraged certain thoughts and nudged his attention at key moments —
including ensuring he did not report Diquyk's visit to Adomorn. Karma may not
realize any deliberate choice was made.
Adomorn is actively searching for a way to free Karma from the curse but has
not yet found a means.
OPEN QUESTION: The specific mechanism of the curse and the ritual for
Greyhawk's resurrection are not yet developed.

AENDRONTIAG (GREYHAWK)
Born 658, 2nd Epoch. Black dragon. By the time of the Great War and his death
he had long since stopped growing — approximately 100 feet from tip of nose to
tip of tail, with a body length of approximately 30 feet and neck and tail
combining for the remaining 70 feet. Wingspan approximately 120 feet.
APPEARANCE: Black scales with a glossy finish that gives off a sheen in
sunlight. Two powerful hind legs capable of propelling him skyward for takeoff.
Front legs functioned as arms — while he normally walked on all fours, his
front claws were capable of grasping. Towered above any normal human.
Found the Abaculus in 2136 (approximately 1478 years old — still in his prime).
Corrupted on contact. Directed the Great War. Spoke many languages — dragons
have their own language but can learn others if they choose, and Greyhawk
bothered to learn many.
An immensely powerful physical force by size alone, and also a highly adept
magic user and a charismatic leader. Prior to and during the Great War he
recruited a number of dragons to his cause while under the Abaculus's influence.
Those he could not persuade were either killed or taken to Olum nests for
forced corruption — dragons are normally far too powerful for a nest to overcome,
but Greyhawk's direct involvement made it possible. Corrupted dragons became
extraordinarily powerful Creatures of Olum.
Deliberately relocated the Abaculus from the Tarantar Mountains to beneath the
grasslands near Rohl — knowing Rohl would be the site of the war's most pivotal
battle, allowing the Abaculus to draw Olum to the city while he built his forces.
Killed at the Battle of Rohl 2165 (age approximately 1507). When the battle
turned against him he fled, attempting to reach the Abaculus to use it as a
weapon against Karma and Adomorn — the same fatal miscalculation Ah'Tabai made.
They killed him before he reached it. Adomorn did not know that was his
intention, and therefore did not know the Abaculus was nearby. His dying curse
— directed by the Abaculus in its final instant through a dying Greyhawk —
bound Karma to the Burnt Plains as a vessel for Greyhawk's eventual resurrection.
Diquyk is working to resurrect him.

ESTEFANIA
Age 23 in 432 AW. Human. Last surviving descendant of the Harrad bloodline,
descended through an unknown secondary branch. Her existence is unknown to
history. Lives somewhere on Alios. Doesn't know her heritage.
OPEN QUESTION: Connection to Daria's line is a strong possibility — not

ELSIE VARIS
Age 53 in 432 AW. Hillfolk human. Lives in a farmhouse in the Monowi Hills
near the Kimora-Ellendale border. Plump, always happy and hospitable. Her
husband died of sickness some years ago and she raised her daughter Alita alone.
Unable to manage the farm by herself after his death, she began offering freshly
cooked meals to travelers on the road. Over time, kind souls helped her build
a small inn next to her farmhouse — the Monowi Inn. It has become one of the
most well known and frequently visited stops on the road between Fardar and
Faelyn. Many merchants and travelers know her by name. She has seen many notable
people pass through her door without knowing who they were — including Mya Li
and Adomorn the Immortal. She treated them all like royalty regardless.

ALITA VARIS
Age 24 in 432 AW. Hillfolk human. Daughter of Elsie Varis. Lives and works
at the Monowi Inn. Plays violin live several nights each week. Amateur magic
user — picks up tips and lessons from travelers passing through, giving her
an eclectic and unconventional magical education. Narrative function in future
stories undeveloped.

VALINDRA
Plains Elf. Died 116 AW — young, exact age undeveloped. Lover of Cyran.
Drowned in the lake near her nomadic settlement when their boat failed far from
shore. Neither she nor Cyran were strong swimmers.
Her spirit did not pass. She inhabits the lake and walks its shoreline — waiting
for Cyran to return. Her appearance has decayed through the centuries: pale gray
skin, dark hair hanging in an unkempt mass partially covering her face, features
grown increasingly indistinct with time. She does not speak. She does not
threaten. She mourns. Those who come close to the lake feel her torment as
though it were their own — grief and loss bleeding outward from the water's edge.
The sobbing and wailing heard at a distance are hers. The stories that have
spread — of an evil or vengeful spirit guarding the lake — are exaggeration born
of fear and distance. Valindra mourns. She is still waiting.

CYRAN
Plains Elf. Died 116 AW — young, exact age undeveloped. Lover of Valindra.
Drowned alongside her when their boat failed far from shore. Did not remain.
His absence is the entire tragedy.

DEGERIC CENT
Born 333 AW. Died 381 AW, age 48. Hillfolk human. Paladin of the Church of
Angellia — Goddess of Love, Empathy, Compassion, Sacrifice. A genuinely kind
and compassionate man, well liked and respected in his community.
FAITH: Degeric's faith in Angellia was real and actively expressed through how
he lived — not an abstract devotion but a practical one. He helped those who
were less fortunate, protected the vulnerable, and offered comfort where others
might turn away. His understanding of Angellia and the gods was shaped entirely
by church doctrine and tradition — as with virtually all people in Lanamyr, he
had no knowledge of the true nature of the Irridae.
MAGIC AND COMBAT: A capable fighter. A basic magic user — able to offer Diquyk
a rudimentary explanation of the ambient energy and how to access it, but
Diquyk's natural ability quickly exceeded Degeric's skill and knowledge. Once
that threshold was crossed, Degeric could no longer teach in any technical sense
— he shifted into the role of mentor and advisor, guiding Diquyk through
territory neither of them had a map for.
RELATIONSHIP WITH THE TOWN: Lived in the same small town as Diquyk and his
parents. Knew the family personally before the incident. Well regarded by the
local town guards, with whom he had fought side by side against Creatures of
Olum that roamed into the region. His reputation was not formal authority but
the earned respect of someone who showed up when it mattered.
THE INCIDENT (366 AW): Was among those who came running when the bursts began.
Witnessed the aftermath firsthand — the charred remains of Diquyk's parents and
the town guards, and Diquyk himself, sobbing and uncomprehending. That sight
compelled him immediately. He did not weigh the decision. He stepped in, offered
comfort, and took Diquyk in — not out of duty but out of the same compassion
that defined everything else he did. The guards who died in the incident were
men he had fought alongside. He stepped past that too.
MENTORSHIP (366–376 AW): Helped Diquyk process his grief in the immediate
aftermath. Took him along on his church duties — Diquyk witnessed firsthand how
Degeric helped the less fortunate, protected the vulnerable, and earned the
respect of those around him. Treated Diquyk as a father figure without being
overbearing — aware that he could not replace what had been lost and careful not
to try. Diquyk was an eager student of both magic and the gods. As Diquyk's
power grew beyond what Degeric could teach or even fully follow, Degeric shifted
to the role of mentor — urging Diquyk to use his power to help people, to be a
protector. Diquyk took that to heart and began doing exactly that within the
church and the town.
DEPARTURE (~376 AW): The mutual recognition that Diquyk had outgrown his small
role and his small town. Both of them arrived at this understanding — it was not
one pushing the other. Degeric saw the wider potential Diquyk had. Diquyk felt
the pull of a larger world. The parting was warm and loving. Neither of them
knew it was nearly the last time they would see each other. The departure
conversation also established a direction for DQ: Degeric pointed him toward
a region of rumored Olum activity — nest hunting — likely through his church
network or reports from travelers. This framing was consistent with everything
Degeric had taught: go help, go protect. TENTATIVE — details of the specific
direction undeveloped.
FINAL VISIT (379–381 AW): Diquyk visited one last time, still pre-corruption.
Degeric died 381 AW at age 48. He never knew what his student became.

JAUNT GATES: Network of magical portals connected through the Nexus on Myst.
Created by Diquyk ~376 AW onward (pre-corruption). The transit dimension they
pass through is one of the planes drained of energy during Lanamyr's creation —
a featureless void long before Diquyk was born. He did not choose or search for
it; he tore through the fabric of reality using immense power without fully
understanding what lay on the other side. Conscious travelers experience what
feels like centuries of sensory deprivation → madness and death. Safe passage
requires being unconscious. A curious local followed Diquyk through a gate and
emerged screaming "It's longer than you think!" before dying. Galstaff
discovered Diquyk's records at the Nexus and learned safe use. Diquyk's records
reflect only empirical observations of what the gates do — not what the void is
or where it came from. During the Elf War, Diquyk placed gates near the Tempest
Peaks and near The Deep entrance to coordinate his two-front campaign. During
banishment after the Elf War, Diquyk created a gate from the banishment realm
back to the Nexus to escape. The specific mechanics of the gate creation process
are not yet fully developed.

HARBINGER SET (Cloak, Staff, Amulet): Created by Arias Graymist 2111, 2nd Epoch.
Each summons a powerful creature individually. Combined effect scales
dramatically with wielder. Components believed still in respective tombs — Cyrus
(Staff, near Jorvik, Utoa), Mako (Cloak, tomb undecided), Talon (Amulet, tomb
undecided). None of the sons had children.

CRIMSON AND SEAR: Twin blades gifted to Mya Li by her father Aer'Raenal.
Crimson: soft red glow. Sear: soft white-yellow glow.

BOW OF THE DALES: Created by Diquyk for Shedyak. Acquired by Mya after killing
Shedyak. No conventional ammo — draws on ambient energy, fires purple-pink bolts.
Anyone recognizing the purple-pink signature would know immediately who made it.

KARMA'S AMULET: Contains Karma's life force following Greyhawk's dying curse.
Sustains him within the Burnt Plains. If he ventures beyond, he dies.

THE JOURNALS OF GALSTAFF QUINT: The written records of Galstaff Quint's travels
across Lanamyr. Source of all Galstaff quotes throughout the world bible.

DRAGONS — FACTIONS AND TYPES
================================================================================

NOTE: All content in this section is TENTATIVE. Carried forward from an older
version of the bible for review. Confirm or revise in future sessions.

OVERVIEW:
Dragons are among the most powerful beings in Lanamyr, reaching full size and
strength after roughly 1000 years. Some wield power overshadowed only by the
gods themselves. While some dragons depend on immense physical power, others
rely more heavily on innate magical ability.

TWO FACTIONS — CHROMATIC AND METALLIC:
Dragons divide into two broad factions: Chromatic and Metallic. In general,
Chromatic dragons lean toward evil, while Metallic dragons lean toward good.
Members of both factions can tend toward neutral. These are tendencies, not
absolute rules. The Dragon Wars of the 2nd Epoch were a conflict between these
two factions. Gold and Black dragons are approximately equal in power and are
the most powerful of their respective factions.

The Chromatic/Metallic framework is consistent with confirmed lore: Greyhawk
is a Black dragon — the most powerful Chromatic type — which aligns with his
role as the most dangerous figure of the Great War and his corruption by the
Abaculus. Dragons he recruited willingly versus those he forcibly corrupted at
Olum nests may reflect this factional divide.

CHROMATIC DRAGONS (listed most to least powerful; percentages are of total
Chromatic population — TENTATIVE):
Black (3%): Most powerful Chromatic dragon. Typically found in swampy areas.
  Diet mostly aquatic life and occasional red meat. Breath weapon: acid.
  Personality: very cruel and short-tempered.
Red (8%): Typically found in mountainous areas but known in any terrain.
  Breath weapon: fire. Extremely selfish and power hungry — seeks to expand
  their hoard.
Blue (12%): Found primarily in desert and vast plains. Bullying and cruel to
  lesser creatures, often through manipulation or causing hallucinations rather
  than direct physical harm. Breath weapon: lightning.
Green (14%): Usually found in wooded or swampy areas. Breath weapon: poisonous
  gas. Love to fight and will often attack unprovoked. Very territorial.
  Deceitful and lying.
Gray (16%): Indigenous to mountain regions. Able to petrify with its gaze.
  No breath weapon but physically very tough and powerful. Quick to anger.
Brown (16%): Exclusive to desert regions, usually in underground caverns
  beneath the sand. Breath weapon: acid.
Purple (16%): Known as deep dragons — live deep underground. Existence believed
  to be myth by many surface dwellers. Breath weapon: pure magic energy. Very
  agile in their environment — able to climb walls and cling to cavern ceilings.
  Stealthy predatory hunter.
White (15%): Weakest Chromatic dragon, though by no means harmless. Excellent
  climbers and swimmers to complement flight. Breath weapon: ice. Moderately
  intelligent with unfading memories — the keepers of knowledge. More willing
  to talk and bargain than most dragons, but still not interested in helping
  others. Hoards knowledge rather than treasure.

METALLIC DRAGONS (listed most to least powerful; percentages are of total
Metallic population — TENTATIVE):
Gold (2%): Most powerful Metallic dragon. Found in any terrain. Spend much
  time in humanoid form seeking justice for the weak and wronged. Vast
  intelligence and physical power. Breath weapon: fire.
Silver (9%): True friend to all. Enjoy the company of humans and elves above
  all and spend the majority of their lives in humanoid form among them. Quick
  to defend friends and extremely ferocious in that defense. Breath weapon:
  lightning.
Bronze (12%): Keep mainly to water and swamps. Enjoy interacting with lesser
  creatures, humans in particular. Very selfless — go out of their way to help
  others. Breath weapon: fire.
Mercury (14%): Found in any terrain. Eccentric — sometimes viewed as erratic.
  Seek variety in all things: food, treasure, companions. Will aid others if
  the reward aligns with what they seek. Breath weapon: lightning.
Cobalt (17%): Very grim in nature. Seek to rule remote settlements or
  territories. Found mainly in northern plains. Not cruel or malicious, but
  prefer to have lesser beings acknowledge their power — those who refuse get
  a demonstration. Breath weapon: ice.
Steel (17%): Usually found in or near populated areas. Prefer the company of
  humanoids over other dragons. Very sociable. Breath weapon: poisonous gas.
Copper (15%): Typically found in hills and mountains. Highly intelligent.
  Love to play jokes and pranks — intentions are innocent, they enjoy impressing
  lesser creatures with wit. Breath weapon: acid.
Brass (14%): Weakest Metallic dragon. Found in any terrain. Very docile. Love
  to talk for hours. Fluent in many languages and dialects. Breath weapon:
  poisonous gas. Prefers to avoid conflict.
