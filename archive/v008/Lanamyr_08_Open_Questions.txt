THE DRASU CARETAKER MANDATE:
- RESOLVED (v005): the Drasu were meant to be the caretakers of Lanamyr in the
  absence of the Irridae. This is the origin of the Wood Elves' affinity for
  the natural world — inherited, not learned, and not a product of remorse.
- RESOLVED: Wood Elves carry no guilt or shame about their impact on the
  forest. They understood the cost from the beginning, accept it, work to
  minimize it, and offset it by replanting at the forest edges.
- RESOLVED (v005): Shepherd animal affinity IS a fragment of the same shattered
  mandate, descended from their Drasu ancestry. Two fragments are now confirmed
  — Wood Elves hold forest and growing things, Shepherds hold animals. The
  mandate fractured into pieces held by separate races, none of which knows it.
- Note the fragment descended to the Shepherds as a RACE, not to humans as a
  family. Hillfolk, Northmen and Water Whisperers did not receive it.
- OPEN: whether further fragments exist in other races, and if so which. With
  two confirmed the pattern is structural rather than coincidental, so this is
  now a live lens for reading any race's signature trait.
- HOW TO TELL A FRAGMENT FROM A CRAFT — the test is NOT inherited versus
  learned. Everything in question here is inherited. Magic capacity, caretaker
  fragments, and the technology baseline all came through the Sundering, and
  the technology baseline in particular is inherited KNOWLEDGE AND MUSCLE
  MEMORY — what a race woke up already knowing how to do in its hands, not
  anything anyone taught them.
  The real distinctions are two:
    - TRANSFERABILITY. Craft knowledge moves between races through contact and
      conquest. Magic never does, and a caretaker fragment presumably never
      does either.
    - SUBJECT. A caretaker fragment is a relationship with the living world —
      tending it. A craft is a technique for working material. Wood Elf
      stewardship and Shepherd animal affinity are relationships. Ice Forging
      and Rock Gnome gem cutting are techniques. The two are intertwined in
      practice and should still be distinguished when testing for a fragment.
- RESOLVED (v005): the WATER WHISPERERS carry a third fragment — affinity with
  sea life. The apparent conflict with their confirmed fishing was resolved by
  scale rather than set aside: the Shepherd fragment works on the individual
  creature and produced abstention, while the Water Whisperer fragment works on
  the ocean as a whole system and produces restrained, informed harvest. A
  fishery is kept by being managed. Their shipbuilding remains craft and is NOT
  part of the fragment.
- PRINCIPLE ESTABLISHED: each fragment produces a different relationship with
  what it tends. Tending does not mean refraining. Fragments must not be
  written as variations on a single gentle temperament.
- OBSERVATION, not yet a claim: two of the three confirmed fragments sit in
  human subtypes, and two of the four human subtypes carry one while Hillfolk
  and Northmen carry none. Whether this is coincidence or says something about
  the human portion of the Drasu fracture: UNDEVELOPED. Worth watching rather

PLAINS ELVES:
- VERIFIED (v005) against the biome map: origin reads Cool Temperate Steppes
  unambiguously, confirming "central steppes of Alios" as recorded. Moderate
  rainfall, cool temperate, deep treeless grassland, one growing season a year,
  no timber. The v004 rule — check every race origin against the biome map
  before building on it — was applied and passed.
- CONFIRMED: strongest expression of the agriculture and cultivation
  inheritance (Class Two). Intuitive diagnosis of crop problems; deliberate
  selective breeding; the surrounding nations know Ellendale has the largest
  and best-tasting produce.
- CONFIRMED MECHANISM: the advantage requires BOTH the inheritance and the
  200-year lifespan. One crop generation per year on the steppe means a Plains
  Elf can personally run ~150 consecutive generations of selection with no
  handoff, where a human farmer gets ~50 and must pass the work to an heir who
  did not see the earlier ones.
- RESOLVED (v005) — THE SEED PROBLEM: the seed is not the secret and is not
  protected. Seeds travel freely and no effort is made to stop them. Three
  factors produce Ellendale's quality and only the seed can be carried off:
  the SOIL of the central plains, richer in nutrients than the surrounding
  land; the GROWER, whose innate skill and intuitive tending is the inheritance
  and cannot be transferred by any means; and the PROTECTION, a sustained and
  organized effort at pest and small animal control, since much of a harvest's
  quality is simply the part that was never lost. A farmer planting Ellendale
  seed elsewhere gets a decent crop and assumes he was cheated or that the seed
  degenerated. Structure deliberately parallels the three-factor Ice Forging
  explanation.
- CROSS-RACE CONTRAST recorded in Races: Plains Elves kill small animals in
  organized numbers to protect a harvest while Shepherds hold an animal mandate
  fragment and will not eat one. Useful illustration that these inheritances
  point in different directions. A Shepherd in Ellendale is an unspent scene.
