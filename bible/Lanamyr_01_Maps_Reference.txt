================================================================================
MAPS AND VISUAL REFERENCES
================================================================================

These map files are stored in the project alongside the bible files and should
be treated as always available. Each filename is descriptive and maps directly
to the content below.

Lanamyr_Continents.jpg
  Full world map showing all three major continents and surrounding islands.
  Elevation map. Color key: blue = ocean/water; dark to bright green = low
  elevation plains and flatlands; yellow = moderate elevation, hills and rising
  terrain; orange = high elevation, significant mountain ranges; red = very high
  elevation, major mountain peaks; gray = extreme elevation, highest peaks in
  Lanamyr. The Tarantar Mountains in northern Alios reach gray — the tallest
  range in the world.

Lanamyr_Features.jpg
  Named geographic features including all major mountain ranges, deserts, hills,
  and major rivers. Only major rivers are shown at this scale — countless unnamed
  rivers, streams, and lakes exist throughout Lanamyr and will be developed as
  needed. Use this map to identify the location and relative size of named
  geographic features.

Lanamyr_Islands.jpg
  Named islands, archipelagos, and major rivers including the Waywin River on
  Edura. Use this map to identify which islands belong to which kingdoms.

Lanamyr_Sundering.jpg
  Origin locations of all races at the start of the 2nd Epoch, shown as labeled
  points on the map. Updated each session as new origins are confirmed.

Lanamyr_Temperature.jpg
  Overlay map showing temperature gradient across Lanamyr. Designed to be read
  overlaid on Lanamyr_Continents.jpg. Color key: deep blue = coldest polar
  regions; purple/blue = cold to cool; purple = cool temperate; purple/red =
  warm temperate; red = hot tropical or equatorial. Elevation causes local
  cooling — mountain ranges read cooler than surrounding terrain at the same
  latitude regardless of their geographic position.

Lanamyr_Precipitation.jpg
  Overlay map showing relative rainfall across Lanamyr. Designed to be read
  overlaid on Lanamyr_Continents.jpg. Color key: black/very dark = little to
  no rainfall (arid); dark teal = low rainfall; medium teal = moderate rainfall;
  bright cyan/white = highest rainfall, very wet. The Void Sands on Edura
  appear as a visibly dark region confirming near-zero precipitation.

Biome_Legend.png
  Color key for Lanamyr_Biomes.jpg. Upload before or alongside the biome map.
  Biomes are grouped into aggregation categories: Polar, Tundra, Cold Parklands,
  Coniferous Forests, Mixed Forests, Steppes, Cool Deserts, Deciduous Forests,
  Chaparral, Hot Deserts, Savanna, Tropical Seasonal Forests, Tropical Rain
  Forests. Each category contains multiple specific biome subtypes identified
  by hex color code.

Lanamyr_Biomes.jpg
  Biome map showing the dominant biome of every region of Lanamyr. Read using
  Biome_Legend.png for the full color key. Key regional biomes: Icefall = Polar
  Ice; northern Alios/Tarantar region = Cold Parklands/Boreal; central Alios
  (Kimora, Ellendale, Faelyn) = Deciduous and Mixed Forests with Steppes;
  eastern Alios = Steppes to Cool Desert; southern Alios = warming to Tropical
  Seasonal Forest; northern Edura/T'sarra = Tropical Rain Forest; Void Sands =
  Hot Desert; southern Edura tip = Tundra; Utoa = mixed Steppes, Cool Desert,
  and Polar at southern extreme; Snow Elf island = Polar Ice and Polar Desert;
  Malulo Archipelago = Tropical Rain Forest; Gleaming Spires island = Polar Ice
  (explaining snow-capped self-luminous peaks); Firethorn Peaks island = warmer
  tones due to active volcanism.

Lanamyr_Epoch1.jpg
  Shows the territorial extent of the Drasian Empire (northern Edura, centered
  on Atral) and Draak Territory (Black Mountains of Fardar, central Alios)
  during the 1st Epoch. The vast majority of Lanamyr was wild and untamed.
  Borders are approximations defined by geographic features.

Lanamyr_Epoch3.jpg
  Shows the kingdoms of the 3rd Epoch in the present day (432 AW). Confirmed
  kingdoms visible: Andar, Kimora, Ellendale, Faelyn, Hellar, Fardar (Alios);
  T'sarra (Edura); Felgard (Utoa). Island kingdoms self-evident from
  Lanamyr_Islands.jpg. Borders are approximations defined by geographic features.
  The Malulo Archipelago is circled in red to show the full extent of Sea Elf
  island territory.
