WORLD OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Lanamyr is a world created by the N'du Irridae (with the participation of some
Itule Irridae) to hide the Abaculus — a sentient, indestructible crystal that
craves the destruction of all existence.

THREE EPOCHS:
1ST EPOCH: The age of the Drasu and Draak — two parallel civilizations created
by the Irridae. Ended at the Battle of Atral (year 1334) when the Abaculus
destroyed all Irridae present, shattering both races into the many modern races
via the Sundering.

2ND EPOCH: The age of kingdoms. Harrad was the dominant human alliance. Ended
at the Battle of Rohl (year 2165) when Greyhawk was killed and Harrad dissolved.
The Common Calendar reset to year zero.

3RD EPOCH (AW / AFTER WAR): The present age. Present day: 432 AW.

MAGIC SYSTEM: All magic users draw from ambient energy permeating everything.
Magni tap into this more easily without spells or rituals. The ambient energy
generated by all living things also acts as a vast masking field that hides the
Abaculus's location — which is why Creatures of Olum exist to destroy life.

KNOWLEDGE OF THE GODS: Virtually everyone in Lanamyr — including church figures
and devout followers — understands the gods only through faith, doctrine, and
tradition. The true nature of the Irridae as higher-dimensional beings of pure
energy, the three factions, the cosmic conflict, and the existence of the
Abaculus are unknown to essentially all living people outside of Adomorn and
the highest ranks of the Var Harma. Genuine faith in a god is entirely possible
and meaningful without any knowledge of what that god truly is.

THE CREATION OF LANAMYR
================================================================================

THE ORIGIN OF THE AMBIENT ENERGY:
The ambient energy that permeates all of Lanamyr — the energy drawn upon by
every magic user, that fills every living thing, and that collectively masks the
Abaculus — was not conjured from nothing. The Irridae are higher-dimensional
beings capable of accessing many different dimensions and planes of existence.
That energy was harvested from other planes and used to fuel the creation of
Lanamyr and everything within it.

Many of these other planes were entire worlds in their own right. When the N'du
and participating Itule Irridae chose which planes to harvest from, they
deliberately chose only those that were devoid of life. As energy was drawn from
these dimensions to build Lanamyr, those planes were gradually stripped bare —
wiped clean and rendered featureless voids.

THE JAUNT GATE DIMENSION:
The featureless void that conscious travelers experience when passing through a
Jaunt Gate is one of these harvested planes — already emptied long before Diquyk
was born. When Diquyk created the Jaunt Gates, he did not choose or search for
this dimension. He tore through the fabric of reality using his immense power,
without fully understanding what lay on the other side or what he was touching.
The void was simply there — the nearest accessible layer beneath the plane of
Lanamyr, already hollowed out by the Irridae's act of creation. Diquyk had no
knowledge of its origin, and he still does not. His records at the Nexus reflect
this: they are empirical observations of what the gates do, not what they are.
The specific mechanics of how he created the gates are not yet fully developed.

WHO BUILT LANAMYR:
The N'du Irridae were the primary architects of Lanamyr. Some Itule Irridae
participated alongside them. The Itule are primarily concerned with balance —
but they understood that the Abaculus, and the Onwu's plans to use it, would
cause catastrophic disruption to that balance. That shared stake was sufficient
to bring them into the project alongside the N'du.

BUILT IN SECRET:
Lanamyr was created without the knowledge of the Onwu Irridae. The Irridae are
not omniscient — not even the gods know everything happening across the universe
at once. The Onwu became aware of Lanamyr only after it was already built. Once
they discovered it and understood its purpose, they came to search for the
Abaculus themselves.

THE BINDING MECHANIC:
Irridae in their natural state are higher-dimensional beings of pure energy. To
meaningfully interact with or search a specific plane of existence, they cannot
do so from their higher-dimensional state — they must descend and bind themselves
physically to that plane. This binding greatly limits their power and capability.
It was this requirement that made the Onwu's search for the Abaculus so
difficult. No Irridae — of any faction — remain bound to Lanamyr in the present
day. All Irridae who had bound themselves to Lanamyr were destroyed at the
Battle of Atral.

ALL LIFE ON LANAMYR:
All life on Lanamyr — every race, every creature, every plant — was created by
the N'du Irridae, with the Itule contributing in a lesser capacity. This includes
the Drasu, the Draak, and every living thing down to the smallest earthworm.

Creating the Drasu and Draak was a fundamentally different undertaking from
creating lesser life. They were given intelligence and greater power, and their
creation required the combined effort of many N'du and Itule working together.
This collective creative investment is precisely why the Sundering ripped them
apart while lesser creatures were unaffected — the resonant shockwave of the
Irridae's destruction reverberated most catastrophically through the things most
deeply woven from that collective effort. A rabbit created by a single N'du with
relatively little effort simply did not carry enough of that collective energy
to be torn apart the same way. Wildlife, plants, and lesser creatures survived
the Sundering mostly unaffected as a result.

THE SUNDERING — ORIGIN OF THE RACES
================================================================================

Each Drasu who died at the Battle of Atral fractured simultaneously into one
individual of each descendant race — elves, humans, dwarves, gnomes, orcs,
trolls, goblins, and all others. The ratio was exact: every Drasu produced
exactly one of each descendant race. The same is true of the Draak, who
fractured into one of each of their descendant races: dragons, dragonborne of
all types, kobolds, wyrms, and others.

Because the full moral spectrum of the Drasu and Draak was preserved through
this fracturing — including those who were evil — all descendant races carry the
capacity for both good and evil. An evil Drasu produced an evil Wood Elf, an
evil human, an evil dwarf, and so on, all from the same fracture.

THE TRUE CAUSE OF THE SUNDERING:
The Sundering was not a direct act of the Abaculus. It was the resonant
shockwave of the Irridae being violently destroyed at Atral. The Drasu and
Draak were the living creation of those beings — when the architects of the
world were suddenly and catastrophically annihilated, that collapse reverberated
through their creation. This shattered the Drasu and Draak simultaneously across
all of Lanamyr, even those who were not present at Atral. The Abaculus caused
it only indirectly — it engineered the deaths, and those deaths did the rest.
Every modern race — every elf, human, dwarf, dragon, and all others — owes
their existence to a catastrophe the Abaculus engineered.

PHYSICAL MATURITY AT EMERGENCE:
All newly formed individuals were fully grown adults at the moment of the
Sundering — not the same numerical age as their ancestor, but the equivalent
level of physical maturity translated into their own race's terms. A young adult
Drasu might produce a 20 year old human and a 50 year old elf — both considered
young adults by the standards of their respective races.

MEMORIES:
The memories of each newly formed individual were a permanent haze before the
moment they found themselves in their homeland. No matter what methods were
tried, memories did not reach beyond that boundary. However, each individual
retained residual intelligence — language, reasoning, the capacity to build and
organize — which is how the races eventually established kingdoms.

DISTRIBUTION ACROSS LANAMYR:
Each race appeared simultaneously in the geographic region that would eventually
become their homeland. The Wood Elves appeared in what would become Faelyn.
The Iron Dwarves appeared in the Black Mountains of Fardar. And so on.

FIRST CONTACT:
For many years each race knew only itself. It was only through the natural
expansion of boundaries that races encountered one another — sometimes
peacefully, sometimes not. Over centuries, friendly races mingled and integrated.
Even today this legacy persists — Faelyn is Wood Elf domain but humans, gnomes,
and other races live there. This is the natural state of the world.

DRAGON PHYSICAL GROWTH:
Dragons stop growing in physical size at approximately 1000 years of age.

VARIANCE — RAYLEIGH-SHAPED DISTRIBUTION:
The stated averages are near the LOW END of the distribution, not the midpoint.
The distribution has a long tail on the high end. Most individuals who avoid
violence, disease, and accident will live somewhat beyond the stated average.
The longer a race's lifespan, the more variance — a very long-lived human might
reach 100 (11% above average), while a very long-lived Wood Elf might reach
nearly 400 (approximately 60% above average).

MIXED RACE OFFSPRING:
If two members of different races have offspring, the child's lifespan will
typically fall somewhere between that of its parents. Random chance determines
whether it is closer to the shorter or longer-lived parent. The same proportional
life stage percentages apply to mixed race individuals, calculated against
whatever lifespan they end up with.

THE ABACULUS & RELICS OF CREATION
================================================================================

THE ABACULUS
A sentient, indestructible crystal of unknown origin — possibly as old as the
universe. Cannot be destroyed by any known means.

APPEARANCE: Large. Black, close to obsidian in color, but not static — faint
swirls move across its surface at rest, becoming more pronounced as a potential
target approaches. The swirls shift through soft greens, blues, and reds in
slow hypnotic patterns.

CAPABILITIES:
- Can destroy Irridae by generating the inverse of their unique energy signature.
- Can consume Relics of Creation on contact, permanently annihilating whatever
  aspect of creation they represent.
- In close proximity, can subtly influence the thoughts and actions of nearby
  beings — minor persuasion and coercion without full control. This influence
  cannot outright control or corrupt without physical contact, but can nudge
  attention, discourage certain thoughts, and guide behavior. Beings are
  typically unaware they are being influenced.
- On physical contact, corrupts the being touching it. Corruption is not
  always instantaneous — the speed depends on the nature and power of the
  being. A mortal may be overwhelmed in seconds. A cosmic being like an
  Irridae may resist long enough to act, though resistance is ultimately futile.
- Can kill an Irridae quickly if it chooses, but may deliberately prolong the
  process to use them as a controlled tool.
- Irridae magic and Drasu magic are the only known types capable of forcibly
  moving the Abaculus without physical contact, bypassing corruption entirely.
  Adomorn is the only living being with this capability.

BEHAVIOR AND GOALS:
Wants to be found. Craves the destruction of all existence — including
eventually Diquyk himself. Pattern: identify an exceptionally powerful being,
corrupt them, use them as a weapon. Greyhawk in the 2nd Epoch. Diquyk in the
3rd Epoch. It has no allies — only tools.
Ultimate goal: have Diquyk ascend to godhood (lose physical form, become pure
energy) → wield the Abaculus against all Irridae → then turn on Diquyk too.
Through Greyhawk's corruption the Abaculus learned of the Heaven's Oak chamber
in Faelyn — built by Adomorn as a permanent containment solution. This made
the Wood Elves and their Guardians a strategic target: breaking the Guardians
and compromising the chamber was a priority before Diquyk could pursue the
larger mission. This is why the Abaculus directed Diquyk to wage war on Faelyn.

CURRENT STATUS: Beneath the Burnt Plains within present-day Kimora. Currently
in active suppression mode — hiding its presence, suppressing its short-range
pull on Creatures of Olum, and concealing itself from detection. It knows
Adomorn has built a containment chamber and does not want to be found before
Diquyk completes his mission. This is why Adomorn's Olum-tracking methodology
has never led him to the Burnt Plains.
Adomorn does NOT know where it is. Adomorn does NOT know Diquyk has returned.

THE APPROACH AND CORRUPTION OF DIQUYK (confirmed scene excerpt):
The Abaculus begins working before physical contact. It narrows the target's
vision, empties their thoughts, and draws them forward in a hypnotic state as
the swirls intensify. By the time contact is made, defenses are already
significantly lowered. At the moment of contact, the body seizes — muscles
locking, involuntary rigidity. The corruption then invades the mind as a
strange foreign presence, dismantling it from within. Resistance is possible
but futile — retreating into memories provides temporary refuge but the dark
tendrils follow. Subjectively the struggle feels prolonged; to an outside
observer it is over in seconds.

MOVEMENT HISTORY (chronological):
1. Year 70, 1st Epoch: Hidden in the Black Mountains of Fardar by Adomorn
   and the N'du Irridae when they departed. A temporary measure while a
   permanent solution was sought. The Draak agreed to actively monitor and
   protect it — lending their physical strength, flight, and magic. The Draak's
   custodial role ended when Ah'Tabai retrieved it prior to the Battle of Atral.
2. Year 1334, 1st Epoch: Retrieved by Ah'Tabai, who intended to weaponize it
   against the incoming Onwu Irridae. The Abaculus corrupted and used him to
   destroy all Irridae present at Atral.
3. Year 7, 2nd Epoch: Relocated by Adomorn to beneath the Tarantar Mountains.
   Adomorn then began the Heaven's Oak chamber project as the permanent
   containment solution he had long sought. The chamber was completed but has
   never held the Abaculus.
4. Year 2136, 2nd Epoch: Greyhawk found it in the Tarantar Mountains via an
   Olum anomaly, was corrupted on contact, and relocated it himself to beneath
   the grasslands near Rohl — deliberately chosen as the site of the war's most
   pivotal battle. The Abaculus would draw Olum to the city while Greyhawk
   built his forces. First time the Abaculus effectively directed its own
   relocation through a corrupted tool. Through Greyhawk's memories the
   Abaculus also learned of the Heaven's Oak chamber and the Guardians of the
   Tree — knowledge it would later use to direct Diquyk.
5. Year 2165, 2nd Epoch to present: Following Greyhawk's death at the Battle
   of Rohl, his dying curse — directed by the Abaculus — transformed Karma and
   bound him to the Burnt Plains directly above the Abaculus. It has remained
   there for 400+ years.

RELICS OF CREATION
Physical objects that are byproducts of the Irridae's act of creation. Each
Relic is a discrete physical object that can be found, held, and carried. Each
represents something that was created — whether a physical thing (a species, a
mountain range, a tree) or an abstract quality (love, grief, courage, jealousy,
curiosity). There is no distinction between physical and abstract Relics — all
work the same way. All are virtually indestructible by conventional means.
If the Abaculus consumes a Relic, it effectively cancels it out of existence
on contact — permanently and totally erasing whatever it represents from all
of reality, taking everything anchored to that Relic with it. The Abaculus
cannot move itself and requires an agent to bring Relics to it.

ABSTRACT RELICS:
- Relics exist for abstract qualities — love, grief, courage, jealousy, etc.
- Destruction by the Abaculus would permanently erase those qualities.

ONE CONFIRMED RELIC IN THE PRESENT DAY:
An acorn carried by the Kobold Skitterskink in a small glass vial around his
neck. Discovered in charred wreckage of a farm his tribe raided and burned —
completely unaffected by the fire. Skitter has no idea of its true significance.
Galstaff Quint encountered Skitter and sensed remarkable power from the vial.
WHAT THE ACORN REPRESENTS: UNKNOWN.

Adomorn is aware of the existence and nature of Relics of Creation — recovered
from the Drasian Vault. He would not share this knowledge unless absolutely
necessary. Many Relics remain undiscovered.

THE IRRIDAE (GODS)
================================================================================

Beings of pure energy, each with a unique signature. Three factions:
N'du (Good/Life), Itule (Neutral/Balance), Onwu (Evil/Death).
Departed Lanamyr early in the 1st Epoch to spread Onwu thin across multiple
worlds. Only a small number were destroyed at the Battle of Atral. The
majority still exist elsewhere in the universe in an ongoing cosmic conflict.

CRITICAL: When an Irridae dies, their domain DOES NOT cease to exist. The
qualities they governed were woven into the fabric of creation at the beginning
and persist independently. Only the Abaculus consuming the corresponding Relic
of Creation can permanently erase a quality from existence.

CREATURES OF OLUM
================================================================================

Created by Olum by corrupting living creatures. Sickly gray-to-black with
glowing red eyes. Not truly living — manifestations of death. PRIMARY DIRECTIVE:
destroy life, to reduce ambient magical energy masking the Abaculus.

SOURCE CREATURES: Drawn primarily from wildlife — bears, deer, boars, and
similar animals are the most common source. Intelligent races can also be taken
if they stray too close to a nest. A corrupted elf, human, or other intelligent
being twisted into a Creature of Olum is a deeply unsettling sight. Dragons are
normally far too powerful for a nest to overcome and would not typically be
taken — Greyhawk's direct intervention was what made forced dragon corruption
possible during the Great War.

OUTSIDE SHORT RANGE (more than a few miles from the Abaculus): Gravitate toward
high concentrations of life to nest and build numbers.
WITHIN SHORT RANGE (within a few miles): Feel a faint pull toward the Abaculus.
Currently SUPPRESSED ENTIRELY by the Abaculus to avoid detection.
HOW GREYHAWK FOUND THE ABACULUS: He investigated an unusual concentration of
Olum in the Tarantar Mountains — Olum congregating in a sparsely populated
area with little life to draw them naturally. The short-range pull of the
Abaculus was the cause.
HOW ADOMORN SEARCHES: Investigates every Olum concentration he finds. Every
lead is legitimate, but 400+ years of investigation have turned up only natural
nesting behavior. The Burnt Plains is lifeless — the one place his methodology
gives him no reason to visit.
Age into large tentacled pack leaders that form birthing chambers. Dissolve to
harmless black dust on death.
