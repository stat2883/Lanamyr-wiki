THE DRASU AND DRAAK (1ST EPOCH)
================================================================================

DRASU
Humanoid, larger than any present-day race — tall, bronze-skinned, lean and
powerful. Superior intelligence and magic users. Created the Drasian Empire on
Edura and the Drasian Vault. The potential for both good and evil existed within
the Drasu — the Sundering revealed and amplified rather than created these
qualities. Split into elves, dwarves, gnomes, humans, orcs, and others.
Adomorn is the last surviving Drasu. Takes human form. Galstaff Quint is one
of the rare few to have seen his true Drasu form.

DRAAK
Most closely resembled present-day dragons but larger and more powerful.
Capable of flight. Intelligent and capable of magic, but not to the extent of
the Drasu. Central heartland in the Black Mountains of Fardar (Draak Territory).
The potential for both good and evil existed within the Draak — same as Drasu.
Split into dragons, dragonborne, wyrms, kobolds, and other types.
The Draak were active, willing participants in monitoring and protecting the
Abaculus during its first hiding place in the Black Mountains — lending their
physical strength, flight, and magical abilities to that purpose. Their
custodial role ended when Ah'Tabai retrieved the Abaculus prior to the Battle
of Atral. All Draak were shattered in the Sundering that followed.
ANCIENT DRAAK SETTLEMENT: The Draak maintained a settlement in the river valley
dividing the eastern and western halves of the Black Mountains — the heart of
their territory. Not a city in the Drasian sense — the Draak were not builders
of that sophistication. More likely a gathering place of natural caves and peaks
modified and expanded to suit their size, with wide passages, open roosting
ledges, and fire-scorched communal spaces. The scale would be enormous by modern
standards given the Draak's size, but crude compared to Atral. The name of this
settlement is undeveloped. What remains of it after thousands of years — and
whether the Iron Dwarves of Fardar have encountered any of it during their
mining operations — is undeveloped.

1ST EPOCH TERRITORIES (confirmed on maps):
- Drasian Empire: Northern Edura, centered on Atral along the Waywin River.
- Draak Territory: Black Mountains of Fardar and surrounding region, central
  Alios. Not a conventional kingdom — a roosting ground and heartland.
Outside these two, the rest of Lanamyr was mostly wild and untamed.

MOUNTAIN TROLL ORIGIN (confirmed):
Troll's Breath Mountains, just south of the ruins of Atral and the Waywin
River on Edura. Emerged there at the Sundering and have never truly left.
The mountain range is named after them. They contested the Atral ruins with
the High Elves during early excavations and were pushed back by the combined
High Elf and Sand Dwarf alliance. They remain a periodic threat to excavation
operations — they know the ruins contain things of value. Extensive underground
caverns originate in the Troll's Breath Mountains; some connect into the outer
edges of the underground ruins of Atral. Hostile contact with Var Harma
excavations occurs periodically when their paths intersect underground. The
Trolls have at most found remnants of buried structures on the outer edges of
Atral — nowhere near the Drasian Vault at the city's center.

NOTE ON HEIGHT CONVENTIONS:
- Hillfolk humans are the baseline standard — equivalent to real-world humans.
- All human subtypes share variety in hair, eye color, and skin tone. They are
  distinguished primarily by physical size and geographic origin, not appearance.
- Dwarf gender height separation is 1 inch (male taller) unless otherwise noted.
- Gnome male and female heights are equal unless otherwise noted.
- Magni follow the physical appearance of their base race — see footnote.

NOTE ON LANGUAGE NAMING:
Present-day language names reflect the world as it exists in 432 AW and may not
reflect what those languages were called in earlier epochs. Andari, for example,
is named for the Andar region — a regional identity the early 2nd Epoch Northmen
would not yet have had. This convention likely applies to other race languages
as well. Native language acquisition is universal to the race. Additional language
knowledge is individual — determined by travels, education, and experience, not
race. Common is widely spoken as a second language across most of Lanamyr.

MAGNI FOOTNOTE: Magni are not a separate race. They are born into any race as
an exceedingly rare fluke. Their physical appearance follows their base race
entirely. The table below does not include a Magni entry for this reason.

KOBOLD SUBTYPES
================================================================================

Four confirmed Kobold subtypes, all descended from the Draak. As with all
races, Kobolds have spread throughout Lanamyr over the 2nd and 3rd Epochs —
any subtype may be encountered in travels far from their origin territory.

MYSTIC KOBOLDS
Origin: Southern Alios, northwest of the Obsidian Dragonborne swamp region.
Lifespan: 50 years — longest of all Kobold subtypes.
Appearance: Taller and leaner than most Kobolds. Shorter tails.
Traits: The most intelligent Kobold subtype. One of the rare subtypes with
natural magical capability — magic is possible but still uncommon even among
Mystics. Skitterskink (Skitter) is a Mystic Kobold. Their greater intelligence
and capacity for magic has allowed many Mystics to move beyond tribal life and
integrate into the wider world more readily than other subtypes.

TUNNELER KOBOLDS
Origin: Far eastern end of the Black Mountains of Fardar, within Fardar's
borders but distant enough from the kingdom's center to avoid significant
conflict with the Iron Dwarves.
Lifespan: 40 years.
Appearance: Shorter and more powerfully built than other Kobolds. Large hands
with sharp claws adapted for digging.
Traits: Average intelligence by Kobold standards. Cave and burrow dwellers —
their origin in the Black Mountains suited their subterranean lifestyle. Their
proximity to the ancient Draak settlement in the river valley means they may
have encountered Draak remnants that the Iron Dwarves have not yet found.

SCAVENGER KOBOLDS
Origin: Southern tip of Edura. Cold, harsh tundra environment.
Lifespan: 35 years — second shortest of all Kobold subtypes.
Appearance: Smaller than other Kobolds. Fast and agile.
Traits: Hunt and operate in packs. Very ferocious — seldom use weapons,
preferring claws and teeth. Opportunistic and animalistic in behavior, targeting
farms and livestock primarily. Generally cowardly unless numbers strongly favor
them. The harsh cold environment of their origin shaped their pack survival
instincts. Short lifespan reflects the attrition of their feral lifestyle.

HARDBACK KOBOLDS
Origin: Evergreen Strand, far eastern tip of Utoa.
Lifespan: 40 years.
Appearance: The most physically imposing Kobold subtype. Thicker hide
throughout, particularly on the back where they have larger and thicker
dragon-like scales — the feature that gives them their name and their notable
durability.
Traits: The least intelligent Kobold subtype. Brutish and reckless. Their
tough hide makes them considerably harder to harm than other Kobolds.

GOBLIN SUBTYPES
================================================================================

Three confirmed Goblin subtypes, all descended from the Drasu. As with all
races, Goblins have spread throughout Lanamyr over the 2nd and 3rd Epochs —
any subtype may be encountered in travels far from their origin territory.

SCREECHER GOBLINS
Origin: Wandering Hills, northeastern Kimora, northern Alios.
Lifespan: 15 years — the shortest lifespan of any race in all of Lanamyr.
Traits: Rudimentary language and speech capability. When excited or frightened,
emit very high-pitched shrieks — the feature that defines and names them. The
brevity of their lifespan shapes a fast and chaotic existence.

MARSH GOBLINS
Origin: Small island off the eastern coast of Alios. Island name undeveloped.
Lifespan: 30 years — longest of all Goblin subtypes.
Traits: Build small earthen huts. The most socially developed Goblin subtype —
a more noticeable social structure than the other subtypes. Reasonably
intelligent. Their island origin may have contributed to their greater social
cohesion and development in relative isolation.

FERAL GOBLINS
Origin: The region between the Ash Mountains to the north and the Blackwing
Hills to the south, south of Kimora.
Lifespan: 20 years.
Traits: The most common Goblin subtype. Very savage and territorial. Capable
of forging and creating weapons and armor, though the quality is very poor.
The name refers to their demeanor rather than their intelligence or
craftsmanship — they are not incapable, simply brutal and aggressive by nature.

ICE DWARVES — EXPANSION AND ICE FORGING
================================================================================

EXPANSION BEYOND ICEFALL:
After several centuries of settling Icefall, the Ice Dwarves felt the need to
expand beyond their island kingdom for additional resources. In year 221 of the
2nd Epoch, the first major expeditions departed Icefall in multiple directions.

Sailing south, they found the Tempest Peaks and the region of the Darkened
Slopes. They found much of that region already occupied but established trade
routes there. These routes helped build friendly relations with the neighboring
peoples over time.

Another expedition sailing in a different direction discovered the Tarantar
Mountains on northern Alios and recognized the abundance of mineral wealth
there. This led to the establishment of Nusul — see Kingdoms_History file.

ICE DWARF RELATIONS WITH IRON DWARVES:
Ice Dwarves and Iron Dwarves occasionally cross paths in the lands between their
kingdoms, but the two kingdoms have no strong direct ties. Trade between them is
indirect — goods pass through intermediary kingdoms rather than through direct
exchange. The two cultures share ancient Drasu heritage but have developed very
differently over the centuries since the Sundering.

ICE FORGING:
A unique weapon-crafting tradition specific to Icefall. Ice Forged weapons are
highly sought after across Lanamyr and carry significant trade value. The
technique cannot be replicated elsewhere. Three factors combine to make it
exclusive to Icefall:

THE ORE: Iron mined from the mountains of Icefall has properties not found in
iron ore from other regions. Superior hardness and durability are inherent to
the raw material. Taking Ice Forging technique to another location and using
foreign ore will not produce the same result.

THE ENVIRONMENT: The quenching process requires extreme cold temperatures that
exist naturally on Icefall but are not found in almost any other part of
Lanamyr. The cold itself is the quenching medium — not water or oil.

THE TECHNIQUE: After the metal is heated and shaped, it is quenched in the
extreme cold. The protected secret lies entirely in the quenching process —
the precise timing, the duration of cold exposure, and the controlled rate at
which the blade's temperature is dropped. These variables, applied correctly
to Icefall ore in Icefall conditions, produce a blade that requires no
tempering. In conventional forging, a blade must be reheated slightly after
quenching — a process called tempering — to prevent brittleness. Ice Forged
blades skip this step entirely and are not brittle after quenching. They hold
their edge remarkably well.

The knowledge is embodied — carried in the hands and instincts of living smiths,
passed down through generations of Ice Dwarf craftspeople. It is not recorded
in any book or vault. This makes the tradition both protected and vulnerable:
it cannot be stolen from a document, but it could be lost if the smiths who
carry it were ever decimated or isolated.

Any smith attempting to replicate Ice Forging without all three elements —
the ore, the environment, and the precise technique — would produce blades
that shatter or perform no better than standard work.

HUMAN SUBTYPES — EXTENDED ENTRIES
================================================================================

SHEPHERDS
Origin: Distant island on the far eastern edge of the world map.
The Shepherds partly get their name for their deep, symbiotic relationship with
animals. Their existence on their island was and remains one of balance with the
natural world around them. By the 3rd Epoch they have spread throughout most of
Lanamyr, as have most races.
ANIMAL AFFINITY: Shepherds possess a deep affinity with all animals that goes
beyond learned behavior. They have a profound intuitive understanding of animal
behavior, body language, threat signals, and how animals perceive friends and
danger. This alone would make them exceptional — but there is likely something
even deeper at play, something fundamental to the Shepherds that other humans
simply do not possess. If a wild wolf encounters an ordinary Hillfolk human it
is likely to attack. A Shepherd might calm it entirely. The Shepherds themselves
may not have a complete explanation for this quality.
COMPANION ANIMALS: Shepherds are almost without exception accompanied by an
animal companion of some kind. The word "pet" is misleading — the relationship
is more accurately described as a partnership. The animal chose to be there, and
the Shepherd chose it back. This mutual selection reflects the deeper nature of
how Shepherds relate to the animal world.
GUIDES: Shepherds are frequently sought out as wilderness guides. Moving through
terrain with a Shepherd is a fundamentally different experience — animals that
would be threats to others are non-threats or even assets. A Shepherd reads
terrain through animal behavior in ways no other guide can replicate.
DIET: Shepherds are vegetarian as a cultural norm. For a people with genuine
partnerships with animals and that deeper affinity, eating them is a fundamental
contradiction of everything they are. It does not feel like a choice to most
Shepherds — it simply is not something they do. A Shepherd traveling far from
home in a region where plant-based options are scarce may adapt out of necessity
rather than choice.
Native language: Tapyr.

WATER WHISPERERS
Origin: Small island cluster to the southwest of the main Alios/Edura landmass,
south of the Malulo Archipelago region.
The most accomplished seafaring civilization in all of Lanamyr. Their shipbuilding
is unmatched anywhere in the world. Unlike Ice Forging — which is locked to
Icefall by its ore, environment, and technique — Water Whisperer shipbuilding
travels with the people. A Water Whisperer shipwright can build an exceptional
vessel anywhere in Lanamyr. Their skill lives entirely in the craftspeople
themselves, making them highly sought after in any port city. They are also
skilled fishermen. By the 3rd Epoch they have spread throughout most of Lanamyr.
Native language: Hikri.

ELF SUBTYPES — EXTENDED ENTRIES
================================================================================

ELVES — GENERAL
All elf subtypes share certain physical characteristics: pointed ears and more
delicate features than most other races. They tend toward leaner and more slender
builds than Hillfolk humans, though this varies by subtype. Elves have finite
lifespans — they are not immortal. Elderly elves exist in the world.

SNOW ELVES
Origin: Large island just east of Utoa, close enough to be considered part of
the continent. Island name and kingdom name undeveloped.
The Snow Elves diverge most dramatically from the typical elf silhouette. Their
extreme environment has shaped them into something that looks almost alien among
their own kind. Both male and female average 5'1" — the shortest elf subtype,
with no meaningful height difference between genders. Their build is thick and
powerful, with strong legs built for trudging through deep snow — very unlike the
lean slender frame of most elves. Their skin is pallid, sometimes with a faint
hint of blue. Their hair is dark — blacks and dark tones — contrasting sharply
with their eyes, which are strikingly bright and light in color. The combination
of dark hair and vivid eyes against pale skin makes them immediately distinctive.
Native language: Tuavasy.

DARK ELVES
Origin: Beneath the Tarantar Mountains, northern Alios. Kingdom: The Deep
(underground). Their skin is darker gray tones, sometimes bordering on hints of
deep purple — similar at a glance to Dark Dwarves but meaningfully different on
closer inspection. Their hair is generally very light silver, gray, or white.
Their eyes appear cloudy, with irises typically gray or pale blue — giving the
impression of compromised vision to those unfamiliar with them. In reality their
eyes are adapted to the low light of The Deep. Dark Elves spend the majority of
their lives underground and are more reclusive than most surface races. They
are more surface-averse than Dark Dwarves.
Native language: Ukuri.

SEA ELVES
Origin: Malulo Archipelago, western ocean. Kingdom: Malulo.
Immediately distinctive among all races. Their skin is aqua in tone — light
blue-green, the color of shallow tropical water. Their hair matches: blues and
greens, the colors of the sea. Their build is lean and slender, suited for
swimming. The majority of Sea Elf civilization exists underwater — the islands
of the Malulo Archipelago are only the surface-visible portion of their society.
TENTATIVE: approximately 90% of Sea Elf presence is underwater, 10% on land —
not yet confirmed. Nature of underwater settlements undeveloped.
Native language: Vhitoshi.

DWARF SUBTYPES — EXTENDED ENTRIES
================================================================================

SAND DWARVES
Origin: Void Sands Desert, central Edura.
A lifetime under the brutal desert sun has left its mark on the Sand Dwarves.
Their skin is brown to dark bronze in tone and carries a weathered, rough,
leathery texture — not just a color but a quality earned by generations of
exposure to the desert heat. Their hair runs in browns and blond tones, matching
their arid environment. Their eyes are generally lighter browns and hazel with
some variety. They have allied historically with the High Elves in the
excavation and defense of the ruins of Atral.
Native language: Obathi.

IRON DWARVES
Origin: Black Mountains of Fardar, central Alios. Kingdom: Fardar.
The tallest of all dwarf subtypes, averaging 4'11" for males and 4'10" for
females. Their build is compact and powerful. Unlike most dwarf subtypes, Iron
Dwarves show significant variety in skin tone, hair color, and eye color —
individual Iron Dwarves can look quite different from one another. Bor
Glitterbeard has reddish-orange hair and hazel eyes; Grimmon Herringer had dark
brown hair and blue eyes. Their native language is called Stone Speak — a
colloquial name coined by the Iron Dwarves themselves rather than a formal
linguistic title. Two major periods of enslavement — under Bourbac and under
Tarik — have left a lingering cultural distrust of humans.
Native language: Stone Speak.

DARK DWARVES
Origin: Forbidden Peaks and Darkened Slopes, western arm of Alios.
Kingdom: Hellar (underground city in the Forbidden Peaks).
Dark Dwarves are similar to Dark Elves in coloring at first glance — darker
skin complexions and lighter colored hair. However their eyes are not clouded
and are generally darker in tone. Unlike Dark Elves, Dark Dwarves are not
deeply reclusive and spend more time above ground, though they are still
effectively blinded by daylight without protection. Their greater surface
presence makes them more commonly encountered than Dark Elves.
Native language: Tecali.

GNOME SUBTYPES — EXTENDED ENTRIES
================================================================================

SKY GNOMES
Origin: Myst island and surrounding smaller islands. Kingdom: Myst.
Sky Gnomes are one of the most intelligent races in all of Lanamyr. This
exceptional intelligence expresses itself through invention, scholarship, and
ingenuity. Wilward Cobbersplint — a Sky Gnome who built and operated an airship
— is a natural product of this cultural trait. The Nexus on Myst, the hub of
all Jaunt Gates, is consistent with Myst as a center of knowledge and invention.
Native language: Irturi.

CAVE GNOMES
Origin: Beneath the Tarantar Mountains, northern Alios.
Cave Gnomes have an almost alien appearance among all the races of Lanamyr.
Their eyes are large and round with a pale, yellow-white color — very little
variety. Many Cave Gnomes have no hair at all; those who do have it in shades
of silver and white. Their skin is pale. They are possibly the most reclusive
race in all of Lanamyr — even more so than Dark Elves. It would be very rare
to encounter a Cave Gnome outside their own region. They do not leave their
caves often, and when they are above ground they almost always have their face
and eyes covered for protection from the sun. Encountering one in the wider
world would be a genuinely unusual event.
Native language: Omathi.

ROCK GNOMES
Origin: Western end of Coldwind Mountains, Utoa.
Accomplished gem cutters and jewelers. One of the largest diamond mines in all
of Lanamyr is located in the Coldwind Mountains where they originated. Their
skill with gemstones and precious materials has shaped their cultural identity
and their reputation across Lanamyr.
Native language: Eugali.

WOODLAND GNOMES
Origin: Southern Edura near the Iron Peaks.
The shortest of all gnome subtypes at 3'4", but their average weight is similar
to taller gnome types — the mass is simply compressed into a shorter frame.
This makes them thicker and wider than other gnomes, and physically stronger
despite their stature. Their compact powerful build makes them effective climbers.
Their skin tones are generally darker browns, sometimes with a hint of a green
hue. Hair and eyes run in darker tones as well.
Native language: Enaric.
