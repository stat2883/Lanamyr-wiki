NUSUL — THE LOST KINGDOM
================================================================================

OVERVIEW:
Nusul was an Ice Dwarf kingdom established in the Tarantar Mountains of northern
Alios during the 2nd Epoch. It was abandoned after 29 years following a
catastrophic encounter with subterranean Creatures of Olum. The name Nusul is
known to the Ice Dwarves and preserved in their records and oral history but is
largely unknown to the wider world, which refers to it only as the lost kingdom.
Many adventurers have tried and failed to find it over the centuries.

By 432 AW the ruins have been abandoned for approximately 2,329 years. The
Abaculus was removed from the Tarantar Mountains in year 2136, 2nd Epoch,
after which the Olum gradually dispersed. The ruins have been sitting undisturbed
and increasingly quiet for approximately 461 years.

THE OLUM CONNECTION:
The Abaculus was hidden in the Tarantar Mountains from year 7 of the 2nd Epoch
onward. Creatures of Olum had been expanding outward from that central location
for 232 years before the Ice Dwarves arrived. The Ice Dwarves never knew the
true reason for the Olum presence — to them the mountains were simply
inexplicably and relentlessly dangerous, overrun with Creatures in numbers that
made no natural sense.

CONTENTS:
The kingdom was unfinished at the time of abandonment. Substantial infrastructure
had been carved into the mountain over 29 years. The ruins contain:
- Locally forged tools, weapons, and structural ironwork produced from Tarantar
  ore using conventional forging techniques. The conditions for Ice Forging did
  not exist in the Tarantar Mountains — smiths there worked with the local ore
  using standard methods. The Tarantar ore was high quality in its own right.
- Genuine Ice Forged weapons and tools that arrived via supply runs from Icefall
  before the fall. Given the remarkable durability of Ice Forged blades and
  the cold mountain environment, these could be in extraordinary condition after
  2,300+ years.
- Architecture, chambers, and mine workings abandoned mid-construction.
- The mine that punched through into the Olum cavern in year 268.

THE LEGEND:
As the story filtered through trade routes and intermediaries over centuries,
details were lost and distorted. Multiple versions circulate — a giant dragon
seeking mineral wealth, Dark Elf treachery, and other explanations have all
been attributed to the kingdom's downfall. The truth — subterranean Creatures
of Olum — is known only to the Ice Dwarves. The Dark Elf version is considered
plausible by many, given that Dark Elves occupy The Deep beneath the Tarantar
Mountains and are geographically the closest neighbors the kingdom had.

NUSUL TIMELINE (2nd Epoch):
Year 7:   Abaculus hidden in Tarantar Mountains. Creatures of Olum begin
          expanding outward from that location over the following centuries.
Year 221: First Ice Dwarf expeditions depart Icefall in multiple directions.
          One fleet discovers the Tarantar Mountains and their mineral wealth.
Year 239: Kingdom location established. Named Nusul. Regular supply runs and
          labor forces begin. Olum have been expanding in the mountains for
          232 years by this point.
Year 253: First major Olum encounter. Dwarves fight them off and continue
          working. Valuable mineral deposits found as they dig deeper. Attacks
          grow more frequent as the population grows.
Year 261: Major defeat. Part of the mines closed off. Reinforcements sent from
          Icefall. Permanent guards and scouts deployed. Expansion attempts
          consistently end in being pushed back.
Year 268: A mine punches through into a large subterranean cavern swarming
          with Olum. The kingdom is overrun and lost. All inhabitants killed.
          Later that same year, the last supply ship arrives from Icefall,
          finds silence and Creatures of Olum, and immediately returns to
          Icefall with the news. The Ice Dwarves never return.

3RD EPOCH KINGDOMS (432 AW)
================================================================================

ALIOS:
Kimora: Central Alios. Hillfolk humans. Capital: Shatazaar.
  Borders: South — Ash Mountains; East — Black Mountains of Fardar;
  West — natural border line of hills and mountains (includes Monowi Hills)
  running from Ash Mountains to Tarantar Mountains; North — Tarantar Mountains
  east to coast; Northeast — low plains bounded by Wandering Hills.
  Ruler: King Gralvin Sutton (The Reluctant King).
  Contains ruins of Rohl and the Burnt Plains.
Fardar: Black Mountains, central Alios. Iron Dwarves. One of the richest
  kingdoms in Lanamyr. Relations with Kimora strained after Tarik's crimes.
  Territory encompasses the western and central bulk of the Black Mountains.
  The far eastern end of the range is Tunneler Kobold territory — within
  Fardar's borders but distant enough to avoid significant conflict.
Ellendale: Central-west Alios. Plains Elves. Played a key role in overthrow
  of Tarik alongside the Herringers. Ruler undeveloped.
Faelyn: Eastern and lower western arm of Alios. Wood Elves. Capital: Caldalus.
  Home of Guardians of the Tree. Mya Li is from Faelyn.
Hellar: Underground city in Forbidden Peaks, western arm of Alios. Dark Dwarves.
  Effectively blinded by daylight without protection.
Andar: Large northern peninsula of Alios. Northmen humans. Ruler undeveloped.

EDURA:
T'sarra: Northern Edura. High Elves. Fairly reclusive. Home of the Var Harma.
  Tropical Rain Forest biome — lush and warm. Ruler and internal politics
  undeveloped.

UTOA:
Felgard: Northwestern Utoa. Capital: Jorvik. Hillfolk humans.
  CONFIRMED SURVIVING INTO 432 AW. Allied with Cobalt Dragonborne 260+ years.
  Steppes and Cool Desert biome.

ISLANDS (each race controls entire island/archipelago):
Icefall: Ice Dwarves. Kingdom name: Icefall. Polar Ice biome — extreme cold
  essential for Ice Forging.
Jokul Island: Silver Dragonborne. Kingdom name: Jokul. Encompasses the main
  large island and all surrounding smaller islands collectively — similar to
  Hawaii. Tundra to cold biome.
Malulo Archipelago: Sea Elves. Kingdom name: Malulo. Tropical Rain Forest
  biome — warm and lush. The majority of Sea Elf civilization is underwater;
  the islands represent only the surface-visible portion of their society.
  TENTATIVE: approximately 90% of Sea Elf presence is underwater, 10% on land.
Myst: Sky Gnomes. Kingdom name: Myst. Main island plus all surrounding smaller
  islands. Mixed to Deciduous Forest biome — temperate. Also contains the Nexus.
Snow Elves: Large island just east of Utoa, close enough to be considered part
  of the continent. Polar Ice and Polar Desert biome — extreme cold. Island
  name and kingdom name: UNDEVELOPED.

ORGANIZATIONS
================================================================================

GUARDIANS OF THE TREE
Ancient hereditary Wood Elf secret order from Faelyn. Protect Heaven's Oak and
the Abaculus containment chamber. Founded early 2nd Epoch by Adomorn — fewer
than 10 founding Wood Elf families chosen personally. Answer only to Adomorn.
Outside normal Wood Elf society structure. Aware of Abaculus and chamber's
three purposes. May mate with non-Guardian pure Wood Elves; no non-Wood Elf
mixing allowed. Still vigilant against Elf War threat; unaware strategy shifted
to Greyhawk resurrection. Internal structure, membership, founding family names:
UNDEVELOPED.

THE MARAUDERS
Elite Wood Elf unit assembled by Mya Li specifically for the final incursion
into Dark Elf territory — a purpose-built unit formed AFTER Mya had already
experienced her crushing first defeat against Diquyk's forces. They are NOT the
unit present in the first encounter with Diquyk's forces.
Mya's first love interest (Kharis Ailwin) was killed in that earlier encounter,
along with several other men under her command.
TOTAL STRENGTH: 10 Marauders.
FINAL BATTLE STRUCTURE: The Marauders were divided for the final incursion into
The Deep. 8 accompanied Mya Li as the deliberate decoy force — entering The
Deep through conventional means with the intent of drawing Diquyk's attention
and engagement. 2 accompanied Adomorn through a Jaunt Gate located inside The
Deep, deep within Dark Elf territory, to flank from behind. Adomorn put the
2 Marauders to sleep for the gate transit. How the Marauders entered The Deep
conventionally and how the sleep was timed or lifted are undeveloped.
Diquyk sensed the magical presence of the gate transit — his ability to detect
others drawing on ambient energy extended to the significant magical activity
of gate use — and was tipped off to Adomorn's approach, foiling the ambush.
Despite this, Diquyk was still engaged on two fronts. He intercepted Adomorn's
spells and redirected them as blue flame, killing multiple Marauders. When Mya
and Adomorn switched to physical combat, cutting off Diquyk's fuel supply, he
was finally banished. All 10 Marauders were dead by battle's end. Only Mya and
Adomorn survived — though see tentative note in Adomorn's entry (Characters
file) regarding his possible death immediately after the banishment.
Individual member names and backgrounds: UNDEVELOPED.
STRATEGIC MOTIVATION FOR THE ELF WAR: The Abaculus learned of Heaven's Oak
and the Guardians of the Tree through Greyhawk's memories at the moment of his
corruption. The chamber at Heaven's Oak — built by Adomorn as a permanent
containment solution — represented a direct threat to the Abaculus's freedom.
The Abaculus directed Diquyk to wage war on Faelyn to break the Guardians and
compromise the chamber before he pursued the larger mission.

THE VAR HARMA (THE CURATORS)
High Elf secretive society from T'sarra. Preserve and retrieve artifacts vital
to Elven advancement. Operates on a need-to-know basis internally — lower
ranking members are not privy to the organization's deepest knowledge or
connections. Known member: Maeglin Leralonde, a renowned assassin within the
organization who operates largely on a need-to-know basis and likely does not
know of Adomorn's involvement or possibly who Adomorn is.
ORIGIN: The High Elves emerged in T'sarra north of the ruins of Atral and
discovered the ruins after some years — exact number undecided. Excavations
began, but the region was contested by Mountain Trolls coming down from the
Troll's Breath Mountains. The Sand Dwarves, expanding from the Void Sands
southeast of T'sarra, eventually entered the region and allied with the High
Elves against the Trolls, pushing them back. As more of Atral was excavated
and more valuables uncovered, the High Elves formed the Var Harma to protect
their findings. The organization grew beyond that original protective role into
what it is today.
HEADQUARTERS: Rumored to be in the ruins of Atral, directly above the Drasian
Vault. The Var Harma have been excavating Atral for centuries.
THE DRASIAN VAULT: Tentatively, the Var Harma have likely found it by 432 AW
given the scope and duration of their excavations. Whether they found it
through excavation alone or with Adomorn's guidance is undecided. Adomorn has
contact with the highest ranks only and would have involved himself to manage
the dangerous knowledge within. NOT YET CONFIRMED.
ADOMORN CONNECTION: Has contact only at the highest ranks. Lower members
including Maeglin likely unaware. The relationship may have begun when Adomorn
rematerialized at Atral during High Elf excavations — undecided.
Internal structure beyond need-to-know hierarchy: UNDEVELOPED.

THE HERRINGERS
Defined not by blood but by common cause and belief. Originally an Iron Dwarf
family of great wealth. Matron Arrama Herringer used family resources to resist
Bourbac's enslavement of Iron Dwarves. Organized resistance under political
protection of Lord Yllyndel the Ancient (Plains Elves). Believed to have
funded Evander's assassination of Bourbac. After Evander freed the Dwarves:
protection vanished; Arrama killed by bandits near Ash Mountains foothills.
Rekindled as Tarik's reign took hold. Grimmon Herringer (born 372 AW, died
429 AW) rebuilt the name, personally organized the coordinated overthrow, told
Mya Li firsthand stories of Tarik's atrocities. Was present at the overthrow
at age 41 (413 AW). Founded Herrington. Ambition: new Dwarven kingdom in the
Tarantar Mountains — a dream informed by the legend of the lost kingdom and
the map that passed through Herringer hands for generations. Grimmon killed by
Orc raiders in 429 AW. Leadership and future: UNDECIDED. New Dwarven kingdom
dream: ON HOLD.

THE FOUR CROWNS
Gang based in Broldendul. Led by Alexa Komoth. Further details: UNDEVELOPED.

KINGS OF HARRAD
Formed 1207, 2nd Epoch under Eisengol during Dragon Wars. First born assumed
throne regardless of gender. Dissolved peacefully after Great War 2165.
Territory became Kimora, Ellendale, Fardar. Bloodline believed ended with
Morgan's death; survived through secondary branch → Estefania (age 23, 432 AW).
See full succession timeline below.

TIMELINE
================================================================================

--- 1ST EPOCH ---
Year 0:    Creation of Lanamyr. N'du Irridae are primary architects; some Itule
           participate. Built in secret from the Onwu. Energy harvested from
           lifeless planes of existence to fuel the world's creation — those
           planes wiped clean in the process. All life on Lanamyr created by
           the N'du and Itule — from the Drasu and Draak down to the smallest
           creature. The Drasu and Draak required the combined effort of many
           Irridae and were given intelligence and greater power. Lesser life
           was created by individual Irridae with comparatively little effort.
           Drasu and Draak civilizations established. Good and evil potential
           exists within both races.
Year 70:   N'du depart. Abaculus hidden in Black Mountains of Fardar (heart
           of Draak Territory) as a temporary measure while a permanent
           solution is sought. Draak agree to actively monitor and protect it.
           Adomorn leads this operation. The Onwu, having discovered Lanamyr
           after its creation, bound themselves to the plane and began searching
           for the Abaculus — the reason all Irridae eventually departed.
Year 245:  City of Atral founded on Edura. Drasian Vault built underground.
Year 1181: Onwu Irridae arrive at Atral. Olum creates Creatures of Olum.
Year 1334: Ah'Tabai returns to Lanamyr to prepare for the Onwu threat. In an
           act of poor judgement, retrieves the Abaculus from the Black
           Mountains intending to weaponize it against the Onwu. Corrupted on
           contact. The Abaculus is NOT in the Black Mountains when Atral falls.
           The Abaculus deliberately prolongs Ah'Tabai's corruption to use him
           as a tool. Ah'Tabai — fighting the corruption with the full force of
           his cosmic nature — uses his remaining time to grant Adomorn
           immortality before being fully claimed. Ah'Tabai then destroys all
           Irridae present at Atral. The sudden catastrophic annihilation of
           the Irridae resonates through their creation — the Sundering.
           Draak and Drasu shattered into all modern races simultaneously across
           all of Lanamyr, even those not present at Atral. End of 1st Epoch.
           All Irridae who had bound themselves to Lanamyr are destroyed.
           No Irridae remain in the world from this point forward.

--- 2ND EPOCH ---
Year 7:    Adomorn moves Abaculus to beneath the Tarantar Mountains. Creatures
           of Olum begin expanding outward from that location over the following
           centuries. Adomorn begins building Heaven's Oak chamber with Wood
           Elves. Chooses fewer than 10 Wood Elf families as Guardians of the
           Tree.
Year 221:  First Ice Dwarf expeditions depart Icefall in multiple directions.
           One expedition sails south and finds the Tempest Peaks and Darkened
           Slopes region — finds much of it occupied but establishes trade
           routes. Another expedition discovers the Tarantar Mountains and
           their mineral wealth.
Year 239:  Ice Dwarves establish Nusul in the Tarantar Mountains. Regular
           supply runs and labor forces begin. Creatures of Olum have been
           expanding in the mountains for 232 years.
Year 253:  First major Olum encounter at Nusul. Dwarves fight them off and
           continue working. Valuable mineral deposits found as they dig
           deeper. Attacks grow more frequent as the population grows.
Year 261:  Major Olum defeat at Nusul. Part of the mines closed off.
           Reinforcements sent from Icefall. Permanent guards and scouts
           deployed. Expansion attempts consistently end in being pushed back.
Year 268:  A mine at Nusul punches through into a large subterranean cavern
           swarming with Creatures of Olum. The kingdom is overrun and all
           inhabitants killed. Later that same year the last supply ship
           arrives from Icefall, finds silence and Olum, and immediately
           returns to Icefall. The Ice Dwarves never return to Nusul.
1206-1214: Dragon Wars. Harrad formed under King Eisengol.
Year 1529: Bourbac takes throne. Iron Dwarf enslavement begins. Herringer
           resistance begins under Arrama Herringer, protected by Lord Yllyndel
           the Ancient (Plains Elves).
Year 1561: Bourbac assassinated. Evander "Freeman" takes throne. Frees Iron
           Dwarves. Arrama Herringer eventually killed by bandits.
Year 2059: Arias Graymist born (Felgard, Utoa).
Year 2111: Harbinger Set created. Cobalt Dragonborne raids on Felgard cease
           by 2114.
Year 2136: Greyhawk finds Abaculus in Tarantar Mountains (short-range Olum
           anomaly). Corrupted on contact. Through Greyhawk's memories the
           Abaculus learns of Heaven's Oak and the Guardians of the Tree.
           Greyhawk deliberately relocates Abaculus to beneath grasslands near
           Rohl — knowing Rohl will be the site of the war's most pivotal
           battle. The Abaculus will draw Olum to the city while Greyhawk
           abducts creatures for corruption and builds his forces. Begins
           building forces for the Great War — recruiting willing dragons and
           forcibly corrupting those who refuse at Olum nests. Creatures of
           Olum in the Tarantar Mountains begin to disperse without the
           Abaculus's pull to anchor them.
Year 2140: Arias Graymist dies (age 81). Sons inherit Harbinger Set.
2140-2148: Felgard-Dragonborne peace treaty brokered by Cyrus Graymist.
Year 2148: Great War — first attacks begin. Lord Yllyndel the Ancient dies
           sometime during this conflict.
Year 2165: Battle of Rohl. When the battle turns against him, Greyhawk flees
           attempting to reach the Abaculus to weaponize it against Karma and
           Adomorn — the same fatal miscalculation Ah'Tabai made. Karma and
           Adomorn pursue and kill him before he reaches it. Adomorn does not
           know that was Greyhawk's intention and therefore does not know the
           Abaculus is nearby. In the final instant, the Abaculus acts through
           the dying Greyhawk — his dying curse transforms Karma into a
           golden-skinned Kobold and binds him to the Burnt Plains directly
           above the Abaculus, as the intended vessel for Greyhawk's
           resurrection. Morgan, Victoria, Nathan killed. Harrad dissolves.
           Common Calendar resets to year 0.
Year 2190: Cyrus Graymist dies (age 86). Tomb near Jorvik. Staff believed
           still there.

         Diquyk is a Magni — but does not anticipate how far his powers have

         The Deep as a deliberate decoy force. Adomorn takes 2 Marauders
         through a Jaunt Gate deep within Dark Elf territory, putting them to
         sleep for transit, to flank from behind. Diquyk senses the gate
         transit and is tipped off. Diquyk intercepts Adomorn's spells and
         redirects them as blue flame, killing multiple Marauders. Mya defeats
         and kills Shedyak, acquires the Bow of the Dales. When Mya and Adomorn
         switch to physical combat, cutting off Diquyk's fuel supply, he is
         finally banished. All 10 Marauders dead. Only Mya and Adomorn survive
         the battle itself. TENTATIVE: Adomorn, badly wounded, dies shortly
         after banishing Diquyk — eventually rematerializes at Atral. Mya,
         unaware of his true nature, believes him dead. Diquyk banished to a
         separate physical parallel world where time moves more slowly. He is
         40 years old at banishment.
394 AW:  Gralvin's village destroyed by Tarik (age 7). Taken as gladiatorial slave.
399 AW:  Bor Glitterbeard born (Iron Dwarf, Fardar).
408 AW:  Gralvin (21) and Balric (23) escape together. Directed to Valeria.
         Gralvin and Valeria become friends, then lovers. Gralvin and Balric
         choose to stay and help her network.
~408-412 AW: Grimmon Herringer travels to Ellendale then Faelyn. Plains Elf
             emissary accompanies Grimmon to Faelyn. Grimmon tells Aer'Raenal
             and Mya firsthand stories of Tarik's atrocities. Mya volunteers
             to help. Aer'Raenal dispatches her.
413 AW:  Mya arrives at Valeria's — pointed there by the same network.
         Everything crystallizes. Simultaneous strikes by Plains Elves and
         Herringers across Kimora. Tarik overthrown and killed. Valeria dies
         in the final battle. Gralvin takes the throne at age 26. Iron Dwarves
         freed and return to Fardar. Grimmon Herringer (age 41) present.
427 AW:  Diquyk returns to Lanamyr through the Nexus on Myst, having spent
         approximately 1 subjective year in the banishment plane while 35
         years passed in Lanamyr. Physically aged to approximately 41 despite
         being 75 by Lanamyr reckoning. Finds his former work on Myst abandoned
         or gone. Brief flicker of hurt and fear before Abaculus redirects it.
         Returns to Burnt Plains. Begins working toward Greyhawk's resurrection.
429 AW:  Grimmon Herringer killed by Orc raiders (instruments of the Kimora
         framing operation). Age 57. Bor obtains the map from Grimmon's
         belongings.
430 AW:  Adenburry sinkhole event. Subterranean Creatures of Olum breach the
         town. Wilward's airship catches fire and tears in half. Bor and
         Qenwick, traveling overland from Fardar, are also present. The group
         fights together and exterminate the nest. Alexa eventually makes her
         way to Broldendul. Wilward stays in Adenburry to help rebuild. Maeglin,
         Bor, Skitter, and Qenwick travel together to inform Herringer members
         across Kimora of Grimmon's death. Qenwick returns to Fardar after.
432 AW:  PRESENT DAY. Diquyk (80 by Lanamyr reckoning, physically ~46) pursues
         Greyhawk's resurrection via Karma at the Burnt Plains. Mya Li (age 85)
         adventures with Balric Silverfox. Maeglin (age 78), Bor (age 33), and

         Alexa leads Four Crowns in Broldendul. Elsie (age 53) and Alita (age
         24) Varis run the Monowi Inn. A Relic of Creation sits in a glass vial
         around a Kobold's neck. Valindra walks the shore of her lake.
