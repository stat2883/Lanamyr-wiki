MAGNI:
- RESOLVED — Magni and moving the Abaculus. A Magni inherits the full magical
  capacity of their progenitor line but NOT the mind behind it. The Drasu mental
  fortitude and resilience — specifically the ability to resist the Abaculus's
  pull while remaining in close proximity long enough to move it — did not
  transfer through the Sundering. Moving the Abaculus safely requires both the
  magic and the resilience. Diquyk has the first, not the second. Adomorn
  remains singular.
  Anyone can still move it by physical contact at the cost of corruption, as
  Greyhawk did in 2136 of the 2nd Epoch. The Abaculus wants to be found and its
  standing pattern is to corrupt a powerful being and use them as a weapon, so a
  Magni attempting to move it is very likely the outcome it would welcome.
- A more recent Magni existing within Aer'Raenal's lifetime (born 244 AW) is to
  be created later — less powerful than Diquyk, giving the council of war a
  shared reference point and sparing Adomorn from having to explain how he knows
  Arias Graymist, who died in 2140 of the 2nd Epoch. Adomorn likely draws this
  comparison at the council.
- How widely the Draak and Drasu are remembered among present-day races, and
  therefore how much of the true Magni origin is knowable in-world: undecided.
