### Age Note (bible inconsistency flagged)
`Lanamyr_02_Overview_Cosmology.txt` lists Wood Elf childhood as 0–42 and young
adulthood as 42–62, but the Timeline has Mya joining the militia in 387 AW at
age 40 — below the childhood boundary. Kharis Ailwin (b. 347 AW) has the same
issue. This is a contradiction in confirmed lore, not a campaign decision.

The PCs at 45 sit cleanly inside young adulthood and do **not** inherit the
problem. The contradiction remains open on the bible side only — see
`reference/lore-deltas.md`.
