### The hall (opened session 2)

7. **The hall itself.** Dressed stone, pillared, extent unknown, runs **north
   and downslope** under the Tempest Peaks. Covered floor to ceiling in
   continuous inscription in a script that is not Aiwiya, Common or Ukuri.
   Ruled out in play: not a mine, not a temple, not a tomb — no altar, no focal
   point, no burials, no soot. Builders unknown. The seam in the ridge is a
   later back door chiselled in decades ago, not the original entrance.
8. **The quiet ones.** Tall, thin, cold-handed, veiled in daylight, no light of
   any kind underground, **entirely silent** — no speech, no signals. A
   disciplined, self-supplied working party that can **read** the hall.
   **Not yet identified in the fiction. Do not let anyone name them.**
9. **The cubes.** Stone, ~3 ft, placed in a line beside pillars, pale metal on
   top, flameless lights above, **freshly cut inscription circles around them**.
   At least one switched on while the party was present. One light went dark
   and reappeared nearer **without crossing the ground between.**
10. **The left-hand corridor at the T-junction.** Never explored. The dust says
    the quiet ones came out that way and left by the stair.
11. **The thing below.** Vast, cold, structured, far beneath everything — and
    explicitly **not** the thing that turned the lights on. Sharii alone.
12. **Hestra's marked pillar.** Three fresh scratches above where she was left,
    watered and alive. She was set aside to be collected, and the party took her
    out from under them.
13. **The stone set down on stone** at the far end of the crawl as the party
    left. Something had a hand on it. It did not follow.
