## ⚠ WHAT THE STRUCTURE IS — as of session 9

1. **⚠ IT IS A BAND ON A BELL, AND THERE ARE THREE RINGS.** The finished work is
   a **thirty-foot belt** with a level lower edge as true as the upper one, on a
   wall that undercuts beneath it. **The space opens out below the road.**
   - **Ring one (the road, 0 ft):** lit, failing, **worn**.
   - **⚠ Ring two (−85 ft):** finished, swept, four abreast, cubes seated square
     on every shelf, **all dark — and NOT ONE has a circle cut round it. No
     chips, no tools, no dust, no worn band.** Nobody ever tried to light these.
   - **⚠ Ring three (−190 ft):** **half-built.** Six empty shelves, then scored
     outlines, then a squared-off last cut and raw mountain. Chips swept left.
2. **⚠ THE WORK GOES DOWNWARD**, housings first — matching the vent-nine gallery
   and the half-mile of empty shelves. **WHICH SPLITS THE DARK IN TWO: the
   eleven north of the break WENT OUT; the ring below has NEVER BEEN LIT.
   Nobody above ground has any way to tell those apart.** Maevis's forty-to-sixty
   was counted off one line by somebody with no reason to think there was more
   than one.
3. **⚠ BELOW ALL OF IT THE WALL IS GONE.** A torn margin at ~−300 ft running out
   of sight both ways, with nothing past it, and **the warm air of the whole
   circuit comes up out of the hole where it used to be.**
   **Which finishes the arch: the top ring is not failing because something is
   hitting it. It is the only one left holding.**
4. **⚠ INSIDE IT THERE IS NO STRAIN.** At ~900 ft down a thread she crossed into
   the thing itself. It extends down and out past any boundary she can put on
   it, and all three rings hang off the top of it like a net off what it
   carries. **No attention. No malice. No mind she could find. Not pressing out.
   NOT TRYING TO GET OUT.**
   **⚠ AGAINST: Maevis signed the fracture — *the force came from the open side,
   from the middle* — and Mya built *we're in with it* on it. NEITHER IS
   WITHDRAWN. BOTH HALVES ARE ON THE PAPER.**
5. **⚠ FOUR SURGES A MINUTE.** The rising air **rotates** and comes in soft
   regular surges. Counted twice, a full minute each. **The rate they breathe
   at.** *No conclusion written. Do not resolve casually.*
6. **⚠ THE TWELFTH CUBE WENT OUT WITH HER LEGS ON IT.** She felt it slipping,
   continuously, like a wet knot. It flickered — *and these lights do not
   flicker* — and one of them put a hand flat on it beside her and the flicker
   stopped and her legs came free **in the same instant, with no way to tell
   which caused which.** Ninety minutes later it was dark. **No circle round it;
   nobody has got to it yet. The thirteenth is carrying.**
7. **⚠ NORTH OF THE SECOND BREAK THE ROAD IS WORN AGAIN.** The unwalked stretch
   is the piece **between the two breaks.** North of there it is in daily use
   **and not by anybody coming up from Faelyn.**
8. **THE RANKS AT THE FRESH BREAK ARE NOT REPAIRING.** Stone goes south hand to
   hand; **nothing comes north.** No fill, no timber, no new-cut block.
9. **⚠ THE ONE WHO CARRIES NOTHING.** Clean — **no dust on him anywhere while
   every other hand on that heap was grey.** Stood beneath her the whole span
   crossing. **Laid his open hand flat against the rock at HER height**, four
   feet up a wall, not at his own chest. **Carried her 900 ft north in three
   minutes, through the ranks, no head turning. Did not move in the sixty
   minutes she was below the rim.** Carried her back. **Has never once looked at
   her.**
10. **NOTHING BELOW THE RIM IS SWEPT.** A twenty-foot fan of smashed cube at
    −130 ft, dust mortared into the crevices. **Things fall past and nobody
    follows them down.**
11. **Still standing from s8:** the stair has no bottom and has never been walked
    on; the ledge is the road; the lamps are the cubes; trunk, branches, ladders;

## ⚠ THE LEAD STRIP

Hammered lead, hand-length, folded twice, two lines **pressed** in **Ukuri**.
Eastern road, **14 May 387**, no direction, a returned soldier's effects out of
Fardar way. **Dead stock; Caul looked at it twice and left it both times**,
because Ukuri is a script a scholar can name.

> ### ***Two thousand four hundred. Half measure. Thirty days.***
> ### ***Eighth road. They are not told.***

- **Alinar's own caveat:** the numbers and *thirty days* are certain. **The last
  four words are not** — *they are not told* / *are not to be told* / *have not
  been told yet*. *"The grammar is doing something I can hear and can't name."*
- **Ignis:** not a raiding party and not a garrison. **A population.** And *half
  measure* implies a full measure that normally exists.
- **Sharii:** *"Somebody is feeding twenty-four hundred people, and not telling
  them something."*
- **⚠ THIS ANSWERS VAEROTH'S OCTOBER QUESTION FROM THE FAR END** — *how many
  mouths* — which the east has never answered.
- **Standing of the translation, named by Maevis:** the least verifiable object
  in her possession. One reader, no second reader in the kingdom, and the
  translator cannot explain how he knows the language.

**The twenty-third of January, 387 AW. Mid-morning. Caldalus.**

nobody in the kingdom can read, and an old man sitting on a cot who has just
