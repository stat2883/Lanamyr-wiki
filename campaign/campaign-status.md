### The hall and the void

8. **The reply.** A **blank wax tablet with a stylus in its spine and one
   mason's test stroke in the corner** is lying in the junction, placed dawn of
   day nine. **Gone, answered, or untouched — all three mean something.**
9. **The void.** Beyond the left-hand corridor, past the end of the original
   construction and down a raw tunnel: a ledge above a space with **no visible
   far wall and no visible ceiling**, with **fifty-plus pale flameless lights**
   in arcs on the floor far below, each above a three-foot cube. **An entire
   arc is still unlit.** The pulse comes from there.
10. **The stair being cut down into it.** New, days old, the **only** stair —
    and the cubes were already placed down there by some other route.
    **It is for somebody who needs steps and has not arrived.**
11. **They are finishing the building, not excavating it.** The original
    inscription stops **mid-line**; eight feet later the dressed stone stops;
    beyond that is raw tunnel being cut, with the inscription already being
    carved into the new walls picking up where the old sentence ended.
12. **The depot** at ~180 ft down the left corridor: sealed water in quantity,
    rope, tools by size, unidentifiable dried food, and **forty to fifty folded
    veils.** Nothing was taken.
13. **The rubbing.** Two feet of their script, copied in wax from the fresh
    cutting at the left-hand turning. Nobody in Faelyn can read it. Maevis
    wants a scholar in Caldalus.
14. **The river stone** (Ignis carries it). Unmarked, matched to a sorted set,
    pocket-smooth, body-warm when given — and **made of rock that does not
    occur anywhere in Faelyn.** *Where is the river?*
15. **The open door.** Five blocks on the ground, a seven-foot doorway, and
    Maevis's verdict that rebuilding it is "a month and a quarry." **Faelyn now
    has an open door in its own border that Faelyn opened**, half a day above a
    village of forty-one, with winter coming.
16. **The head-turn.** At the edge of the lantern light, one of them **stopped
    and turned its head** — the first head-turn anyone has observed from any of
    them. Neva says it was looking at Sharii and is certain. Wick says the
    wolf. Mya says the stair. **The disagreement is the record. Do not
    resolve it.**

### The war and the region

17. **Alinar's hypothesis, unproven:** the Stone Giants may not be invading
    Faelyn — **they may be leaving home.** Two giants staggered on the same
    heading in s1; Knight's mother moved the den for the quiet ones and
    explicitly *not* for giants; and the whole mountain's game is now drifting
    south. A fox reported that **the ground itches** — worse higher up, much
    worse at night, started around when the leaves turned, and got suddenly
    worse recently. **Nothing is being eaten. There is no predator.**
18. **Nothing connects any of this to the Dark Elf pressure from the east or
    to whoever is directing the war.** Vaeroth insisted this be said out loud.
    It may be the same thing. It may be a thousand years older than the war.
19. **Vaeroth is writing to the king.** He has never written to a king. The
    earlier letter to the tacticians (s2) has still produced nothing.
20. **The watch — APPROVED IN PRINCIPLE, NAMES NOT CHOSEN.** A rotating pair
    based in Ashwell, walking up the draw daily to observe the seam from the
    treeline at 400 yards, never approaching, three-week rotations, resupply
    from Caldalus every three weeks. Candidates are Wick, Neva, either twin,
    and Dalla Sarn. **Snow closes the draw by January. Reporting lag to the
    capital is four and a half days.**
