### The hall and the void

9. **THE TABLET WAS TAKEN.** Left at dawn day nine. *Locate Object* from the
   draw: stationary for eight minutes, then **moving — away, down and north,
   not up the stair, at a walking pace** — and still moving when it passed out
   of range at ~9½ minutes. Nothing came for it for the better part of half an
   hour and then something did. **Nobody saw who, and nobody can.**
10. **Established limitation, now part of command planning:** *Locate Object*
    gives direction and movement only. Never distance, speed or identity. To
    know *who*, somebody must sit in that junction in the dark. **Vaeroth has
    refused to order that on his own authority.** Maevis: a man with a lantern
    among people who need none is not an observer, he is an audience.
11. **The void.** No visible far wall or ceiling. **Fifty-plus pale flameless
    lights** in arcs, each above a three-foot cube. **An entire arc still
    unlit.** The pulse comes from there.
12. **The stair being cut down into it.** New, the only one, and the cubes were
    already placed by some other route. **For somebody who needs steps and has
    not arrived.**
13. **They are finishing the building, not excavating it.**
14. **The depot** at ~180 ft: sealed water in quantity, rope, tools by size,
    dried food, **forty to fifty folded veils.** Untouched.
15. **The rubbing — LOST TO THE CHAIN.** A Caldalus scholar could not read it,
    could not place it, and **did not believe it was a daughter of anything he
    knows.** He asked to keep it; Maevis refused; three days later somebody
    considerably more senior asked less politely and **it went up the chain.**
    She is furious and vocal. **Who has it now is open.**
16. **The river stone** (Ignis carries it). Matched, sorted, pocket-smooth,
    body-warm when given, **made of rock that does not occur in Faelyn.**
    *Where is the river?* — noted silently at Dorn's Ford, which has one. **No
    connection drawn and no evidence of any kind. Do not resolve this casually.**
17. **The open door**, which cannot be shut. A month and a quarry.
18. **The head-turn.** Neva says Sharii, Wick says the wolf, Mya says the
    stair. **The disagreement is the record. Do not resolve it.**
19. **NEW — HESTRA'S TESTIMONY.** Given to Aldric on the bench, a fortnight
    after the fact, and it is the single most important thing established about
    the quiet ones to date. **Three of them**, counted by breathing, one
    lighter on the floor. And: the first time they set water by her it was a

    it was exactly where her hand came down.** They watched her fail once and
    never let her fail again. *"I've been fed by people and I've been kept by
    people, and those are different."*

### Ashwell and Dorn's Ford

26. **DORN'S FORD — PLAYED AND SIGNED.** Aldric asked in his own voice, in the
    open yard, including the shame. Terms:
    - **Game rights — unconditional, immediate.** Ashwell takes deer and hare
      under Dorn's Ford hedges, **first frost to first sowing**, reported to
      **Keth**, breeding does left in spring. **This closes the
      midwinter-to-spring gap.**
    - **Boundary — surveyed by Dovin Reyl**, walked with Merrick personally,
      including the nine acres the grandfathers fell out over. Lodged with the
      Office of Works. Dorn's Ford named on the plan's north edge.
    - **Charter — CONDITIONAL.** Ashwell goes on the roll now so the Caldalus
      clerk has a village to issue grain against. ⚠ **IT LAPSES AT THE SPRING
      ASSESSMENT IN MARCH IF THE LEVY RELIEF IS NOT GRANTED.**
27. **⚠ THE LEVY PETITION — THE SESSION'S LIVE DEBT.** Thale supplied the
    clause: **a chartered settlement that hosts and victuals a crown post is
    relieved against its levy by the heads it supports.** The Ashwell watch is
    that post. **He cannot grant it** — it is a petition over the commanding
    officer's signature, and **he put his own name on the recommendation before
    his captain had heard of it.** He walked straight into Vaeroth's office on
    arrival rather than be reported on. **The door is still closed. UNRESOLVED.**
28. **Merrick Dorn knows everything about the hall** — told in full by Ignis, in
    his own yard, in front of a dozen witnesses who will repeat it. He said he
    believed it and said why: *nobody inventing a tale tells you the part that's
    their own fault.* **This is now outside anyone's control.**
29. **Dorn's Ford has a deer problem it spent a season thanking the gods for.**
    Winter wheat, all through the hungry months. Merrick worked it out himself
    mid-conversation.
30. **Aldric told his entire village everything**, and **allowed the north
    meadow to be grazed in spring anyway**, on the strength of Hestra's
    testimony alone. Nobody goes up the north draw.
31. **COLL FENN.** Re-splinted the ankle every day for fourteen days at the
    same hour to the exact mark he was shown — **on a leg whose swelling had
    gone down** — and rubbed the skin raw, and checked his own work twice as
    taught, and carried on, because nobody told him to stop. He reported it
    himself, braced. Hestra said nothing for ten days: *"He'd have cried. And
    you said three weeks, lass."* **The warning has now come true once at
    almost no cost. Instructions to Coll must carry their own conditions.**
32. **Ashwell's spring is solved** (seed corn, Fenn loft). **Winter is now
    solved** (game rights). **What is not solved is March.**
