## ⚠ THE SIGNED ORDER — new, and it changes how this campaign is run

Signed day 49 in Vaeroth's office. **Full text in `sessions/006-the-ladder.md`.**
The load-bearing clauses:

1. **One objective per order, named, with a date on it.**
2. **Forward judgement inside the ground is held by Ignis, by name.** Nothing
   decided outside overrules her while she is out of sight, and nobody pretends
   otherwise afterwards. *(Reconciles with Sharii's autumn grant — Vaeroth
   conceded he had been running two incompatible doctrines at once.)*
3. **THE CONDITION: if any of them indicates she should leave, she leaves — at
   once, without weighing it.** Derived not from her answer but from her
   account: it is the one instruction she has ever obeyed inside that mountain,
   and it came from the thing in the room.
4. **Nobody follows her in. At all. For any reason.**
5. The outside party holds the count and the witness and **is not a rescue.**
   **5(b), in Alinar's words:** the "nobody follows you in" clause must be
   **said aloud to her face**, by each person, every time — not merely
   initialled.
6. Nothing taken; objects may be left to be answered and answers collected, at
   the junction, the seam, or a vent mouth.
7. **No fallback** (Thale's standing ruling).
8. Stated expiry. Weather beats the order every time.
9. **The consequence is held where the judgement is.** No person acting under
   this order may be described, in any report or inquiry, as having been
   following instructions — **there are none to follow.**

**Vaeroth signed last, under everybody**, for the first time in forty years of
writing orders. He required Ignis's signature first on the stated grounds that
she has no oath on her and a signature was the only binding available.

**⚠ DM consequence:** the usual shield is gone. When something goes wrong inside
the ground, *she was following orders* is not available to her, to the party, or
to the DM.

## ⚠ VAEROTH'S DOCTRINE, REWRITTEN BY HIM — session 6

He read the account, asked four questions, and then took his own winter's work

> *"A stopping condition is not a wall. **It is a promise.** It works on a person
> who wants to stop. I have spent three months congratulating myself on fences
> and what I had was a line of people being polite to me."*

> *"And the first time I put one in front of somebody who had already decided to
> pay for crossing it, it did exactly nothing. **It told her the price.**"*

> *"I built the whole thing on a nine-year-old who does exactly what he's told,
> all the way down."*

> *"The only person in my company who has broken one of my conditions is the one
> person I deliberately made it impossible to discipline. **I disarmed myself in
> October and I was pleased with myself for it.** I'd do it again. That's the
> trouble."*

**No charge, no reprimand, no removal from forward work** — *"punishing it would
be theatre, and I'd be punishing her for a condition I'd have cancelled a day
later."*

**What he had actually written north**, five days too late, was **yes to
contact** — answer what they leave, leave things to be answered, at the junction
or the seam, in daylight, nobody out of sight of two other people, with an
expiry. *"I gave permission for a conversation. **And you had a meeting.**"*

**He refused to settle anything at midnight**, citing Thale's own fallback
ruling against himself, and then did a thing he has never done for anybody:
**"you'll read it before I sign it."**

**And he noticed the other thing:** *"I have spent this entire winter handing
authority down to recruits. And this afternoon the three of you walked in here
and handed one back. Which is correct — naming four people out of twenty is a
captain's job and I'd have stopped you. I'd just like it on the record that I
noticed, and that I don't yet know whether what I'm looking at is good judgement
or relief, and neither do you."*

## ⚠ THE BOARD

Bellis Ordow has left a blank space beside Ignis's name since the granary yard
in the rain, on the stated grounds that *"that's hers to tell me."* On day 49,
unprompted, **he filled it in** — with one word he had asked the captain for and
copied, because **he writes down what he is told and he had no word of his own
for a person who goes out of sight.**

> **IGNIS** — *judgement.*

She asked him, confused and annoyed, **what one would even write in that spot.**
He explained the board: *"it isn't what people are good at. It's **where to look
for you when it's gone wrong.**"* — *Kade: forward. Corrin: where she can see
the whole of it. Pallowen: not where you left him. Ailwin: between — the only
one I've never had to change.* And **Tam Ellisan's space has been empty since
the autumn**, *"not because I'm being delicate. He hasn't done anything yet."*

Then: *"**It's a pencil.** Everything on this board is pencil. I have rubbed out
more words than I've written."* He held it out to her, blunt end first.
*"It's your spot. I only keep the board."* And, because Alinar told her six weeks
ago that Ordow likes being surprised: *"Two hundred years of this and I am very
rarely surprised. **I'd like it on the record that I was.**"*

**She rubbed out his word and wrote her own.**

> **IGNIS** — *where she was told not to go* — **and back**

**The last two words are Ordow's**, added in his own hand as the one privilege
the job carries. His reasoning: hers is **the only entry on the board that is
actually an instruction** — *"if you go missing, nobody wastes an hour looking
where you were posted; they go and read the order and then they go to the line
in it that says* don't*"* — and the clause she left off is the one his whole
trade turns on: *"**Things that go out and come back are stores. Things that go
out and don't are losses.** You went down and you came up. That's the bit you
left off, and you'd have left it off, because you lot hand me the knife every
time and think it's modesty."*

*"It's pencil. If it ever stops being true I'll rub out the last two words
myself and I'll not enjoy it."* Then, to the sacks: *"Surprised twice now.
That's a record."*

## Session 7 opens

**387 AW, day forty-nine or shortly after. Caldalus, deep winter.**

A signed order with a recruit's name in it as the judgement and no sentence
behind it. Four of the squadron's twenty going east on the twenty-eighth with no
return date, and none of them the people who have been under the mountain.
A boundary that needs a clerk and a page that needs glancing at. A draw that
shuts in January and a staircase that finishes in spring, with nothing anywhere
that says what *done* looks like.

**And a wolf at two feet, who will be there tomorrow at the same hour,
downwind, because he was given a job.**
