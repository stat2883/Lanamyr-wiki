## ⚠ WHAT THE STRUCTURE IS — as of session 8

1. **⚠ IT IS A RING, AND WE ARE INSIDE IT.** The lamp line curves away on both
   hands and closes. **Radius one and a quarter to one and a half miles**, error
   large. **Depth roughly three hundred and forty feet** below the mountain
   surface. **Maevis's centre and Dovin Reyl's land in the same few hundred
   yards — three constructions by three methods. She refuses to call it a
   convergence.**
2. **⚠ THE LAMPS ARE THE CUBES**, on cut shelves in the wall, **at arm's reach
   from the stair.** **Fifty-eight lit.** Evenly spaced and **all at one height —
   somebody ran a level**, which means they have a surveyor's trade and not only a
   mason's.
3. **⚠ IT IS ONE CONTINUOUS LINE WITH GAPS IN IT**, not three arcs. Three gaps
   resolvable from the ledge. **This independently reproduces *they open new
   ground*.**
4. **⚠ THE MISSING ARE NOT MISSING. THEY ARE INSTALLED AND FAILED.** Dark cubes
   on finished shelves, each ringed by two and three concentric cut circles, the
   outermost unfinished. **Forty to sixty of them. Maevis publicly retracted
   "two-thirds done" and will not say whether it has ever been nearer finished
   than it is now.**
5. **⚠ THE ARCH. IT FAILS IN STEPS AND THE STEPS COME FASTER.** At the break there
   is **no load in the stone at all**, and **the two cut ends either side are
   carrying more.** Every failure loads its neighbours. **Which matches the
   torn-eared female exactly: *it did not grow, it stepped*, more times than she
   could count, each step shedding animals, beginning before the leaves went.**
6. **⚠ IS IT COMING DOWN. ANSWERED: YES, AND FASTER EACH TIME.** Asked by a wolf
   in session 6 and by Aldric in session 7, independently. **Nobody in Ashwell or
   Caldalus has been told.**
7. **⚠ WHICH SIDE IS FAELYN ON. ANSWERED: THE INSIDE.** Maevis read the fracture
   and gave a rare conclusion — **the face was struck from the open side, from the
   middle.** Mya: *"We're in with it."*
8. **THE STAIR HAS NO BOTTOM.** Four hundred feet, unfinished, ending in a
   half-shaped tread and air. **Never walked on. It is for somebody who has not
   arrived.** The warm air of the whole circuit comes up past its last tread —
   **the other end of Maevis's draught instrument.**
9. **⚠ THE LEDGE IS THE ROAD AND IT IS WORN.** Eighteen-inch polished band.
   **Gallery mouths at intervals** running back into the rock. **Trunk, branches,
   ladders** — which is why there has never been a track on that snow and why they
   have never used the seam. **The road is worn; the stair never has been.**
10. **THE FAR SIDE OF THE FIRST BREAK IS NOT WORN.** Nobody walks it from here.
11. **⚠ ONE CUBE IS CRACKED CORNER TO CORNER AND STILL LIT**, first past the
    break, **braced with at least five complete concentric circles.** The one that
    took the hit, carrying double, shored up over and over by people who came back
    to it.
12. **⚠ THE SECOND BREAK IS FRESH.** Wider, span unseen, **dust in the air**, heap
    unfinished, **more of them working it than have ever been seen in one place**,
    and **three dark cubes past it.** The failure propagated.
13. **VENT ELEVEN — a candidate at last: a chimney, not a working.** Warm with no
    work under it because the deep exhaust comes up it. **This does not explain
    why it is cleaned inside the week with nobody under it.** No right-hand column.
    **Four times.**
14. **THE LOW LAMP.** East end of the furthest resolvable span, eight to ten feet
    below the line — **and the shelf it stands on is cut low too.** Deliberate.
    **Maevis has offered a day's surface traverse from the seam to test its
    bearing against vent eleven. Not taken.**
15. **THEY STOPPED, AND THEY DID NOT RESUME.** Nine of them set tools down in the
    row by size and stood for nineteen minutes while six people were on their
    working face, and **everything in the mountain restarted at once eleven
    minutes after the party had gone.** Second simultaneous restart this winter.
16. **THE VOID, THE DEPOT, THE FIFTY-PLUS CUBES.** Depot still untouched —
    **sealed water, rope, baskets, worn tools by size, dried food, forty or fifty
    folded veils.** *Not a people with nothing. A people with no possessions.*
