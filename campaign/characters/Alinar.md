## Class Features — Level 1

### Favored Enemy: Monstrosities
Advantage on Survival checks to track monstrosities and on Intelligence checks
to recall information about them. **No language gained** — Creatures of Olum do
not speak.

**Campaign note:** this is the Creatures of Olum pick. Per the bible they are
corrupted living creatures — sickly gray-to-black with glowing red eyes, drawn
primarily from wildlife (bears, deer, boars) — and are ruled **monstrosities**
at this table rather than undead, since they are corrupted rather than raised.
They dissolve to harmless black dust on death. They appear throughout Lanamyr,
not only in this war.

### Natural Explorer: Forest
In forest terrain: difficult terrain doesn't slow the group's travel; cannot
become lost except by magic; remains alert to danger even while tracking,
foraging, or navigating; can move stealthily at a normal pace when alone; finds
twice as much food when foraging; when tracking, learns the creatures' exact
number, sizes, and how long ago they passed.

## Character

**The twin dynamic.** Alinar is the younger brother by a few minutes but has
always been the one leading. Sharii follows him and looks up to him. Alinar's
choice to become a Ranger is what set Sharii's path — whether Alinar knows the
weight of that is undeveloped, and is worth playing with.

**Appearance.** Average height for a Wood Elf male (roughly 5'7"), lean, light
tan skin, dark brown hair, dark eyes. Unremarkable. He and Sharii are difficult
to tell apart at a glance.

**He knows about Sharii.** Alinar is aware of his brother's involuntary
sensitivity to magic and has been for their whole lives. He does **not**
understand it, has no framework for it, and cannot explain it any better than
Sharii can. He has never had adequate words for it. How he feels about it —
protective, unsettled, dismissive, or some mix — is undeveloped and worth
playing with.

**Why he enlisted, 387 AW.** The twins grew up in Caldalus. As the Stone Giant
attacks in the north began, they watched messengers and refugees come into the
city asking for help. **Alinar wanted to lend a hand.** That was the whole of
it — no grand cause, no grievance, just an unwillingness to watch people ask
for help and do nothing.

Sharii followed him. Alinar may or may not have registered the weight of that
at the time.

**Note:** this is the same impulse that drives Mya Li — compassion and a refusal
to stand by. They enlisted for the same reason, from opposite ends of Faelyn's
society. Worth surfacing in play if the moment comes.

**Personality, ideals, bonds, flaws:** undeveloped.
