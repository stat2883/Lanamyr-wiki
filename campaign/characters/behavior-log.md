# PC Behaviour Log

A running, dated record of **what the player characters actually did** —
observed actions, not declared traits. Covers Sharii, Alinar, and (from her
arrival before session 3) Ignis.

**Why this file exists.** The character sheets list personality, ideals, bonds
and flaws as *undeveloped*, deliberately, so they can emerge through play. This
file is the compromise: it preserves the pattern across sessions without
boxing either character in. A future session reads evidence rather than
re-deriving a personality from twelve pages of event logs, and the DM reaches
for the same answer the players would.

**Rules for this file:**
- Record **behaviour with a date and a session number**. Never record a trait.
  Not *"Sharii is empathetic"* — instead *"S2: asked Mya's opinion first and in
  public."*
- Everything here is **reversible**. If a character turns out to be someone
  else, an entry simply becomes a thing he used to do.
- Record **what NPCs observed**, where it matters, since that is what drives
  how they respond later.
- Append only. Newest at the bottom of each character's section.

## Sharii

**S1 — 387 AW, Caldalus barracks.** Held back on the first evening and
catalogued the whole squadron's rough strengths rather than introducing
himself. Acted on that read tactically a week later.

**S1 — 387 AW, Caldalus barracks.** Needled by Neva Corrin about the scale
mail. Leaned into the insult instead of deflecting it — *"it's bear bait, and
not everyone has the courage to be the bait"* — and took ownership of the
nickname. Kept the joke running himself for the rest of the session.

**S1 — 387 AW, Ashwell.** Volunteered to be live bait, sprinting at a Stone
Giant in the open to pull it off fleeing villagers. Dropped to 0 HP doing it.
Was back on his feet and shooting before the fight ended, and did **not** close
to melee again afterward — stayed at range at 1 HP rather than risk it.

**S2 — 387 AW, Ashwell.** Spent a spell slot to interview a four-month-old
wolf, then argued the wolf's testimony to a sergeant on the grounds that
*an animal has no capacity to lie, and everyone else in this yard does.*
Delivered to the whole squadron, not privately.

**S2 — 387 AW, Ashwell.** Asked **Mya her opinion first, by name, in front of
everyone**, explicitly in order to establish that her input was valued. She
understood exactly what he had done. *She did not thank him and will not.*

**S2 — 387 AW, the north draw.** Removed his scale mail and left it in the
village so as not to be the loud one on an approach. Took AC 11 into giant
country for it. Line to his brother: *"I trust my brother and Mya not to let me
die out there."*

**S2 — 387 AW, the hall.** When Unbidden fired and he lost speech, he reported
it afterward in a flat, affectless voice — facts end to end, no cadence —
and closed with *"Don't ask me how I know."* **Alinar recognised the voice; it
is how Sharii holds a thing at arm's length so it can't touch him.** Nobody
else in the squadron knows that.

**S2 — 387 AW, Caldalus, Vaeroth's office.** Referred to Mya as "the princess"
in front of a captain. When she reacted, **he did not retreat — he escalated
toward honesty.** Disclosed *Unbidden* directly to a superior officer
unprompted, then named the real reason he had invoked her: not the weight of
her title, but *the risk to her identity that she took by speaking at all.*
Put his own secret on a captain's desk to defend hers. Persuasion 22.
**Witnessed by Mya, Alinar, Thale and Kharis.**

**S2 — 387 AW, Caldalus, downtime.** Burned every spell slot he had, every day,
for eight consecutive days, building a wordless working vocabulary with Knight
rather than training the animal to obey commands. Abandoned the obedience
approach the moment he discovered the wolf had assigned himself a job.

**S2 — 387 AW, Caldalus, downtime.** Traded 2 points of AC for stealth, with a
documented habit of running at things on purpose. Accepted the trade knowingly.

**S3 — 387 AW, the Caldalus gate.** Answered a captain's question **on
Ignis's behalf** before she could, and was wrong ("obviously she's a scout").
Publicly corrected by her. Reframed the whole exchange to his brother within
the minute as a relief: *"Thank god I'm no longer the focal point of
anything."*

**S3 — 387 AW, the north face.** Was prepared to ignore a standing order to
attempt a capture, weighed it on the merits, and **withdrew because the
arithmetic was bad, not because of the order.** Said so later, unprompted, to
both his sergeant and his captain.

**S3 — 387 AW, the shelf.** Proposed conjuring water behind the sealed plug as
a lever. The proposal was wrong and the direction was right; the engineer built
the plan that opened the door out of it. He asked her for her opinion on his
next idea rather than arguing for his own.

**S3 — 387 AW, the shelf, dawn.** Approached Thale privately and asked for his
honest assessment of their captain. On being given it, **volunteered the
party's near-disobedience in return**, framed explicitly as owed in exchange
for candour — and kept every word of it in the first person, naming nobody
else. Vaeroth noticed the shielding and chose not to ask the others.

**S3 — 387 AW, the shelf.** Told Thale *"my only loyalty is to my brother"* and
dismissed Vaeroth as *"a figurehead... I'd rather live to see another 40
years."* Was corrected hard on both counts by a sergeant who had just defended
him, and did not argue back.

**S3 — 387 AW, Vaeroth's fire.** Asked permission to speak freely and then told
a company commander to his face that he would not promise to obey an order he
judged wrong in the moment. Was not punished; was instead **formally made
"forward judgment"** with the power to stop the party, and told plainly that
the cost of that now lands on other people with his name attached.

**S3 — 387 AW, past the horse lines.** **Convened the forward party privately**
to share his view of the captain, out of earshot of the column. The civilian
engineer refused to attend and walked out. Mya attended, agreed with the
substance and objected to the venue in terms specific to her own position. He
then announced he would defer to his brother's preference and **left
abruptly.**

**S3 — 387 AW, the ledge.** Read the work rather than the workers: identified
that the staircase was new, was the only one, and was therefore **not for the
people cutting it.** The single most useful finding anyone made down there.

**S3 — 387 AW, the ledge.** **Called the withdrawal himself** — "not much more
to learn without risking detection" — and then **opened the floor for
objections before anyone moved.**

**S3 — 387 AW, the junction.** Asked for Ignis's opinion, then Maevis's, then
**gave the final decision on what to leave to Mya**, in front of a captain,
for the second time in two sessions. She told him again to stop doing it and
took the decision anyway.

### Pattern so far — observations, not conclusions
- He notices what a thing costs a person and says so out loud, in public,
  including when it costs him.
- He follows his brother's decisions and then does something entirely his own
  once he gets there.
- He handles the Unbidden by going flat and getting the sentence over with.
- He volunteers to be the one in front, and he is the squishiest person in the
  squadron. These two facts have not yet collided fatally.
- **New, s3:** he escalates toward honesty under pressure and keeps doing it
  past the point of advantage — to a sergeant, to a captain, about himself.
- **New, s3:** he hands decisions to other people. Mya twice, his brother
  twice, the room once. He also answers for people who did not ask him to.

### Live question
Per `../campaign-status.md` thread 4: he enlisted because Alinar did. The open
question is whether that borrowed conviction becomes his own. **Note for the
DM: the evidence above suggests he may already have stopped following and not
noticed.** Do not resolve this for him.

**Sharpened in s3.** Thale put it to him directly after hearing *"my only
loyalty is to my brother"*: *"You've put yourself in front of a captain for the
princess, got a village its spring corn, and walked away from a prisoner
because it'd cost forty men their quiet. You want to have another look at that
sentence sometime. It might not be true any more."* Sharii did not answer.
Later the same day he deferred to Alinar's preference and walked out of his own
meeting. **Both of those are now on the record and they do not agree with each
other.**

## Alinar

**S1 — 387 AW, Caldalus barracks.** Sat with Wick Pallowen by choice and built
the squadron's other real partnership out of barbed small talk. Told Wick
plainly why he enlisted: refugees arrived asking for help and he preferred
being useful to being alarmed on their behalf.

**S1 — 387 AW, the patrol.** Found the second giant print nobody else caught,
which changed the squadron's entire read of the situation.

**S1 — 387 AW, Ashwell.** Closed to blades on both giants. Did the triage work
afterward alongside Kharis without being asked.

**S2 — 387 AW, Ashwell.** Took the village count and the food survey off
Aldric's hands without asking permission, because Aldric was a farmer being
asked to be a mayor for the first time.

**S2 — 387 AW, the meadow shelf.** **Opened the question of what to do with the
boy to the entire squadron**, in front of the boy, and let Wick, Neva, Kharis
and Mya each argue it before anything was decided.

**S2 — 387 AW, the collapse.** Committed silently to not leaving without the
grandmother or knowledge of her fate, **said nothing about it**, and went down
on his stomach into a twelve-foot crawl first. Went last on the way out.

**S2 — 387 AW, the hall.** When Vaeroth later asked for the account, he gave a
flat list with no adjectives and no argument. Untrained Charisma 19. **This is
what actually moved the captain**, not the persuasion.

**S2 — 387 AW, Caldalus.** Handed Sharii his entire 10 gp for the armour
without being asked and without comment.

**S2 — 387 AW, Caldalus.** Spent eight days of downtime on **paperwork** for
forty-one people he had met once. Returned to the same clerk's desk four days
running and recited the same list of names until someone dealt with him.
Found the seed-corn release by accident and did not stop there.

**S3 — 387 AW, the road north.** Walked beside the new arrival for a day and
**handed over everything he knew about Ashwell unasked** — forty-one people, a
burnt barn, a jar of preserve and an apology — and did not ask her the question
Ordow had left hanging. Also told her how to read Ordow, unprompted.

**S3 — 387 AW, the road north.** Identified the real obstacle in his own plan
before anyone else saw it: *"it's a fine sentence until you ask what Dorn's
Ford gets."* Had been chewing on it for eight days and had not solved it.

**S3 — 387 AW, Ashwell.** **Refused everything the village offered** — floor
space, food, the smokehouse — and had the squadron camp outside the walls so
that forty-one people who had lost a barn were not asked to feed twenty-two
soldiers. Cut Aldric's objection off in four words.

**S3 — 387 AW, Ashwell.** Played the flagged scene: gave Aldric the cart
manifest by number, then the charter route, then **asked the headman for ideas
rather than presenting a plan.** Aldric supplied all three levers without
realising he had.

**S3 — 387 AW, Ashwell.** With Wick, **split the flour four ways across four
buildings** rather than stacking it. Nobody asked them to. Aldric: *"We've
always put it in the barn."*

**S3 — 387 AW, the shelf.** **Stepped aside and let Vaeroth speak to Aldric**
rather than take the conversation himself, having just been the man Aldric was
relieved to see.

**S3 — 387 AW, the north face.** Backed off his own capture plan on Mya's
argument and **said out loud that he was doing so**, naming her as the reason,
before anyone could call it his idea.

**S3 — 387 AW, the left-hand corridor.** Set the march order around the
druid's thirty feet, **put Wick forward at the edge of it on purpose**, and
stopped the party to listen when his brother reported a sensation he could not
explain.

**S3 — 387 AW, the raw tunnel.** When the engineer fell and made the only
noise anyone made underground, **he had her off the floor and onto his back
before the last chip stopped moving** — at Strength 9, uphill, for as long as
he could carry her, and did not look back while doing it.

**S3 — 387 AW, the junction.** Drew both blades, put himself in the front rank,
and **challenged the thing in Common at thirty feet** exactly as he had told
the party he would, neither early nor late.

**S3 — 387 AW, the fire.** Took the accumulated facts of a fortnight and
assembled the hypothesis nobody else had: *the Stone Giants may not be invading
Faelyn, they may be leaving home.* Offered it as a hypothesis and accepted
immediately that it could not be proven.

**S3 — 387 AW, the fire.** **Asked Maevis directly what she thought** after
noticing she had gone quiet, which nobody else had done all evening. Her answer
dismantled the party's plan and produced a better one.

### Pattern so far — observations, not conclusions
- He leads by going first, not by arguing. He commits and lets people catch up.
- He opens decisions to the room rather than ruling on them, then acts without
  waiting once the room has spoken.
- He is persuasive specifically because he does not try to be.
- He does the unglamorous version of the job. Twice now, the thing that
  mattered most was something he found while being patient.
- **New, s3:** he gives credit away by name, in public, including when the idea
  was his first. Three separate times.
- **New, s3:** he notices who in the room has not spoken, and asks them.

**Added s3:** he also now knows his brother has started convening private

## Ignis

*Joined before session 3. Entries begin with the granary yard scene on the eve
of the survey column's departure.*

**Pre-S3 — 387 AW, the granary yard, Caldalus.** First act on meeting the
party was to **put her hand out to the wolf before speaking to either human**,
and to greet the animal warmly and by no name at all — *"Hey handsome."*
Animal Handling 12 (unproficient). Knight did not come to her hand; he crossed
four feet, scented her at length, and **sat down just out of reach.** Sharii
noted aloud that this is not something Knight does with anyone.

**Pre-S3 — 387 AW, the granary yard.** Gave Ordow her name when asked,
plainly and without embroidery.

**Pre-S3 — 387 AW, the granary yard.** Asked directly by Ordow *what she is
for*, in front of a listening squadron, she answered **"I am here as support
for the escort squadron."** She did not name her class, her training, her
guild, or her craft. Ordow wrote it down verbatim, told her to her face that
it was an honest word that lets a person not say a thing, and **deliberately
left room on the board beside it.** Alinar visibly wanted to ask and did not.

**S3 — 387 AW, the Caldalus gate.** Asked by a captain what to write beside her
name, **after another PC had answered wrongly on her behalf**, she corrected
him flatly and in public and disclosed her class, her role and her value in one
sentence: *"Actually, I'm a druid and I'm great for healing, which you're going
to need."* **This answers the question Ordow left open in the granary yard, and
she answered it to a captain rather than to Ordow.**

**S3 — 387 AW, the road north.** Sought out the sergeant unprompted and gave
him **her ranges, her limits and her spell count** — healing distance, how many
times, the thirty-foot radius, the cantrip — so he could build orders around
them. Asked why: *"Having information keeps you well prepared and less people
killed."*

**S3 — 387 AW, four nights running.** Spent slots every night on the comfort
and cover of forty people who were not her squadron — dry ground under the
bedrolls, fog on the perimeter — without being asked and without mentioning it.

**S3 — 387 AW, Ashwell.** Treated an old woman's badly-setting ankle, and then
**spent the evening teaching a nine-year-old to do it himself** for the three
weeks she would not be there. Made him name the herbs back and let him check
his own work twice.

**S3 — 387 AW, Ashwell.** Asked four families why they left Dorn's Ford two
generations ago, listened past the facts, and came back with the thing that
actually matters — **that the obstacle is shame, not grievance.**

**S3 — 387 AW, the shelf.** Turned a bonfire violet and green with a cantrip,
knowing it added no heat, **for the forty people who had been carrying wood
since dawn.** When the engineer said "it's not hotter," she said "no," and when
asked if it was for them, said "yes."

**S3 — 387 AW, the plug.** Spent her entire daily allowance of slots for two
nights and a day on stone — flooding joints at dusk, quenching a fire-set face
— as a labourer. Kept the ordered reserve untouched the whole time.

**S3 — 387 AW, the camp.** Wild Shaped into a wolf and **played with Knight for
an hour** in front of the squadron. Later cut a long piece off the better deer
and gave it to him by hand.

**S3 — 387 AW, the north face.** **Declined to spend her last reserved slot**
on the capture plan she herself had proposed, on the grounds that she would be
the one responsible if the rest of the column paid for it.

**S3 — 387 AW, the seam.** Volunteered to go through a four-inch gap alone as a
one-hit-point animal, took a sergeant's objections one at a time without
flinching, and went in front of a captain to get it authorised rather than
round him. *"I'm not a delicate flower."*

**S3 — 387 AW, the junction.** With a thing that had followed them five hundred
feet standing thirty feet away, **walked up to it alone**, crouched, took the
stone it had put down, and bowed her head before turning her back on it.

**S3 — 387 AW, the fire.** Argued for attempting contact on the grounds that
the head-turn was *"a comma, not a period"* — and that the beings who know what
is emptying the mountain are the only ones who can say so.

### Pattern so far — observations, not conclusions
- She tells people her capabilities, in detail, unprompted, to anyone who can
  use them — and gives the same answer to a captain, a sergeant and a wolf.
- She spends herself on people outside her own unit: a village, a column, a
  child, an animal.
- She went to the animal before the people on day one and has gone toward every
  unknown thing since, including the one in the corridor.
- She is the one who says the uncomfortable thing to Sharii's face.

## What NPCs have observed

**Mya Li.** Has watched Sharii's Unbidden fire twice and **chose not to ask**.
Told him on the road that it was his, that she'd believe him when he told her,
and asked only *"Can I use it?"* Heard him name the cost of her speaking, in
public, to a captain. Told him afterward not to do it again — and then, without
turning round: *"I noticed."* **She does not thank people. She files them.**

**Sgt. Serwyn Thale.** Accepted a wolf as a witness on Sharii's argument. Has
begun asking both twins what they think before he decides. Gave Sharii a wall
to stand behind at the shelf ("a draught and a bad feeling in the report")
without being asked to.

**Capt. Ilyen Vaeroth.** Knows about Unbidden. Credited Sharii for saying *what
to do is beyond my experience* rather than telling a captain his business.
Corrected him sharply on invoking Mya's title in a building where it reads as
the king's daughter wanting soldiers sent somewhere. Was turned by **Alinar's**
flat account, not Sharii's argument. Told Sharii to his face that protecting
him and using him were the same act.

**Neva Corrin.** Stopped needling Sharii for four days after the hall. Paid 8 gp
toward his armour and denied it. Called the armour swap "the right call" out
loud before she could stop herself.

**Kharis Ailwin.** Volunteered to stand with them on the food fight. Stood
behind Alinar's desk argument for four days. Said *"You got them spring."*
**Per `../npcs/mya-li.md`, this closeness is what determines whether the twins
are among the few who know about him and Mya from 388/389 AW onward. It is now
forming.**

**Bellis Ordow.** Has Ignis's name on his tally board with the word
*support* written beside it and space left deliberately empty. Told her
plainly that it was a word that lets a person not say a thing. He is not
hostile about it and will not raise it again unprompted; he writes down what
people tell him and then watches to see whether it was true.

**Knight.** Broke his own established pattern on meeting Ignis — neither
making a fool of himself nor putting himself between her and Sharii, which
are the only two things he has ever done with a stranger. He crossed to within
four feet, scented her, sat, and later walked her across the yard to her door
and came back unbidden. **Neither brother knows what it means and neither do
I. Leave it unexplained for now.**

**Alinar.** Registered that Ignis had dodged Ordow's question and chose not to
press it, then volunteered useful information about Ashwell instead. Also told
her, unprompted, how to read Ordow — which is the closest thing to a welcome
he has offered anyone outside the squadron.
