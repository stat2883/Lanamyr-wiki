# Kharis Ailwin

Bible-confirmed character. **Dies in 392 AW.** He is not a Marauder.

---

## Identity

- Wood Elf, male. Born **347 AW**. **Age 40 in 387 AW** — the same age as Mya.
- Common-born. No title, no connections.
- Enlists at essentially the same time as Mya Li and the twins, in the same
  intake. Assigned to the same squadron.

---

## Why He Is Here

He survived a **Stone Giant attack on a small Wood Elf village** and fled to
Caldalus to deliver word of it. **His family was killed in that attack.**

That is his entire motivation and it does not soften. He is not here out of
duty, patriotism, or ambition. He is here because something took everything from
him and he intends to be standing in front of it the next time it tries.

Play him accordingly: steady, quiet, and harder than he looks. Not brooding —
he is good company, and genuinely kind to people who are struggling, which is
most of the squadron in 387 AW. The grief sits underneath rather than on top.
He rarely discusses the village. He will if asked directly by someone he
trusts.

---

## Arc

- **387 AW** — Enlists. Shows great promise from the start, as does Mya.
- **388/389 AW** — He and Mya become serious. **Kept quiet.** Only their
  closest friends knew. The twins may or may not be among those friends,
  depending on play.
- **392 AW** — **Killed** during Diquyk's first major push, the engagement that
  becomes Mya's most crushing defeat.

His death is a fixed beat. It happens in a general slaughter, not a duel —
Diquyk killed a multitude of soldiers before the Wood Elves retreated. The
party is likely present.

---

## Approximate Mechanics

**Fighter**, **Protection** fighting style — he puts himself between danger and
other people, which is the whole man in one mechanic.

| Year | Approx. Level |
|---|---|
| 387 AW | 1 |
| 388–391 AW | 2–6 |
| 392 AW | 7 — dies |

Keep him roughly comparable to the PCs.

---

## DM Notes

- He is the squadron's emotional anchor for five years, which is the point. The
  loss should land on the party, not just on Mya.
- He carries no plot-critical knowledge.
- **Do not foreshadow his death heavily.** The bible's version is abrupt and
  buried in a rout. That is more effective than a telegraphed sacrifice.
