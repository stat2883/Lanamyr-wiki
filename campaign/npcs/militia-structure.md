# The Faelyn Militia — 387 AW

**Campaign-invented structure.** The bible confirms Faelyn has a militia that
Mya Li joins in 387 AW and that she later commands men, but gives no
organizational detail. Everything below is built for play and is **not bible
canon** unless explicitly promoted. See `../reference/lore-deltas.md`.

---

## Scale and Character

Faelyn has not fought a war in living memory. The militia in 387 AW is small,
underfunded, and built for bandits and dangerous wildlife — not for Stone Giants
coming down out of the Tempest Peaks. Stone Giant attacks on the outskirts have
just triggered the first serious recruitment drive in generations.

The practical consequence: **almost nobody in this army has ever fought a war.**
The sergeants are competent and untested. Drill is real but theoretical. This is
a force learning its trade while being attacked, and it should feel like one in
the early years.

It is also a **mixed force**. Faelyn is Wood Elf domain, but humans, gnomes, and
others live there, and all of them are being recruited.

---

## Structure

- **Squadron** — roughly 20, led by a sergeant. The working unit.
- **Company** — several squadrons under a captain.
- Above that, Faelyn's tacticians and ultimately King Aer'Raenal.

## The PCs' Squadron

Sharii and Alinar are assigned here at enlistment in 387 AW. **Mya Li and
Kharis Ailwin are in the same squadron**, having enlisted at essentially the
same time.

Roughly 20 bodies: the six named below, the four principals (Sharii, Alinar,
Mya, Kharis), and around ten unnamed others who can be named as needed and who
should be treated as real people when they die.

### Sergeant Serwyn Thale
Wood Elf, 212. Squadron sergeant and the twins' direct superior. Twenty years
in the militia, all of it chasing poachers and putting down rabid animals. He is
a genuinely good drillmaster who has never seen a battle, and he knows it. Dry,
patient, unwilling to pretend he has answers he doesn't. He treats Mya exactly
like every other recruit, which is precisely what she wants and which almost
nobody else manages.
*Fighter 3, Protection fighting style. Reliable, not exceptional.*

### Neva Corrin
Wood Elf, 88. The best natural archer in the squadron and insufferable about it
until she isn't. Sardonic, quick, allergic to sincerity. She will needle Sharii
about the scale mail — the noise, the weight, all of it — for as long as he
wears it. Under the deflection she is frightened, and hides it badly.
*Ranger 2, Archery.*

### Bellis Ordow
Wood Elf, 260. Quartermaster and cook, too old for the line and aware of it.
Knows everything about everyone before they do. The squadron's information
exchange and its unofficial conscience. If the party wants to know something
about another soldier, Bellis is the answer.
*Commoner-tier. No combat value.*

### Ohren Kade
Hillfolk human, 31. Volunteered out of a farming family on Faelyn's southern
edge. Being human among elves, he is acutely aware that he is the only person in
the squadron who will die of old age before this generation is finished
arguing about it. Eager, loud, works twice as hard as he needs to.
*Fighter 1, melee. Brave and mediocre — a dangerous combination.*

### Wick Pallowen
Woodland Gnome, 54. Scout and trapper. Small enough to go where nobody else
can, cheerfully unbothered by risks that should bother him. The squadron's other
real stealth asset alongside Alinar, and the two of them will naturally pair.
*Rogue 2. Excellent scout, fragile.*

### Tam Ellisan
Wood Elf, 44. Barely into adulthood and the youngest in the squadron by decades.
Enlisted because his friends did. Openly out of his depth and trying hard to
conceal it. **Tam is deliberately positioned as the first meaningful loss the
squadron can take** — he should be likable and he should not be protected.
*Fighter 1. Green.*

---

## Company Command

### Captain Ilyen Vaeroth
Wood Elf, 190. Commands the company the squadron belongs to. Competent
administrator, cautious to a fault, promoted in peacetime for peacetime
virtues. He is not a villain and not a fool — he is simply the wrong shape of
officer for the war that is starting, and the early years should show that
costing lives.
*Fighter 4.*

## DM Notes

- Named NPCs advance in capability across 387–392 AW alongside the PCs, but
  more slowly. They are not a second party.
- **None of these NPCs carry plot-critical knowledge.** Per
  `../CAMPAIGN_INSTRUCTIONS.md` §4, they have opinions, not secrets.
- They are mortal. Five years of war should visibly thin this roster, and the
  named ones dying should cost something.
- Unnamed squadron members: name them on the spot when they matter, and record
  the name here afterward so it persists.
