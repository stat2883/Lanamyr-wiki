# Mya Li Liaphyra

**The single most important NPC in the campaign.** Bible-confirmed character.
Do not alter her confirmed history. See `../reference/timeline-387-392.md`.

---

## Identity

- Wood Elf, female. Born **347 AW**. **Age 40 in 387 AW.**
- Daughter of **King Aer'Raenal Liaphyra** of Faelyn. Elder sibling of
  **Phelloden**, 30 in 387 AW.
- **Green eyes** — explicitly uncommon among Wood Elves, and the one thing that
  makes her physically recognizable. Otherwise typical Wood Elf appearance.
- She **stepped aside from the throne deliberately.** Phelloden is heir by her
  choice, not by birth order.

---

## Status in 387 AW

She is a **brand-new recruit**, exactly like the PCs. No rank. No command. No
reputation. She has **insisted on following the same path as any other
recruit**, and she means it.

This matters enormously for the early campaign. The twins do not meet a
commander. They meet another nervous volunteer in the same intake who happens
to be the king's daughter and who visibly hates being treated as such. Sergeant
Thale gives her no special handling; most others cannot help themselves, and
their failure to manage it irritates her.

Her standing across 387–392 AW is **earned, not granted.** By 392 she has men
under her command and volunteers to lead the offensive into The Deep.

---

## Agenda — How To Play Her

Per `../CAMPAIGN_INSTRUCTIONS.md` §4, Mya is the **sole exception** to the
companion rules. She does not defer to the PCs and will not follow them because
they are the player characters.

**What actually drives her:**
- **Compassion, and a refusal to stand by.** This is the root of everything. It
  is why she enlisted and why she will eventually volunteer to die.
- **Protecting Faelyn and its people** — the specific ones, not the abstraction.
- **Being taken seriously on her own merit**, not as royalty.

**To move her, the party must establish that their proposal serves those
things.** Arguments from authority, rank, or convenience will not work.
Arguments that protect people will. She is persuadable — she is not stubborn —
but she must be persuaded, and she will remember who argued what.

She forms her own judgments about Sharii and Alinar over five years. Those
judgments should be tracked in `campaign-status.md` and should shift based on
play.

---

## Kharis Ailwin

They become serious in their **second year** — 388/389 AW — and keep it
**quiet**, because their roles demanded professionalism. **Only their closest
friends knew.**

This is a live opportunity: the twins are in the same squadron from day one. If
they earn that closeness, they are among the few who know. If they don't, they
watch two colleagues be conspicuously professional around each other for four
years and may draw their own conclusions.

The payoff is brutal. Kharis dies in 392 AW and, because the relationship was
never public, **Mya cannot grieve him openly.** Whether anyone in the squadron
is in a position to recognize what that costs her depends entirely on what the
players built in the preceding years.

## Approximate Mechanics

Stats are a DM tool, not canon. She is a **Fighter** with the **Archery**
fighting style — bow-focused, consistent with eventually wielding the Bow of
the Dales.

| Year | Approx. Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 387 AW | 1 | Raw recruit. Promising, untested. |
| 388–391 AW | 2–6 | Shows great promise from the start. Grows into leadership. |
| 392 AW | 7–9 | Commands. Survives Diquyk's push. Kills Shedyak. |

Keep her **roughly comparable to the PCs** in the early years. She should not
outshine them mechanically — her significance is narrative, and making her a
superior combatant would undercut both her arc and theirs.

---

## Hard Limits — What She Cannot Know

- The **Abaculus** exists or directed the war.
- **Heaven's Oak**, its chamber, or the **Guardians of the Tree**.
- What **Adomorn** truly is. She knows him only as a powerful legendary wizard
  and is entirely unaware of his Drasu nature or immortality.
- What a **Magni** truly is. In 392 AW Adomorn tells her Diquyk is a Magni and
  gives her a **partial truth** — withholding what that means and how he knows.

She also does not know, in 387 AW, that any of this is coming.
