  Marauders who volunteered in 392 AW and died in the final battle in The Deep.
- **Source:** Campaign premise, agreed at setup.
- **Bible status:** `Lanamyr_08_Open_Questions.txt` currently lists "Individual
  member names and backgrounds: undeveloped" under THE MARAUDERS.
  `Lanamyr_07_Stories_Outlines.txt` lists "Who are the ten Marauders?" as a key
  unresolved question.
- **Files affected if promoted:** `Lanamyr_08_Open_Questions.txt`,
  `Lanamyr_07_Stories_Outlines.txt`, and likely `Lanamyr_06_Characters_Items.txt`
  (new character entries) and `Lanamyr_05_Kingdoms_History.txt` (THE MARAUDERS
  organization entry).
- **Status:** NOT YET EARNED. Promote only if the campaign actually reaches and
  plays the 392 AW volunteer call **and** the twins die there, matching the
  confirmed account.
- **User ruling:** if Sharii and Alinar survive the final battle, the outcome is
  **NON-CANON** and is not promoted. The bible's account — all ten Marauders
  dead — stands unchanged. The campaign becomes its own branch.

### Faelyn militia structure — campaign-invented, NOT for promotion
- **Established:** Squadron (~20, led by a sergeant) → Company (several
  squadrons, led by a captain) → Faelyn's tacticians → King Aer'Raenal.
  Named NPCs: Sergeant Serwyn Thale, Neva Corrin, Bellis Ordow, Ohren Kade,
  Wick Pallowen, Tam Ellisan, Captain Ilyen Vaeroth.
- **Source:** Built for play, session 0.
- **Bible status:** The bible confirms Faelyn has a militia Mya joins in 387 AW
  and that she later commands men, but gives no organizational detail.
- **Recommendation: DO NOT PROMOTE.** This is table scaffolding, not lore. It
  does not contradict anything confirmed, but the bible has no need of it.
  Listed here only so the campaign's inventions are transparently logged.

### Draconic as the dragon language — placeholder only
- **Established:** Sharii's Favored Enemy (dragons) grants him a language. The
  bible confirms dragons have their own language but **never names it**.
  Using 5e's "Draconic" as a table placeholder.
- **Note:** Draak-descended languages are explicitly stated to stand outside the
  Drasu language family, so naming one is a real lore decision rather than a
  table convention.
- **Recommendation:** Flag to the user in a bible session if a name is ever
  wanted. Do not promote "Draconic" — it is an out-of-setting name.

### Resurrection — campaign ruling, NOT for promotion
- **Established:** Resurrection exists in Lanamyr but is exceedingly rare,
  costly, and practiced by uncommon individuals. Never routine.
- **Source:** User ruling, session 0.
- **Bible status:** The bible confirms clerics serve the twenty Irridae and that
  divine magic functions, but is silent on raising the dead.
- **Does not contradict** anything confirmed. Necessary so that Kharis Ailwin's
  death and the Marauders' sacrifice retain their weight.
- **Recommendation:** Do not promote unless the user wants the bible to take a
  position on resurrection generally. Flag it in a bible session if so.

### Statistics for Crimson, Sear, and the Bow of the Dales
- **Established:** Full 5e mechanics — see `magic-items.md`.
- **Bible status:** The bible confirms the blades exist, that Aer'Raenal gifted
  them to Mya at the 392 AW council of war, and their glow colors (Crimson soft
  red, Sear soft white-yellow). It confirms the Bow's origin, its purple-pink
  bolts, and that it needs no conventional ammunition. **No statistics of any
  kind are given.**
- **Design constraint honored:** the blades are explicitly NOT spellcasting,
  because Diquyk is banished when Mya and Adomorn switch to physical combat and
  his fuel is cut off. Spell-based blades would break the confirmed ending.
- **Recommendation: DO NOT PROMOTE.** Game statistics are table mechanics, not
  lore. The bible has no need of them.

## Contradictions Flagged (bible-side, not campaign-side)

### Wood Elf childhood boundary vs. Mya's enlistment age
- `Lanamyr_02_Overview_Cosmology.txt` gives Wood Elves childhood 0–42, young
  adulthood 42–62.
- The Timeline in `Lanamyr_05_Kingdoms_History.txt` has Mya joining the militia
  in 387 AW at **age 40** — below the childhood boundary. Her character entry in
  `Lanamyr_06_Characters_Items.txt` repeats age 40. Kharis Ailwin, born 347 AW,
  has the same issue.
- This is an arithmetic inconsistency in confirmed lore. Raised per root
  `SESSION_INSTRUCTIONS.md` §9 ("Flag contradictions and arithmetic errors when
  noticed, rather than papering over them").
- **Resolution: PENDING USER DECISION**, but no longer blocking the campaign.
  Sharii and Alinar are both 45 (born 342 AW), cleanly inside young adulthood,
  and do not inherit the problem. This remains a bible-side inconsistency
  affecting Mya Li and Kharis Ailwin only.
- Possible fixes if ever addressed: lower the Wood Elf childhood boundary, or
  treat the life-stage percentages as soft guidelines rather than hard gates.
  Either is a lore decision with cross-file consequences and belongs to a bible
  session, not a campaign session.
