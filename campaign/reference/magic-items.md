# Named Weapons — Mechanics

Bible-confirmed items. **Appearance and provenance are canon; all statistics
below are campaign-invented** and logged in `lore-deltas.md`.

---

## CRIMSON and SEAR

**Confirmed lore:** Twin blades gifted to Mya Li by her father, King
Aer'Raenal. Crimson glows soft red. Sear glows soft white-yellow.

**Confirmed timing:** Given at the **392 AW council of war** — the same meeting
where the offensive into The Deep is decided and Mya volunteers to lead it.
Per `Lanamyr_07_Stories_Outlines.txt`: *he armed her himself.* Aer'Raenal built
the chain that produced the offensive and put his daughter at the head of it.

**The party will most likely be present for this.** She does not carry them
during 387–391 AW.

---

### ⚠ Critical Design Constraint

Diquyk is banished **when Mya and Adomorn switch to physical combat**, cutting
off his fuel supply. He feeds on intercepted spells.

**Therefore Crimson and Sear must not function as spellcasting.** Their
properties are intrinsic to the blades — no spell slots, no casting, nothing
Diquyk can intercept, steal, or convert to blue flame. This is load-bearing: if
the blades cast spells, the bible's ending stops making sense.

Describe their effects as physical and elemental, never as magic being *worked*.

---

### CRIMSON
*Paired blade · finesse, light · soft red glow, bright enough to read by*

- **+2** to attack and damage rolls
- Deals an extra **1d6 fire damage** on a hit
- **The Unyielding Edge.** Once per turn, when Mya hits a creature that has
  damaged an ally within 30 feet since the end of her last turn, that attack
  deals an extra **2d6 fire damage**, and the ally regains **1d6 hit points**.

*Crimson is the blade of refusing to stand by. It rewards her for fighting on
someone else's behalf, which is the whole of who she is.*

---

### SEAR
*Paired blade · finesse, light · soft white-yellow glow*

- **+2** to attack and damage rolls
- Deals an extra **1d6 radiant damage** on a hit
- Sheds **bright light in a 20-foot radius**, dim light 20 feet beyond. Cannot
  be suppressed. She is always visible when holding it drawn.
- **Unshadowed.** Advantage on attack rolls against creatures that are
  invisible, hidden, or benefiting from magical darkness. Ignores resistance to
  radiant damage.
- Scores a **critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20**.

*Sear is the answer to an enemy who fights from The Deep and the dark. It also
makes concealment impossible — she cannot sneak while holding it, and she will
not put it away.*

---

### PAIRED — while wielding both

- **+1 AC**
- Cannot be disarmed, and she cannot be forced to drop them while conscious
- She may draw or sheathe both as a single free action

**Total profile when paired:** two attacks at **+2**, each adding **1d6** of
elemental damage, +1 AC, expanded crit range on one blade, and a per-turn burst
tied to protecting someone. Formidable, as requested — but front-loaded toward
defending others rather than raw damage, which keeps her from simply out-killing
the party.

## BOW OF THE DALES

**Confirmed lore:** Created by **Diquyk for Shedyak**. Acquired by Mya after she
kills Shedyak in the final battle of the Elf War. Draws on ambient energy and
fires **purple-pink bolts** — no conventional ammunition. Anyone who recognizes
the purple-pink signature knows immediately who made it.

*Acquired in the campaign's final battle. Stats included for completeness.*

- **+3** to attack and damage rolls
- Requires no ammunition; conjures its own bolts
- Deals **1d8 + 3 force damage**
- **Ambient Draw.** Once per turn, Mya may spend extra effort on a shot, dealing
  an additional **2d8 force damage**.

**⚠ Diquyk can sense it.** The bow draws on ambient energy, and sensing that is
his defining ability. Post-war, this is why
`Lanamyr_08_Open_Questions.txt` asks whether he knows she carries it. Within the
campaign it matters for the closing moments only — but it should be described
so the players feel the weight of her carrying his work out of that battle.
