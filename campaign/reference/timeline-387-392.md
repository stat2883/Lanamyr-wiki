# The Elf War, 387–392 AW — DM Reference

Everything here is **confirmed lore** drawn from `bible/`. It happens. See
`CAMPAIGN_INSTRUCTIONS.md` §3.

---

## Before the war (383–387 AW) — offscreen, party unaware

- **383 AW** — Diquyk, drawn by curiosity to the Burnt Plains in Kimora,
  is corrupted on contact with the Abaculus.
- **383–385 AW** — Diquyk recruits the **Stone Giants** of the Tempest Peaks.
  Demonstrates overwhelming power as a show of force; offers plunder and the
  riches of Faelyn. They respect strength and are persuaded.
- **385–387 AW** — Diquyk travels to **The Deep** and recruits the **Dark
  Elves** — chosen for obscurity, deniability, no outside alliances, and no
  existing relationship with Faelyn. Equips them with magical and enchanted
  weapons. Places Jaunt Gates near both locations.

**Why Faelyn?** The Abaculus learned of the **Heaven's Oak chamber** — Adomorn's
permanent containment solution — through Greyhawk's memories at his corruption
in 2136, 2nd Epoch. Breaking the **Guardians of the Tree** and compromising the
chamber is a strategic priority before Diquyk pursues the larger mission. No one
in Faelyn knows this. The Guardians are a hereditary secret order answering only
to Adomorn, outside normal Wood Elf society.

---

## 387 AW — The war begins

- Two-front assault opens. Mixed units — platoons of Dark Elves with a handful
  of Giants — probe from the **north through the Tempest Peaks** and from the
  **east out of The Deep**.
- Stone Giant attacks strike Faelyn's outskirts.
- **Mya Li joins the militia at age 40.** The attacks are the final tipping
  point after a long accumulation of restlessness. She steps aside from the
  throne deliberately; Phelloden becomes heir by her choice. She insists on
  following the same path as any other recruit.
- **Kharis Ailwin joins at essentially the same time**, age 40. He survived a
  Stone Giant attack on a small Wood Elf village and fled to Caldalus to deliver
  word. **His family was killed in that attack** — his primary motivation.
- **The party joins here.**

---

## 387–391 AW — The probing years

Four years of two-front pressure. This is where the campaign lives and where
player agency is real.

- Diquyk directs operations **via Jaunt Gates** from between the two kingdoms.
  He is **not on the front lines** and is not personally seen.
- Mya builds as a soldier and leader. Both she and Kharis show great promise
  from the start.
- Mya and Kharis become serious in their second year — **kept quiet**, as their
  roles demanded professionalism. Only their closest friends knew.
- The war is real but manageable at this stage. Neither side is decisive.

---

## 392 AW — Everything, compressed into one year

**1. The first major push.** Diquyk escalates and appears on the field for the
first time. Dark Elves and Giants under his command carry magical and enchanted
weapons. He kills a multitude of soldiers before the Wood Elves retreat.

- This is **Mya's most crushing defeat** and her great learning experience. She
  underestimated the scale of the threat.
- **Kharis Ailwin is killed here.** He is not a Marauder.
- Confirmed image: before the engagement, Mya and her forces advancing see a
  **large blue flame in the distance.**

**2. The aftermath.** Mya survives and reports back. Because the relationship
was never publicly acknowledged, **she cannot grieve Kharis openly.**

**3. Adomorn.** Aer'Raenal urges Mya to seek him out. She knows him only as a
powerful legendary wizard and is **entirely unaware** of his Drasu nature or
immortality. Based on her account, Adomorn suspects Diquyk is a **Magni** — but
does not anticipate how far his powers have developed.

**4. The council of war.** Aer'Raenal, Adomorn, Mya, and Faelyn's tacticians.
Decision: abandon the defensive posture, strike into Dark Elf territory. Adomorn
gives a **partial truth** — names Diquyk a Magni, withholds what that means and
how he knows.

**5. The Marauders form.** Mya volunteers to lead. She does **not** hand-pick
the unit — she tells her peers plainly that the mission is dangerous and will
very likely end in death, that she believes it is Faelyn's best chance, and that
anyone who stays behind carries no blame and no ill will.

**Ten come forward.** All volunteers. All understood.

*(Sharii and Alinar are two of the ten — see CAMPAIGN_INSTRUCTIONS.md §3.)*

**6. The final battle, inside The Deep.**
- 8 Marauders with **Mya** as a deliberate decoy force, entering conventionally.
- 2 Marauders with **Adomorn** through a Jaunt Gate deep in Dark Elf territory,
  put to sleep for transit, to flank from behind.
- **Diquyk senses the gate transit** — his ability to detect others drawing on
  ambient energy extends to gate use. The ambush is foiled before it begins.
- Engaged on two fronts anyway, Diquyk **intercepts Adomorn's spells and
  redirects them as blue flame**, killing multiple Marauders.
- **Mya defeats and kills Shedyak**, acquiring the **Bow of the Dales.**
- When Mya and Adomorn switch to **physical combat**, Diquyk's fuel supply is
  cut and he is finally **banished** — to a parallel plane where time runs
  slower. He is 40 at banishment.
- **All ten Marauders are dead.** Only Mya and Adomorn survive.

**7. The coda.** *TENTATIVE in bible:* Adomorn, badly wounded, dies shortly
after banishing Diquyk and later rematerializes at Atral. Mya, unaware of his
nature, believes him dead. She walked in with ten people and walked out alone.

## What the party can never know

- The Abaculus exists, or that it directed the entire war.
- Heaven's Oak, its chamber, or the Guardians of the Tree.
- What Adomorn truly is.
- What a Magni truly is (the Drasu/Draak inheritance mechanism is not generally
  known — Magni are understood as a rare, unexplained fluke of birth).
- That Diquyk will return in 427 AW.
