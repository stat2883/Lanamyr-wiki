# Session 001 — Ashwell

**In-world date:** 387 AW, 3rd Epoch. Autumn. Recruitment through the night
following the squadron's first engagement.
**Party level:** 2 → **3** (milestone, at the closing long rest).
**Location:** Caldalus (opening) → forest track north toward the Tempest
foothills → Ashwell (closing).

---

## Summary

First session played. Recruitment into Faelyn's militia, muster into a
20-person squadron alongside Mya Li and Kharis Ailwin, several weeks of drill
compressed via montage, then the squadron's first patrol — which turned into
its first real fight within two days of setting out.

---

## Scenes Played

### The recruitment line, Caldalus
Sharii and Alinar enlisted together, describing themselves as foragers and
hunters ("angry wildlife... as large as a bear"). Sergeant Serwyn Thale took
the two seriously — self-sufficiency in the field mattered more to him than
combat boasts — and noted the mismatch of scale mail on a bow-arm without
further comment. Both were assigned to his squadron.

In the same line: Kharis Ailwin (quiet, alone, walked to Caldalus, "my
village doesn't need [a woodcutter] anymore") and a green-eyed woman who gave
only "Mya" — Thale asked neither for anything more.

### First evening, the barracks
- Sharii held back to observe the squadron, cataloguing rough strengths:
  Neva Corrin as the best shot outright; Neva vs. Wick Pallowen split between
  "finds what's moving" and "finds what's waiting" for tracking; Wick and
  Alinar tied for quietest on foot, Kharis quietest in speech; Ohren Kade
  flagged early as "hits hard, defends worst."
- Alinar sat with Wick Pallowen; the two traded barbed small talk that became
  the seed of a real partnership later in the montage.
- Sharii introduced himself to Mya. She gave no family name. Conversation
  turned partway to Sharii's reasons for following Alinar — he told her,
  truthfully but incompletely, that following was still his own choice, not
  passive habit. She noticed the extra weight in that answer and filed it
  without pressing. She also asked him to go gently on Kharis's history if it
  ever comes up.
- Alinar told Wick plainly why he enlisted: refugees arriving from the north,
  a wish to be useful to them rather than just alarmed on their behalf. Wick
  approved more than he let on.
- **Neva Corrin** needled Sharii hard about the scale mail. He leaned into it
  rather than deflecting — "it's bear bait, and not everyone has the courage
  to be the bait" — and the name **"bear bait"** stuck immediately and will
  likely follow him for a while. Running gag established; played for a
  laugh at least three separate times before the session ended.

### Montage — weeks of drill
Fast-forwarded per player request. Established during the montage:
- Alinar trained with pure economy, impressing Thale by inference rather than
  display, and became genuinely close with Wick through repeated stealth
  pairing.
- Sharii's read on the squadron (above) solidified into something he could
  act on tactically.
- Autumn set in.

### The muster to patrol
Word arrived of Stone Giants moving down from the Tempest foothills, closer
than in living memory. Thale pulled the squadron out on a 4-day patrol within
the hour — the party's first taste of "this is not a drill."

### Tracking, day 2
Wick found the first giant print (three feet across, 1–2 days old, heading
south-southeast — toward settled land, not away from it). Neva confirmed the
read and gave direction/age. **Alinar searched further and found a second,
separate print** — a smaller/lighter stride, crossing the first track roughly
half a day later, same heading. This changed Thale's read from "one giant,
maybe strays this way" to "two giants, independently, possibly being pushed
out of the foothills by something else" — a thread left open, not resolved.

Players asked (OOC) about the nearest settlement in the tracks' path; DM
established **Ashwell**, a small unwalled farming settlement half a day
south-southeast — invented for this session, not bible canon. Given the
timing, the party chose to push the last two hours of daylight to close
distance rather than make camp and report back.

### The Ashwell engagement (full combat)
Smoke was visible before arrival — a burning barn and grain store. Two Stone
Giants were already inside the settlement perimeter. Thale split the
squadron: himself, Neva, Kharis, and Mya toward evacuating villagers; the
rest toward drawing and engaging the giants.

**Plan:** Sharii sprinted in as bait to pull Giant A away from the village;
Alinar and Wick flanked and set an ambush line; ranged squadron members
engaged from cover once in range.

**Combat beats:**
- Giant A closed hard on Sharii and Ohren (who followed Sharii in). Sharii
  was dropped to 0 HP by a devastating hit while trying to lead the giant
  into the ambush — went down, then failed one death save, then rolled a
  natural 20 (full recovery to 1 HP) right as Giant A finally went down to
  combined fire from Wick (sneak attack), Alinar (blades), and Ohren.
- Meanwhile Giant B turned on the fleeing villagers; at Alinar's shouted
  request, **Mya and Neva broke off the evacuation to draw it away** — Mya's
  first real combat initiative on record, entirely her own call. Kharis
  covered the villagers' retreat.
- Neva was dropped to 0 HP by Giant B. **Mya stood over her unconscious body
  alone against the giant**, took a heavy hit, and held — closing the gap on
  what she eventually becomes.
- Alinar and Wick closed and finished Giant B in melee/ranged combination
  while Sharii, still critically low, kept firing from range rather than
  closing further.
- **Both giants killed. No squadron deaths.** Neva was stabilized by Sharii's
  Cure Wounds the moment the fight ended.

**Aftermath:** Villagers safe, barn and grain store lost, no other buildings
burned. Sharii healed Neva (full consciousness, immediately talking again).
Alinar and Kharis worked triage on villagers and Sharii's own wound. Thale
credited the twins specifically — the second print, and whatever kept the
fight from being worse — and gave the squadron leave to rest. The village
headman, **Aldric**, offered floor space for the night.

Sharii closed the evening with one more "bear bait" callback ("now I can add
2 giants to the two bears"), left deliberately ambiguous as to whether the
bears were ever real.

## Mechanics and Rulings Established This Session

- **Free weapon switching** adopted as a house rule: no action cost to swap
  equipped weapons.
- **NPC roll visibility** changed: DM shows all player-facing rolls in full;
  monster/NPC rolls are no longer shown, with DM discretion on pacing. Player
  requested this; DM flagged explicitly that this is a scoped exception and
  does **not** override the "play it straight" lethality ruling or the
  no-fudging-for-the-party principle — see `../house-rules.md` for the full
  caveat as written into the permanent record.
- **Milestone level-up to 3**, applied at the long rest closing the session
  (first real combat, both PCs critically low, no deaths — judged earned).
  - Both PCs: +1 spell slot (3 total), +1 spell known.
    - Sharii: **Longstrider** added.
    - Alinar: **Ensnaring Strike** added.
  - Both PCs gained **Primeval Awareness** (base Ranger class feature).
  - **Archetypes chosen:** Sharii → **Beast Master**. Alinar → **Hunter**,
    taking **Colossus Slayer** as the 3rd-level feature.
  - Sharii's Beast Master companion: **Knight**, a black wolf, established
    as found orphaned at Ashwell's treeline in the fighting's aftermath (den
    or pack likely scattered by the giants) — did not run from Sharii, never
    left. Full stat block in `../characters/Sharii.md` and
    `../npcs/combat-blocks.md`.
