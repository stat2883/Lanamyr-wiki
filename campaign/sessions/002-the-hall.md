# Session 002 — The Hall Under the Tempest Peaks

**In-world date:** 387 AW, 3rd Epoch. Autumn. Morning after the Ashwell
engagement, through four days' march to Caldalus, eight days in the capital,
closing on the eve of departure with the survey party.
**Party level:** 3 (no level-up this session).
**Location:** Ashwell → the north draw → the hall → Ashwell → Caldalus.

---

## Summary

The squadron finished its patrol by going up the north draw after two missing
villagers. They found the boy alive, and they found a worked stone corridor and
a pillared hall beneath the Tempest Peaks that no one in Faelyn knows exists —
currently occupied by a silent, disciplined party installing equipment in it.
Both PCs went through a collapse into the hall itself, recovered the missing
grandmother, and got out ahead of whatever was coming through behind them.

The session's second half was the report at Caldalus, where Sharii voluntarily
disclosed *Unbidden* to Captain Vaeroth, and eight days of downtime.

**Nobody died. Nobody was even seriously hurt.** This was a session about
discovery and consequence, not combat — there was no combat at all.

## Scenes Played

### Morning at Ashwell
- **Alinar** counted the village: 43 names, 39 bodies. Two missing — **Hestra
  Fenn** (~75) and her grandson **Coll** (9), up the north draw with goats
  since before the giants came through.
- Food survey: grain store and seed corn both lost. Root cellars, smokehouse
  (~3 weeks pork), orchard, and livestock intact. Verdict: Ashwell eats to
  midwinter and then starves unless outside food arrives or the herd is
  slaughtered.
- **Sharii** cast *Speak with Animals* and interviewed Knight. Established:
  Knight's mother moved the den four or five nights before the giants came,
  frightened, away from **"quiet ones"** — two-legged, cold, *underneath-smell*,
  who went through the ridge without hunting or speaking. Knight has a separate
  and emphatic smell for giants; these were not giants.
- Sharii argued to Thale, in front of the squadron, that an animal lacks the
  capacity to lie. Thale accepted the wolf as a witness on that basis.
- **Sharii asked Mya for her opinion first, by name, in front of everyone.**
  Deliberate — to establish her input was valued. She noticed exactly what he
  had done and filed it. Her recommendation (go up fast, get the pair, be back
  before dark, don't follow anything) was substantially what Thale adopted.
- **Neva Corrin refused to be left behind** ("leave me and I'm done"). Thale
  relented and left **Tam Ellisan** in the village instead.

### The north draw
- Party decided on the twins' call: Caldalus is the answer to the food problem,
  but it is a four-month problem, not a four-hour one. Patrol first.
- Marching order set by the players: Sharii, Knight, Alinar, Mya in the lead
  element. **Sharii removed his scale mail and left it in the village** to stop
  being the loud one — AC 15 → 11 for the duration.
- Wick's advice followed: track the goats, work backward to the people.
- Found: the meadow shelf, ~12 goats (of ~20), one goat killed cleanly and
  bled with a straight edge, one haunch taken. A **vertical seam in the ridge**
  with decades-old chisel work and cut steps going down. Knight would not
  approach it.
- **Unbidden fired (1 of 2 this session).** Sharii sensed something vast, cold
  and *structured* far below — not in the seam, past it and deep. Plus four or
  five smaller sharp points, stationary, "like objects being carried and set
  down." He lost the power of speech briefly. **Mya watched it happen.**
- **Coll Fenn** found wedged in the boulders, where he had been ~30 hours
  because his grandmother told him to stay. Came out for **Kharis**, not Mya.
  Reported six or more "quiet men" who came *out of* the crack, wore cloth over
  their eyes in daylight, never spoke, and walked his grandmother inside.

### The decision about the boy
Alinar opened the floor to the whole squadron. Wick, Neva, Kharis and Mya all
argued it; Kharis argued (visibly about his own village) that the boy should
not be marched away while his grandmother was still inside, Mya argued the
opposite and won on practicality. Boy sent down with **Wick Pallowen** and a
militiaman named **Dalla Sarn**.

Thale laid out three options. **Sharii: "Option one is off the table."**
Option two chosen — a small party in, short, no exploring.

### Into the hall
**Entry party:** Sharii, Alinar, Mya, Kharis, Thale, Knight.

- The stair comes down into a **T-junction**, not an endpoint. Corridor runs
  left and right. The seam and stair are a later back-door cut into a road that
  already existed. Inbound prints went right; the prints that came back out and
  up the stair came from the **left**.
- **Right-hand corridor:** 350 ft, dressed and squared, ceiling beam, and
  **continuous inscription at chest height down both walls** in a script that
  is not Aiwiya, Common or Ukuri. Ends in an old collapse with a crawl pulled
  through it and the stones stacked tidily to one side.
- Found at the base: a **veil** of fine dark weave, loose enough to see light
  through. And, through the fill, **tapping** — three strikes, pause, repeating.
- **The crawl** proved ~12 ft with two bends. Alinar found Hestra's lost shoe
  inside it. **Knight refused to enter and stayed with Thale.** Thale and Kharis
  could not fit. Mya stripped her chain shirt and went through in gambeson
  (AC 11).
- **The hall:** pillared, floor and pillars inscribed, no visible walls from the
  middle, air warm and dry and dead still. Not a mine. Investigation and raw
  INT ruled out temple and tomb — no altar, no focal point, no burials, no soot.
  Sharii's read: a machine, a library, or a road.
- **Hestra Fenn** recovered: alone, against a pillar, water and uneaten goat set
  beside her, **broken ankle**, blind in the dark and navigating by sound for
  30 hours. The pillar above her head bore **three fresh parallel scratches** —
  a mark, not her work.
- Her account: six or more, taller than a Wood Elf, thin, **cold hands**, veils
  over the eyes in daylight, **no light of any kind underground**, and
  **completely silent** — no speech, no signals, no whistles. Polite. One waited
  while her ankle gave out. They watered her and left her. *"You don't water a
  thing you've finished with."*
- **Unbidden fired (2 of 2).** One of the small points **opened** — a sense of
  something switching on — accompanied by *attention* with a direction to it.
  Dropped Sharii to one knee. **Mya's response was to ask "Which direction" and
  nothing else.**
- **Alinar then saw the lights.** Pale blue-white, no flicker, not flame. First
  at ~100 ft beside a **stone cube ~3 ft square placed on the floor**, with
  pale metal on its upper face and **a freshly cut circle of inscription around
  it in the same script as the walls.** Second at ~200 ft, third at ~300+.
- **The second light went out and came back on nearer — twice — without
  crossing the ground between.** It did not travel. It arrived.
- **Extraction** in the order the players set: Hestra (hauled by Kharis),
  Sharii, Mya, Alinar last. Alinar jammed at the first bend and got free. As the
  last man he got a clear look at the cube and the cut circle.
- As the party formed up to run, a single sound came from the far end of the
  crawl: **a stone set down neatly on stone.** Nothing emerged. The party ran.
- **350 ft of corridor and the stair, no pursuit, no sound at all.** Out onto
  the shelf into Neva's drawn bow.

### Out and back
- Sharii spent a slot on *Cure Wounds* for Hestra — not enough to mend a break,
  enough to turn a stretcher problem into a shoulder problem.
- Thale's call: off the shelf, through Ashwell, onto the road in the dark.
  **The seam was left open — nothing to close it with.**
- Neva had the squadron drive the surviving goats down to the village.
- Coll and Hestra reunited. Aldric gave Alinar a jar of apple preserve.
- **Kharis volunteered**, unprompted, to come to Caldalus and be in the room
  when the food was argued for. First thing he has ever volunteered for in
  front of the twins.

### The report at Caldalus — Capt. Ilyen Vaeroth
This was the session's centrepiece.

- Vaeroth took the giants and the food easily. He went **careful** on the hall —
  not mocking, but testing. ("Do you know what is under the Tempest Peaks?
  Rock. That's four hundred years of survey.")
- **Mya spoke up** as a soldier for the first time in her life — cost/benefit:
  if wrong, a wasted scouting party; if right and ignored, a door in the border
  nobody looked at. Vaeroth asked pointedly whether it was her assessment or her
  father's. She answered that she hadn't spoken to her father in six weeks.
- **Sharii referred to her as "the princess."** This landed badly in the room —
  and instead of retreating, he held her eye and went further: disclosed
  *Unbidden* directly to the captain ("I've felt a presence less than a dozen
  times in my life... today was the first time I've felt it twice"), then named
  explicitly that he had not invoked her title for its weight, but to
  acknowledge **the risk to her identity that she took by speaking at all.**
  Persuasion 22.
- **Vaeroth's response to the disclosure:** did not dismiss it. Cited a
  grandmother with something similar. Declined to put it in writing — not from
  doubt, but because *"the first tactician who reads 'the scout had a feeling'
  stops reading."* It goes into the letter as *the forward element reported the
  installations active prior to visual confirmation.* He told Sharii plainly
  that this was both protection and use.
- **Alinar gave the plain account** — the full list, flat, no adjectives,
  untrained Charisma check of 19. This is what actually turned the captain.
- **Vaeroth's analysis**, which the party had not assembled themselves:
  - Raiders take the whole goat. Bleeding it is **rationing** — they intend to
    stay and are carrying their own supply.
  - They don't speak, which means either they can't, **or they have a way of
    speaking that cannot be heard.**
  - A prisoner left alive, watered, against a *marked* pillar is **a thing set
    aside to be collected**, not mercy and not carelessness.
  - They are installing equipment in a line, in a structure **they can read**.
  - *"They are not squatters. They are a working party. Somebody sent them,
    somebody is supplying them, and somebody expects a result."*
  - **The hall runs north** — downslope, under the Tempest Peaks, the direction
    everything has come from since midsummer.
- **Outcome:** survey party authorised, 20 plus engineers, within the week. A
  second letter over Vaeroth's own seal to Faelyn's tacticians stating that in
  his judgement the Stone Giant attacks are **not the whole of what is happening
  to Faelyn.** Ashwell's grain submitted as a requisition, not a request.

### Eight days in Caldalus
**Sharii — mornings with Knight.** Three *Speak with Animals* castings daily for
eight days (all three slots, every day — he was a man with no healing left by
noon for over a week). Outcome: no mechanical change to Knight's block, but an
established working vocabulary that functions **without** the spell —
flat palm (stop/quiet, solid), two fingers and a direction (go and wait,
reliable under ~40 ft), low whistle (recall). And Knight's own signal: a hard
silent shoulder-press meaning *something is wrong and I will not explain.*
Established limits: can't count past three, can't distinguish dangerous from
merely present, won't go where he can't smell Sharii, and is four months old and
chases cats. **Day seven: Knight's head came up and looked directly at Sharii
when Unbidden brushed him. The wolf can tell when it happens.**

**Sharii — afternoons, the armor.** Quartermaster refused a swap; issue is
issue. Solved socially instead: Bellis's leatherworker contact dropped the price
45 → 30 gp; Alinar gave his 10 gp; **Neva Corrin contributed 8 gp** and
immediately denied it; Thale closed the gap with a hazard disbursement for the
Ashwell engagement that he appears to have invented and backdated. Scale mail
returned to stores.

**Alinar — the requisition.** Eight days of paperwork, which turned out to be
the week's real education. The blockage was not malice: **Ashwell is not a
chartered settlement** — four families on crown ground, and crown stores release
against charter. Underneath that, Caldalus had absorbed ~400 new recruits in six
weeks and every sack in the city was already committed.
- **Won: seed corn**, released from a different store under a different office
  with a box for *crown ground under cultivation*. Found by accident on day
  five, and the single most important thing anyone did for Ashwell this year —
  **the village can plant in spring.**
- **Won: forty days of flour**, diverted from a militia column resupply (Thale
  signed something he probably shouldn't have).
- **Not won:** bread grain, winter stores. The midwinter-to-spring gap remains.
- **Got a name and a route:** clerk **Ivell Sarrow**, the one person in the
  granary office who tried, who said: *"Bring me a charter and I'll fill it in
  an hour. Nobody's going to charter four families. Get them attached to a
  bigger village's roll and they stop being invisible."* → **Dorn's Ford**, a
  day east of Ashwell, larger, with a mill.
- Kharis stood behind Alinar at that desk four days running. Closing line:
  *"You got them spring. That's the one that was going to kill them."*

## NPC Status Changes

- **Mya Li.** Spoke as a soldier in a captain's office for the first time, and
  held when challenged on whether it was her father's view. Scouted both giant
  trails alone before dawn at Ashwell. Stripped armour and went into the hall
  in a gambeson. Watched Sharii's Unbidden fire twice and **chose not to ask**
  — told him on the road that it was his, that she'd believe him when he told
  her, and asked only *"Can I use it?"* Her judgement of the twins has moved
  substantially this session, in their favour.
- **Neva Corrin.** Post-near-death. Refused to be left behind at Ashwell. Was
  put in command of nine militiamen on the shelf by Thale, obeyed without
  noticing it was happening. Contributed 8 gp toward Sharii's armour. Has not
  needled him once since the hall — then resumed with "still bear bait," which
  the squadron read correctly as her being all right.
- **Kharis Ailwin.** Visibly spent in the hall; got the boy out of the rocks
  when nobody else could. Volunteered unprompted to come to Caldalus for the
  food argument. Standing in the background of Alinar's requisition fight for
  four days. **This is the beginning of the twins being close to him**, which
  per `../npcs/mya-li.md` is what determines who knows about him and Mya later.
- **Sgt. Serwyn Thale.** Went into the hall himself rather than send people down
  it. Has started asking the twins what they think before deciding. Signed at
  least two things he had no authority to sign, both to the squadron's benefit.
- **Tam Ellisan.** Left behind at Ashwell instead of Neva. Still alive, still
  unprotected, still positioned.
- **Capt. Ilyen Vaeroth.** Materially upgraded. Played as the cautious
  peacetime officer right up to the point where the evidence turned him, then
  moved fast and wrote the letter he didn't have to write. **He is coming on the
  survey party personally.** Note for future sessions: the file says his caution
  should cost lives in the early years. That still stands — this session showed
  he can be moved by a well-made case, not that he is a good wartime officer.

## Unresolved Threads Exiting This Session

1. **The hall itself.** Builders unknown. Script unreadable — not Aiwiya,
   Common, or Ukuri. Purpose unknown. Extent unknown. Runs north and downslope
   under the Tempest Peaks.
2. **The quiet ones.** Tall, thin, cold-handed, veiled in daylight, no light
   underground, entirely silent. A disciplined, supplied working party. Neither
   PC has ever seen a Dark Elf and neither has anyone they know — but both of
   them speak Ukuri, and neither has ever thought to ask why they were taught
   it. *Not yet identified in the fiction. Do not have anyone name them.*
3. **The cubes.** Stone, ~3 ft, placed in a line, with freshly cut inscription
   circles and flameless lights. At least one was switched on while Sharii was
   present. One light relocated without crossing the ground between.
4. **The left-hand corridor at the T-junction.** Never explored. The party came
   out that way in the dust record; they went in to the right.
5. **The thing below.** Vast, cold, structured, far beneath everything, and
   **not** the thing that turned the lights on. Sharii only.
6. **Hestra's marked pillar.** Three fresh scratches. She was set aside to be
   collected, and she was taken out from under them.
7. **The stone set down on stone** at the far end of the crawl. Something had a
   hand on it. It did not follow.
8. **Ashwell's winter.** Spring is solved. Midwinter to spring is not.
9. **FLAGGED FOR SESSION 3 — carry this in:** *Alinar intends to talk to Aldric
   on arrival at Ashwell and explain that the fastest route to aid is to get
   Ashwell chartered onto **Dorn's Ford's** roll. Play this scene.*
10. Vaeroth's second letter to the tacticians — what comes back, and when.
11. Session 1's open thread (why two giants, staggered, same heading) is now
    partly answered: something came through the ridge ahead of them. Not fully
    resolved.
