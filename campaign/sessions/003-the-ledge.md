# Session 003 — The Ledge Under the Tempest Peaks

**In-world date:** 387 AW, 3rd Epoch. Autumn. Nine days, from first light at
Caldalus through the march to Ashwell, the sealed seam, the north face, two
nights of fire-setting, the descent, and the column's departure at dawn on
day nine.
**Party level:** 3 → **4** (milestone, applied at the long rest on the march
back to Ashwell).
**Location:** Caldalus → the road north → Ashwell → the meadow shelf → the
north face → the hall, the left-hand corridor, and the ledge.

---

## Summary

The survey column marched north and found the seam **sealed** — dressed stone,
keyed, laid from the inside, with the three-scratch mark cut fresh beside it.
The party scouted the north face, found the vent and the goat trail, and
watched two of the quiet ones pass each other on open ground without
acknowledgement. They opened the plug in two nights and a day using wedges,
freeze-thaw and fire-setting quenched by conjured water, went down the
left-hand corridor for the first time, and found a depot, the end of the
original construction, a raw tunnel still being cut, and a **void the size of
a city** with fifty-odd lit cubes on its floor.

They came back out without a fight, were followed by one of the quiet ones at
a walk, held the junction while the engineer took a rubbing, and **made first
contact** — which consisted of a stone being set on the floor, taken, and a
wax tablet being left in its place the following morning.

**Nobody died. There was no combat at any point in the session.**

## Scenes Played

### Caldalus, the gate
- **Ignis challenged at the gate by Capt. Vaeroth** over being on no roll. She
  gave her name plainly; Sharii answered for her ("obviously she's a scout")
  and was flatly corrected — **"Actually, I'm a druid and I'm great for
  healing, which you're going to need."**
- Vaeroth reassigned her to mid-column on the spot, ordered the squadron to
  know her face by nightfall, and told her to her face that she was now the
  most valuable body in the column and the one everything would run toward.
- Sharii reframed his correction as liberation ("I'm no longer the focal point
  of anything"). Neva Corrin: *"You're unsupervised."*

### Four days on the road
- **Alinar** briefed Ignis on Ashwell unprompted and laid out the Dorn's Ford
  charter problem, correctly identifying the real obstacle himself: *what does
  Dorn's Ford get?*
- **Sharii** spent a slot interviewing Knight about Ignis. Result deliberately
  unexplanatory: she does **not** smell like the underneath-thing (emphatic,
  a closed category); she smells like *the far side of a fire*; she *was still
  arriving*; and "she is one of the ones," of which there appear to be about
  six and Sharii is one. Sharii relayed this to Alinar, who concluded she is
  pack. **That is the brothers' interpretation, not a confirmation.**
- **Ignis** foraged herbs (8 / 21 / 18), ending with a working field kit — real
  materia for non-magical medicine. Dried the camp ground and laid perimeter
  fog each night with *Create or Destroy Water* (ruled: not concentration,
  so it persists and she can sleep). Thale: *"Keep doing that."*
- She told Thale to stay inside *Healing Word* range once fighting starts,
  offered *Pass Without Trace* for the approach, and flagged *Thorn Whip*.
  Thale converted all three into orders and used the 30-ft radius as his
  argument to the captain for a **small forward party** instead of twenty on
  the shelf.
- Asked why she volunteered so much: *"Having information keeps you well
  prepared and less people killed."* Thale: *"That's the first sensible thing
  anyone's said to me this week."*

### Ashwell
- **Alinar gave Aldric the cart manifest** including the seed corn. Aldric's
  reaction — *"We'd have eaten it... That's the one that was going to kill
  us"* — is the second time that sentence has been said to Alinar in a
  fortnight.
- **THE FLAGGED SCENE WAS PLAYED.** Alinar laid out the Dorn's Ford charter
  route. Aldric supplied, without realising it, the three levers: Ashwell
  families came *out of* Dorn's Ford two generations back (no quarrel — land);
  Ashwell has no mill and never will, so every harvest goes east and pays a
  toll that has risen twice; and **"we're the ones between them and the
  mountain."**
- Headman of Dorn's Ford named: **Merrick Dorn**, miller, "says no to a favour
  and yes to a bargain."
- **Ignis treated Hestra Fenn's ankle** (Medicine 17). Setting a few degrees
  off true — not needing re-breaking, but left alone it costs the old woman
  bad ground for life. Re-splinted at a corrected angle, willow bark and
  comfrey, needs redoing for three weeks.
- **She taught Coll Fenn to do it**, and he is an extraordinary student for an
  unsettling reason: he does not improvise, does not stop, and repeats
  instructions back verbatim. Hestra's warning: *"He does what he's told. All
  the way down... be careful what you tell him."*
- Ignis also got the Dorn's Ford history out of the families (Persuasion 18,
  Insight 14). The real obstacle is **shame, not grievance** — they're the ones
  who left, and asking to be put on the roll is admitting it didn't work. One
  woman: *"Merrick'll say yes. That's not what I'm worried about."*
- **Vaeroth told Aldric about the hall himself**, unprompted and privately.
  Aldric's reaction: *"Hestra knew. She's known a fortnight and said nothing to
  me."* Vaeroth: *"Then she's a better officer than most of mine."*
- The squadron camped outside the walls on Alinar's insistence and refused
  everything Ashwell offered.
- **Flour dispersed four ways** on Alinar and Wick's joint call rather than
  stacked in one building. Seed corn to the Fenn loft. Aldric: *"We've always
  put it in the barn."*

### The shelf — the seal
- **The goats were gone.** No bones, no blood, no kill sign.
- **The seam was closed**: 8–9 dressed blocks, keyed into the jamb, set back a
  hand's breadth, laid dry from the inside, 2–3 ft thick, with no spoil
  anywhere on the shelf. Joints 1–3 mm. **Three fresh parallel scratches cut
  into the rock beside it** — the same mark as Hestra's pillar.
- **Wick found the draught.** Warm air still leaving through the joints, which
  established that the corridor behind was open and that the system vents
  somewhere else.
- Maevis Tarn's three methods (plug-and-feather, undermining, fire-setting);
  her verdict *"it's not a seal, it's a door they don't want used."*
- Sharii proposed conjuring water behind the plug as a lever. **Ruled no** —
  can't target an unseen point, water doesn't push, weights don't work — but
  the direction was right and produced the actual plan.
- **Dovin Reyl** (surveyor) plotted the corridor onto the surface from
  Alinar's recall (Wanderer feature): far end of the known corridor is ~400 ft
  north and 120 ft down, beyond reach from above. His steer: look over the
  shoulder, down the north face, low, for green grass or absent frost.

### The north face
- First Wood Elf eyes on that ground in living memory. *Pass Without Trace* up;
  stealth 20–30 across the board.
- Alinar's Survival 14 (unfamiliar country, two dead-end gullies). Wick's
  Investigation **natural 1** on scree. **Neva Corrin found it** (Perception 17):
  a thirty-foot green patch with no frost on it, and a four-inch split in the
  rock breathing warm air. **Far too small to enter.**
- **The goats were driven out alive** — churned hoofprints bunched, with long
  narrow low-tread footprints walking the outside of the herd, heading down
  and north. They are not carrying supply. They are farming.
- **A figure sighted** at 400 yards, walking a known route downhill, carrying
  something wrapped. Entirely covered, veiled, no skin exposed.
- **Alinar's INT 19 on Dark Elf lore returned nothing** — and the shape of the
  nothing is the finding. No face, no colour, no height, nothing. And at the
  bottom of it: he speaks Ukuri fluently and **has never once wondered why.**
- **The party discussed capturing it and decided against**, on Mya's two
  arguments: they don't speak so there is no interrogation, and taking one
  costs the quiet they need to open the plug. Sharii and Alinar had both been
  willing to ignore Vaeroth's standing order; the decision was made on the
  merits, not the order.
- **Staying put bought two more findings:** a second one came *up* the same
  line, and **the two passed each other at six feet with no acknowledgement of
  any kind.** And Neva marked the gully, three-quarters of a mile down and
  round the shoulder, where the first one went in and did not come out.
- Vaeroth, on the report: *"Six soldiers had a clean shot at the first prisoner
  anyone has ever taken in this war, and they worked out what it would cost,
  and they came home and told me instead. **That is the standard.**"*

### Two nights and a day — opening the plug
- Maevis's revised plan, built from Sharii's water instinct: **oak wedges**
  driven into the two workable joints and kept wet (swelling, silent), plus
  **nightly flooding of every joint** so the frost does the splitting, then one
  day of **fire-setting** with an instant full-face quench. Five days of
  hammering reduced to two nights and a day, loud only at the end.
- Ignis flooded the face three to four times each night. The plug **ticked**
  audibly in the dark as water froze in the hairlines.
- **Ignis used Thaumaturgy to flare the bonfire violet and green**, purely for
  the forty people who had been hauling timber since dawn. No heat benefit;
  acknowledged as such. Ohren Kade sat down. Thale: *"Today we're loud on
  purpose. Tomorrow it would matter."*
- **Alinar and Ignis hunted** (Survival 17 with Druidcraft drawing game): two
  roe deer, eleven hares, birds, forage — about a day and a half of ration
  stretch and one hot meal for forty-two.
- **Alinar's Perception 19 in the wood:** all game sign more than a day or two
  old runs **south and downhill**, more than seasonal movement accounts for.
- **Ignis's Speak with Animals** confirmed it from the other side: **the ground
  itches.** A fox described a feeling in the pads and jaw, starting around when
  the leaves turned, worsening suddenly and recently, **worse higher up and much
  worse at night.** Nothing is being eaten. No predator, no fear-smell. The game
  is leaving because the mountain has become unpleasant to stand on.
- **A crow had been over the ridge**, following the goats. It described a cut
  in the hill with a flat place before it, constant traffic in and out, more of
  them than there are of the party's forty-two, and — aggrieved — **the goats
  are alive, penned, and fed.**
- Maevis got a bar into the lowest left joint on the morning of day seven and
  **moved a block half an inch** before the fire went in. After the quench,
  her working roll was 10: **one block out**, leaving a 2 ft × 18 in gap at
  knee height. Full plug openable by mid-morning.
- **Sharii's Perception 23 through the gap:** wet steps, marks in the water
  showing something had come up to the inside of the plug and gone back down
  more than once, and **a single river stone set upright in the middle of the
  bottom step, facing up the stair.**

### The conversations
- **Sharii asked Thale his honest read of Vaeroth.** Thale gave it: the best
  peacetime officer he has served under in eleven years, and **slow** — and the
  thirteen days that caution cost at the hall is a bill not yet presented.
  *"And the worst of it is, I think he knows."*
- Sharii then disclosed that the party had been prepared to ignore the
  captain's order on the north face. Thale's three-part answer: you didn't do
  it, so it's not a disciplinary matter; **tell me before, not after**; and
  *"you just told me instead of him — that's you picking a chain of command,"*
  with the cost being that it lands on Thale, not Sharii, and Thale taking it
  anyway.
- **Sharii dismissed Vaeroth as "a figurehead" and Thale came down on it hard** —
  a figurehead does not come up the mountain and sleep on the grass; the half
  plate and horse are *issue*; and *"don't tell me you're being careful. You're
  just choosing who's allowed to spend you."*
- **Sharii then asked Vaeroth to speak freely and did**, to his face, in front
  of Thale — including that he would not promise to obey an order he judged
  wrong in the moment. Vaeroth's answer:
  - he is right about the slowness, and the thirteen days are owned;
  - **a line is what an officer has instead of information**, which is why they
    exist for the dark;
  - charging the giant at Ashwell was his own body to spend — **breaking a line
    is different, because the cost lands on whoever has to come and get him.**
  - Terms: *tell somebody first* (warning, not permission), and report it
    afterward before he hears it elsewhere.
  - **And he formalised it: Sharii is "forward judgment."** If he calls a stop,
    the party stops and the argument happens on the surface. Explicitly framed
    as moving the cost, not removing it.
- **Vaeroth also noticed and let go** that Sharii's account of the near-capture
  was entirely in the first person, shielding the others, and declined to ask
  them.
- **Sharii convened the forward party privately** to share his view of the
  captain. Maevis refused to attend and left. Mya attended, backed him on
  substance, and objected to the venue in terms specific to her — *"six people
  out past the horse lines agreeing about the captain... the only fact that
  survives is that I was here."* Sharii then announced he would wait because
  his brother preferred to, and left abruptly.
- **Mya on shadows**, to Sharii after he said he found his brother's shadow
  comfortable: *"A shadow's comfortable because you don't have to stand in the
  weather... the day I stepped out of it, nobody actually stopped me. Turned
  out it was me. Whether yours is the same, you'd know. Or you wouldn't, which
  is sort of the point."*
- **Ignis to Sharii**, after the "speak freely" conversation: *"Sometimes you
  need to learn to shut your mouth... Don't do anything too stupid because I'm
  the one who has to save you."* He went flat and said *"You're right. I'll
  try."*
- **Ignis Wild Shaped into a wolf and played with Knight for an hour.** First
  Wild Shape anyone in the militia has seen. **Knight was not fooled for a
  second** — he crossed thirty feet in the dark the instant the shape changed,
  with no sniffing and no confusion, and did the full submissive-pup display
  to a full-grown adult. Two villagers went indoors and did not come out.

### Down the left-hand corridor
- Forward party: **Sharii, Alinar, Ignis, Mya, Wick, Neva, Maevis**, plus
  Knight. Thale held the stair head with fifteen; Vaeroth held the shelf.
  Three hours on the glass.
- The stone on the bottom step: **river stone**, smooth, water-worn.
  **No watercourse exists on this mountain.**
- **The fresh cutting** at the left-hand turning: same script, same tooling,
  cut in a morning rather than a week. Maevis: *"That's a message, not a
  monument."*
- The left corridor descends 1-in-20. **A line of matched river stones every
  thirty feet**, leading left.
- **~180 ft: a depot.** Sealed water jars in quantity, rope, baskets, worn
  tools laid out by size, unidentifiable dried food — and **forty or fifty
  folded veils, stacked like linen.** Neva: *"That's not six people's worth."*
  Nothing was touched.
- **~260 ft: the inscription stops mid-line**, and eight feet later the dressed
  stone stops too. Beyond it the corridor is **raw**, freshly cut, descending —
  with tools set against the wall and the inscription **already being carved
  into the new rock, picking up where the old line ended.**
  Maevis: ***"They're not digging it out. They're finishing it."***
- ***Unbidden* fired.** The vast cold structured thing from the meadow shelf,
  now directly beneath and far closer — and the sense was not of something
  being *there* but of something **being worked on.** Dropped Sharii to one
  knee. Knight pressed against him silently.
- Ignis put *Pass Without Trace* up (**RAW corrected at the table: caster plus
  five, six total** — Maevis and Knight were outside it). Alinar sent Wick
  forward at range, which the spell permits since the bonus attaches at
  casting.

### The ledge
- The raw tunnel opens onto a cut ledge ~40 ft wide above a space with **no
  visible far wall and no visible ceiling.**
- On the floor far below: **fifty or more pale flameless lights in long arcs
  and runs**, each above a three-foot stone cube. An entire arc to the left is
  **still dark.**
- The pulse is coming up out of it — felt in ribs, jaw and the backs of the
  eyes.
- **Eight or nine of them working 80–140 ft along the ledge**, cutting a stair
  down into the void, in **absolute darkness and absolute silence.** No veils
  underground. Spoil stacked neatly.
- **Knight would not leave the tunnel mouth.**

#### The rolls at the ledge
- **Maevis, natural 1.** No knowledge whatsoever. Said "a Drasian sort of
  thing" with no evidence and stopped talking. **Unreliable.**
- **Ignis, 2 on Arcana.** The lights are not fire and not a spell being cast;
  past that, nothing. Standing in the pulse made it hard to think *about* the
  pulse.
- **Sharii, 14 on Investigation — the useful one.** The stair is days old, it
  is the **only** stair, and there are already fifty cubes placed down there.
  **The cubes did not come down this way.** The stair is being cut after the
  fact, for somebody who needs steps.
- **Wick, 22.** Six foot two to six foot six. Built thin, long in forearm and
  shin. Pale hair, tied back, all of them. No armour, no weapons. Long hands.
  Pointed ears, longer and more swept than a Wood Elf's. Eyes open and tracking
  in pitch dark. **And they never look at each other** — two working a face
  fifteen inches apart, swapping tools, never a turned head.
  *"That's not discipline. That's a thing I can't do."*
- **Identification impossible and will remain so by daylight-free means:**
  darkvision renders only greyscale, so no colour was available at any range
  without striking a light.

### The withdrawal and first contact
- Party voted unanimously to pull out. **Maevis asked for ten minutes at the
  junction to take a wax rubbing of the fresh cutting**, which was granted.
- **Maevis rolled a natural 1 on stealth (no PWT, barefoot, loose chips)** and
  went down hard, scattering stone. Everyone else was 18–34.
- **All nine stopped simultaneously for three seconds**, each where they stood,
  facing nothing — then resumed at exactly the same rate. **The spoil-hauler,
  140 ft away and facing the wrong direction, put down its basket and began
  walking toward the tunnel.**
- Alinar carried Maevis (Str 9 — noted and costed); Sharii took over at the
  corridor.
- **It followed at a walk for 560 feet.** Never closed, never fell back, never
  called out, never drew anything — it was carrying nothing. Ignis noted aloud
  that nothing hostile had occurred at any point in a fortnight. Alinar: *"Not
  hostile isn't the same as not a problem."*
- **The party held the junction** while Maevis worked: Alinar, Mya and Wick
  front; Sharii, Neva and Ignis second; Knight on Sharii.
- **At 30 ft Alinar challenged it in Common. It stopped.** Lantern light from
  the stair gave colour for the first time: **white, bloodless, faintly
  translucent skin with dark vessels visible beneath; white hair; and
  enormous, entirely black, wet eyes with no whites and no visible iris.**
  *Unbidden was silent — it was not drawing on anything.*
- **It crouched with both hands visible, set a river stone on the floor halfway
  between them, stood, took three deliberate steps backward, and waited.**
- It watched Maevis take the rubbing and **did nothing to stop her.**
- **Ignis walked up alone and took the stone.** Close detail: it breathes, about
  four times a minute; its hands are a working man's — heavy callus, split
  nails, old stone-chip scars up both forearms. **The stone was body-warm and
  worn smooth from being carried.**
- She bowed. **It did not bow back** — Mya's later reading: gestures are for
  people who look at each other.
- It turned and walked away. **At the edge of the light, 80 ft out, it stopped
  and turned its head** — the first head-turn anyone has observed from any of
  them. Two seconds, then gone.
- **Nobody agrees what it was looking at.** Neva says Sharii and is certain.
  Wick says the wolf. Mya says the stair. Alinar was watching the rear. Maevis
  was looking at her tablet. Ignis had her back to it. Sharii saw it and has
  not said. **This disagreement is the record. Do not resolve it.**

### The stone and the inference
- **Wick, 17:** no markings anywhere. But it is a **matched** one — same size,
  shape and heft as the four on the corridor floor. Somebody sorted these.
- **Maevis, 24:** the rock type is **not local and not anything she has seen
  come out of the ground in Faelyn.** Dense, dark, fine pale flecks. "That came
  a long way before it ever went in a river."
- **Sharii, 19:** handled for a long time — pocket-smooth.
- Party and command assembled, between them:
  - **They are not an army.** No armour, no weapons, no sentries, no patrols,
    no challenge, no alarm.
  - **Long schedule.** Rationing, stocked water, a depot, and keeping livestock
    alive. They intend to be here next spring.
  - **The job is unfinished** — an entire arc of lamps unlit.
  - **Somebody else is in charge.** They reported the intrusion and were
    answered; and they are cutting stairs for someone who cannot get down the
    way they do.
  - **Alinar's hypothesis, unproven:** the Stone Giants may not be invading
    Faelyn, they may be **leaving home.** Two giants staggered on the same
    heading, Knight's mother moving the den for the quiet ones and not for
    giants, and now the whole mountain's game drifting south.
  - **Explicitly not inferable:** nothing connects any of this to the Dark Elf
    pressure from the east or to whoever is directing the war. Vaeroth insisted
    this be said aloud.
- **Thale:** *"They sealed it when they didn't know who we were. Now they do.
  Whatever they do next, it won't be the same thing twice."*

### The decision and the reply
- Vaeroth's three-part statement of position: this is past a captain and he
  will write to the **king**; they are on day eight of three days' rations; and
  **they opened a door they cannot shut.** Maevis confirmed the plug cannot be
  rebuilt with what is on the hill — "a month and a quarry."
- **Alinar proposed a watch**, and Vaeroth worked the logistics with him and
  approved it in principle: **a rotating pair based in Ashwell**, observing the
  seam daily from the treeline at 400 yards, never approaching; three-week
  rotations; resupply from Caldalus every three weeks; snow closes the draw by
  January; and a **four-and-a-half-day reporting lag** to the capital.
  **Names not yet chosen.**
- **Ignis proposed attempting contact.** Vaeroth refused on jurisdiction, not
  merit — *"a captain cannot open relations with an unknown people inside his
  own king's borders"* — and said he would recommend it in writing to the king.
- **Mya found the middle and it was adopted:** they already spoke. Leave an
  object at the junction in reply, in their own grammar, and walk away. No
  meeting, nobody in reach, reversible. Vaeroth authorised it on the grounds
  that it is not a conversation.
- **Maevis dismantled the "shotgun" proposal** (preserve, game, herbs, a gold
  coin): one chosen thing is a sentence, a heap says *we did not understand
  you.* Not gold (says what we value), not food (could read as tribute or as
  feeding an animal; and the preserve is Aldric's, not Alinar's), and above all
  **not a stone** — *"you cannot read their writing. You would be signing a
  document in a language you don't speak."*
- She offered her own **mason's chisel**, forty years' worn, grip dished by one
  thumb — object for object, carried thing for carried thing, claiming nothing.
- **Sharii proposed the wax instead**, reasoning that the creature had watched
  Maevis work and would know what it was for. Maevis took it apart and conceded
  it was better: wax is a **surface and a means of marking it**, portable,
  takeable to Caldalus — but it **asks**, where the chisel does not.
- **Sharii deferred the choice to Mya.** Mya chose **the wax**: the chisel is
  the right answer and a thing that cannot go wrong cannot do anything either,
  and Faelyn does not have room for that.
- **Maevis added a single demonstration stroke** to the corner of the blank
  tablet — *"every man who's ever cut a letter makes one before he starts"* —
  and seated the stylus in the tablet's spine so it read as one object.
- **Dawn, day nine:** six went down. The four river stones were untouched.
  Ignis set the tablet in the gap where the first stone had been. The column
  marched off the shelf for Ashwell. Knight was the only one who looked back.

## Mechanics and Rulings

- **No combat. No damage taken. No deaths.** Three sessions, two without a
  single attack roll.
- **MILESTONE LEVEL 3 → 4**, applied at the long rest on the march back.
  Details in the character sheets; summary below.
- ***Pass Without Trace* corrected to RAW**: caster **plus five**, six total,
  chosen at casting, bonus does not require them to stay close afterward. This
  had been run loosely in earlier scenes; corrected mid-session and the party
  re-planned around it. Maevis and Knight were the two left outside it, which
  is directly why Maevis's natural 1 mattered.
- ***Create or Destroy Water* played loose per house rule**, and it carried the
  session: dry camp ground, nightly perimeter fog (not concentration, so it
  persists while she sleeps), nightly flooding of the plug joints, and the
  full-face quench. Fire-setting that would have needed forty men carrying
  buckets became one caster and four slots a day.
- ***Thaumaturgy* flare ruled cosmetic only** — no heat contribution. Allowed
  and enjoyed.
- ***Thorn Whip* refused as a retrieval tool.** It targets a creature and pulls
  a creature; it cannot pick up an object, and using it at that moment would
  have read as a strike.
- **Conjuring water behind the plug refused**: cannot target an unseen point,
  water does not push, and ~83 lb against a ~550 lb keyed block.
- **No explosive is constructible.** No saltpetre, no sulphur, nothing in a
  militia column's stores. Also tactically wrong — would drop the ridge face
  into the seam.
- **Darkvision is greyscale**, which is the mechanical reason the quiet ones
  could not be identified on the ledge and could only be described once
  lantern light reached the junction.
- **Alinar's Strength 9 costed properly** when carrying Maevis uphill.
- **Locate Object confirmed viable** for checking the tablet from the meadow
  shelf: 1,000 ft range, she has handled it within 30 ft, stone does not block
  it (only lead). **Not viable from Ashwell** — far out of range.
