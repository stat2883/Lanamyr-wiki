# Session 004 — Dorn's Ford

**In-world date:** 387 AW, 3rd Epoch. Late autumn into first snow. Days nine
through seventeen of the survey expedition — from the march off the meadow
shelf, through Ashwell, a day east to Dorn's Ford, and back to Caldalus.
**Party level:** 4 (unchanged).
**Location:** the north draw → Ashwell → Dorn's Ford → Ashwell → Caldalus.
The party split for most of the session and reunited in Caldalus on day 17.

---

## Summary

**No combat. No damage taken. No deaths. Four sessions, three without a single
attack roll.**

The session answered one of the campaign's oldest open questions — **the wax
tablet was taken** — and closed one of its oldest problems: **Ashwell is on a
roll and will eat this winter.**

Ignis asked the captain's permission to track the tablet with *Locate Object*
on the march down, got it, and the column halted ten minutes for her. The
tablet sat still for eight minutes and then **moved, down and north**, and was
still moving when the party walked out of range. Nobody saw who took it, and
the party established on the record that nobody ever will without sitting in
that junction in the dark.

The watch was named (**Wick Pallowen and Dalla Sarn**) and given written
standing orders. Alinar briefed Aldric and then refused to be the authority on
it, sending him to Hestra Fenn instead — which produced the single most
important new fact about the quiet ones to date. Alinar then **handed the
Dorn's Ford negotiation away entirely** and marched west with the column.

At Dorn's Ford, Aldric asked in his own voice in his own yard. Merrick Dorn's
objections were a winter and a levy, not a grudge. Ignis found the way through
by asking about wildlife; Thale broke his word at her invitation to supply the
levy clause; Dovin supplied the boundary. **Charter signed, conditional, with a
deadline in March.**

## Scenes Played

### The march off the shelf — *Locate Object*

- **Ruling established:** *Locate Object* is concentration, not a tripwire.
  Nothing was sensed retroactively. The window was ~15–20 minutes of walking
  before the junction passed out of 1,000 ft.
- **Ignis went to Vaeroth and asked** rather than casting. His only question:
  *"Does it tell them you're doing it?"* On being told no — **"Then yes"** —
  and he **halted the entire column of forty-two for ten minutes** on a frozen
  slope. *"Don't give me the good answer. Give me the one you get."*
- She stood with **Maevis**, who counted and recorded. One 2nd-level slot.
- **Minutes 1–7: stationary.** Everyone present had quietly concluded the
  answer was *untouched*.
- **Minute 8: it moved.** Away, **down and north** — not up the stair — at a
  walking pace, unhurried.
- **Just past minute 9½: out of range, still moving.** Lost, not stopped.
- Maevis's arithmetic: nothing came for it for the better part of half an hour,
  and then something did. *"That's not a patrol finding it. Somebody went and
  fetched it."*
- Mya: *"That's not them tolerating us. That's them answering the post."*
- **Ignis told Vaeroth plainly that she could never have seen WHO took it** —
  only that it started moving. He thanked her for taking it away from him, and
  rewrote his line to the king accordingly: *to know who, a man has to sit in
  the dark in that junction and wait, and I will not order that.*
- Maevis's counter: a man with a lantern in a room with people who need none
  isn't observation, it's an audience.

### Ashwell — Aldric, and Hestra's new detail

- Alinar gave Aldric the full account flat and in order, including that **the
  goats are alive, penned and being fed.** Aldric could not get past *fed*.
- Asked whether "no danger" was his word or the captain's, **Alinar refused to
  answer and sent him to Hestra Fenn** — the only person who had actually been
  in their hands.
- **THE NEW FACT.** Hestra counted **three** of them by breathing, one lighter
  on the floor than the others. And: the first time they set water by her it
  was a hand's width out of reach and she nearly put it over. **Every time
  after, it was exactly where her hand came down.** They watched her fail once
  and never let her fail again. *"I've been fed by people and I've been kept by
  people, and those are different, and I know which one that was."*
- **Aldric's three rulings:** nobody up the north draw; **the north meadow gets
  grazed in spring anyway**; and he tells the whole village everything, because
  sitting on it is how a headman becomes a liar.
- He told Alinar his modesty was a lie, counted the evidence off on his
  fingers, and concluded: *"It's not that you're no good at it. You've decided
  it's better if it isn't a soldier who does it. And you're right. That's what
  makes it hard to thank you for."*

### The watch

- **Named: Wick Pallowen and Dalla Sarn.** Thale accepted both and warned that
  three weeks of being told daily not to walk closer is not a kindness to Wick.
- **Standing orders written by the party** (Vaeroth explicitly delegated this,
  the second formal authority handed to a recruit in a fortnight):
  **do not interact**; on a sighting, both withdraw together to Ashwell,
  village indoors, animals close; the **only** permitted approach is if the wax
  tablet is set on the ground outside the seam.
- **Vaeroth's three amendments:** indoors has a bottom of **three days**;
  both withdraw together, and he accepted the cost of losing eyes out loud;
  and **if the tablet appears they take it** — to Ashwell, then to Caldalus
  under seal, and nobody reads it aloud in a village lane.
- **Maevis objected on the record** and asked that the objection be written:
  taking and leaving nothing is itself a sentence in their grammar. Vaeroth
  noted it, did not change the order, and conceded he had drawn the line where
  it suited him: *"Leaving an object is relations. Collecting one is
  housekeeping."*
- Wick, leaving: *"If that tablet turns up outside, I'm not opening it. I want
  to be stood in a room when somebody who can read it opens it."*

### Ignis, Coll and Hestra — the payoff of the session-3 warning

- **Medicine 21.** The bone is knitting straight and clean, better than it had
  any right to.
- **Coll re-splinted it every day at the same hour for fourteen days**, and can
  recite the hour for each day.
- **The swelling went down and he never adjusted the strap.** The skin under it
  is raw, two fingers wide, broken but not yet infected — because he tied it to
  the exact mark he was shown, on a leg that had changed.
- He noticed on the fourth day, checked his own work twice as taught, found it
  matched what he was shown, and continued. He showed Ignis immediately,
  braced to be told he was wrong.
- Hestra said nothing for ten days: *"I told him it was fine. He'd have cried.
  And you said three weeks, lass."*
- **The lesson is live and unresolved: instructions given to Coll Fenn must
  carry their own conditions, or he will follow them off a cliff.**

### The handoff

- **Alinar and Sharii did not go to Dorn's Ford.** Alinar briefed the four who
  did on all three levers and went west with the column.
- Lever assignment in practice: **Dovin** took the boundary, **Ignis** took the
  toll-and-tonnage bargain, **Thale** took lever three and told Aldric to leave
  it in his pocket — *we're between you and the mountain* is a gift or a threat
  depending on the mouth it comes out of, and out of a uniform only one meaning
  is available.
- **Thale's unanswered question:** *"Do you tell Merrick Dorn?"* Vaeroth
  declined to forbid it and noted he would be four days away.
- Alinar, asked by Aldric why he wasn't coming: *"This kind of thing isn't
  really my forte... there are people better suited. I think they will serve
  you well."*

### Knight

- Ignis asked Knight to come east. He crossed to her, then to Sharii, then back
  — **three times** — and lay down exactly between them, which a four-month-old
  pup has no business doing about a woman he has known nine days.
- **Established limit held:** he will not go where he cannot smell Sharii.
  Ignis accepted it without argument and did not spend a slot to press it.
- She Wild Shaped and played with him for an hour. **Different from session 3:**
  no submissive display this time. He played with her as an equal.
- At the end he fetched a deer bone and **set it down on the grass exactly
  halfway between her and Sharii, stepped back, and sat.**
- Wick, loudly: *"It's a puppy. With a bone. That's what they do."* Mya agreed
  in a tone that agreed with nothing. **Nobody picked it up. Still unexplained.
  Leave it.**

### Dorn's Ford

- A day east. Two hundred people, a mill, a smithy, a chapel. **A river** —
  noted silently by Ignis, who is carrying a water-worn stone of non-local
  rock. No conclusion drawn. No evidence of any kind.
- **Aldric asked, badly, and then honestly**, in the open yard in front of a
  dozen people, and got all of it out including the shame. Ignis stayed silent
  and let him.
- **Merrick Dorn did not gloat.** *"You could have come twenty years ago and
  I'd have signed it at that table in an afternoon. It's not twenty years ago."*
- **His two objections, neither of them a grudge:**
  1. **Relief.** A roll is a list of who owes. Ashwell on his book means his
     barn feeds them from January to spring, and his barn is counted for two
     hundred with nothing spare.
  2. **The levy.** Crown takes men by the head off a chartered roll. Forty-one
     more souls is two more men north, and Ashwell hasn't the men to cover its
     own share, so the difference comes out of Dorn's Ford. *"One of them's got
     a wife expecting in March."*
  - *"And I'd still rather find a way to do it than tell you no in front of my
    own people. So somebody had better give me one, because I can't."*
- **THE TURN — Ignis asked whether anyone here had noticed wildlife coming down
  off the mountain.** A woman named **Keth** answered: the best autumn for game
  in living memory, snare lines full every morning since the leaves turned,
  roe in the stubble in daylight unafraid, a boar in the orchard twice. And
  **Jorrun and his lad went three days up into the hills and came back with two
  birds** — everything is *here*, nothing is *there*. Keth had thought something
  was wrong for weeks and had no words for it.
- **Merrick got the back half himself:** winter wheat, deer in it all winter in
  numbers nobody planned for. *"We've been eating very well off a thing that is
  going to take a third of my harvest next August, and not one of us saw it,
  because it arrived as good luck."*
- He then demanded to know what they knew and where from, and warned he would
  read it as intimidation if handled badly.
- **Ignis told him everything. Persuasion 22.** The hall, the people, the
  sealed door the militia opened and cannot shut, the goats, the captain's
  letter to the king, the watch, the pulse driving the animals off — and that
  the fox and the crow told her, said flatly and without dressing up.
  Then the bargain, then Coll: *"I'd rather be the one who taught the boy than
  the one who was needed."*
- **He believed her, said so in front of witnesses, and said why:** *"nobody
  inventing a tale tells you the part that's their own fault."*
- **Thale broke his word at Ignis's explicit invitation** — and announced that
  he was breaking it before he said anything else. The clause: **a chartered
  settlement that hosts and victuals a crown post is relieved against its levy
  by the heads it supports.** The watch at Ashwell is a post. He cannot grant
  it; it is a petition over the commanding officer's signature, and **he will
  put his name on the recommendation.** Sold as a petition, not a promise.
- **Dovin** supplied the thing nobody had thought of: a charter clerk will not
  amend a roll against a settlement with no drawn boundary, and will bounce it
  twice. He offered to survey it in two days — including the nine acres the
  grandfathers fell out over — and noted the plan's north edge carries the
  neighbouring holding's name, in ink, in a crown archive, permanently.

### The terms Merrick signed

1. **Game rights — unconditional, immediate.** Ashwell takes deer and hare
   under Dorn's Ford hedges, first frost to first sowing, reported to Keth,
   breeding does left in spring. *"I'd have given you that anyway. Don't think
   it buys the charter."*
2. **Boundary — surveyed.** Dovin walks it with Merrick personally. Lodged with
   the Office of Works.
3. **Charter — CONDITIONAL.** Ashwell goes on the roll now so the clerk has a
   village to issue grain against. **It lapses at the spring assessment in
   March if the levy relief is not granted.** *"That's me being able to stand
   in the chapel on Sunday."*

Keth and Jorrun witnessed. Aldric stopped once in the lane on the walk back and
said nothing at all.

### The return and the reunion

- **Column reached Caldalus day 13.** Dorn's Ford party arrived **day 17**,
  four days behind, in the first snow.
- **Vaeroth's letter to the king went on day ~15.** He wrote it four times and
  sent the shortest. **It contains nothing about a levy, a charter, or Dorn's
  Ford**, because none of that had happened yet as far as he knew.
- **The rubbing was taken out of Maevis's hands.** A college scholar could not
  read it, could not place it, did not believe it was a daughter of anything he
  knew, and asked to keep it. She refused. Three days later somebody
  considerably more senior asked less politely and **it went up the chain.**
  She is furious and vocal.
- **The session-2 letter to the tacticians was finally answered — in four
  lines.** Thanks for his diligence; the kingdom's attention is presently on
  the eastern approaches.
- **The war reasserted itself.** Word of a mixed probe out of the east — Dark
  Elves with a handful of Giants, two villages touched, a company engaged, dead
  on both sides. Nobody the party knows. First time in a fortnight anyone in
  the barracks talked about the actual war.
- **Ordow** heard from Neva what Ignis said at the gate, listened all the way
  through, and **did not pick up his pencil.** *"That's hers to tell me."* The
  space on the board is still empty.
- Alinar met them at the gate — he had been going to the gate for two days —
  and asked only *"Did he sign?"* On hearing the full shape of it: *"So
  Ashwell's on a book because of a petition that hasn't been written, over the
  signature of a captain who doesn't know about it, in a war that just took two
  villages in the east. **That's a better position than we had.**"*
- **Thale went straight past everyone to the captain's office** without
  stopping in the yard, because he gave his word in a mill yard and broke it
  and would not let Vaeroth hear it from a surveyor's report. **The door stayed
  closed a long time. Unresolved.**

## Mechanics and Rulings

- **No combat, no damage, no deaths.**
- **No level change.** Party remains 4.
- ***Locate Object* is concentration and nothing else.** It is not a tripwire,
  it has no memory, and it reports direction only — never distance, never
  speed, never identity. Confirmed at the table and then confirmed *in the
  fiction* by Ignis to her captain, which is now a recorded limitation the
  command structure is planning around.
- **Slots spent:** one 2nd-level (*Locate Object*). Everything recovered on
  subsequent long rests. **No standing resource changes.**
- **Wild Shape** used once, non-combat, for an hour.
- **Knight's "will not go where he cannot smell Sharii" limit was tested and
  held.** It is a real constraint, not flavour.
- **Rolls shown:** Ignis Medicine 21 (16+5); Ignis Insight 16 (11+5); Ignis
  Persuasion 22 (17+5).
- **The Persuasion success bought belief and the game bargain. It did not and
  could not solve the levy**, which was a structural problem and needed
  Thale's clause and Dovin's boundary. Noted deliberately: a high roll did not
  substitute for the other two party members having something real to give.
