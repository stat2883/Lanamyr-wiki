# Session 005 — The Vents

**In-world date:** 387 AW, 3rd Epoch. Deep winter. **Days seventeen through
thirty-seven**, ending with an order signed for a march on day thirty-eight.
**Party level:** 4 (unchanged).
**Location:** Caldalus → the Tempest Peaks shoulder (eleven vents) → Ashwell →
Caldalus.

---

## Summary

**No combat. No damage taken. No deaths. Five sessions, four without a single
attack roll.**

The session's spine was a captain writing a letter out loud in a room and being
corrected by the people in it, and then an eight-day surface survey that turned
a hole in a mountain into a **building of unknown extent**.

Three things changed the campaign's shape permanently:

1. **Maevis Tarn priced the staircase.** Two months to a year, most likely
   **spring** — and the one thing she is confident of is that it is cut
   **four or five abreast on a cathedral pitch**. That is not a stair for a
   visitor. That is a stair for a column.
2. **The draw closes in January and the stair finishes in spring.** Faelyn goes
   blind for precisely the period in which the job completes.
3. **Wick Pallowen's eleven vents**, walked and surveyed, lie on an **arc** over
   two and a half miles, with a constructed centre deep under the high Tempest
   Peaks. Maevis demolished the inference herself and then refused to demolish
   the shovel: *a natural fault does not have somebody keeping eleven holes open
   and stacking the spoil on the left-hand side every single time.*

The session also closed the campaign's oldest live debt — **the levy petition is
signed and lodged** — and ended with a written order, carrying its own stopping
condition, for a return to the mountain.

## Scenes Played

### Day seventeen — the morning in Caldalus

- **Alinar** chased the eastern rumours. Ordow gave the shape of it; Alinar then
  found a firsthand source and sat on a step with him until he talked.
- **Ignis** took Knight round the town for an alchemy restock, discovered the
  wolf's leash-radius governs which quarter of the city she may enter, and
  mingled.
- **Thale** had been behind the captain's door since the previous evening.
  Nineteen hours.

### Oris Venn — the eastern testimony

**NEW NPC.** Wood Elf, left arm strapped, invalided west because the eastern
command has one healer for four companies. Sat on the infirmary step because the
ward smells.

What he gave, with his own caveats attached unprompted:

- Both engagements **at night**, second one with no moon.
- Approximately **thirty Dark Elves**. Fast, disciplined, and **they broke
  contact all at once**, which he kept returning to.
- A blade took **Hessel Dray** through a shield, a chain sleeve and the shoulder
  behind it, in one. **He did not see the blade.** He volunteered that he would
  not swear to it in front of an officer.
- **Four giants, at the rear of the line, not in front.** When Ferrow's company
  broke contact into the trees, **the Dark Elves pursued and the giants did
  not.** They stayed with the taken stores.
- *"Everyone's telling it as giants came down on us. They didn't come down on
  anybody. They stood in the field."*

**Alinar Insight 20 (17+3):** Venn is accurate. He under-claimed twice, flagged
his own uncertainty unasked, and the thing he was flattest about — the giants
not pursuing — is the thing he had least reason to invent.

### The sulphur

**NEW NPC: Phessa Molt**, glassblower and herbalist, the Low Weaving. Sold Ignis
a full restock for **5 gp**. **Ignis Persuasion 21 (16+5).**

**There is no sulphur in Caldalus.** About a fortnight before, **three men from
the Office of Works** bought out what stock there was; then **the crown bought
what came in behind them.** Phessa asked neither party what for.

**Maevis's reaction, later, in the captain's office** — and it is the whole
value of the thread:

- Sulphur is the most boring thing in her trade: you melt it to set iron into
  stone. **The Office buys it by the barrel, on a requisition, through the yard
  on Attery, and it takes six weeks and everybody complains.**
- *"We don't send men round the shops."* Three men on foot buying out an
  herbalist means somebody wanted it **that week** and did not want a
  requisition with their name on it.
- She does not know those three men. She would know a cart.

**Vaeroth on the crown's half:** sulphur in quantity is **granary fumigation** —
you burn it in a sealed granary before you fill it, in autumn, when you intend
to store a great deal of grain and have it still there in spring. He noted aloud
that the ordinary explanation (there is a war, a sensible kingdom lays in food)
is probably true and that he had noticed he did not want it to be.

### The letter — the two-column discipline

Vaeroth cleared his desk and **ruled the page down the middle**: what a man saw
on the left, what it means on the right, and **they do not go in the same
letter.**

> *"*Somebody is controlling the Stone Giants* is not a feeling. It is a
> conclusion, it is probably correct, and it is the single fastest way to make a
> man in Caldalus put my letter down. Whereas *the giants stood in the field and
> did not pursue* cannot be argued with, and any man who knows his business will
> arrive at your sentence himself and believe he thought of it. That is not me
> softening it. That is me loading it."*

**Mya's question** was the one nobody else asked: *what did they take?* A raid
takes what it can carry; it does not post four giants on a cart while thirty
soldiers chase men into trees. *"That's not plunder. That's a load. Somebody set
a guard on it."* And: if they are hauling, there is a line, and it has two ends,
and Faelyn has been staring at the other one for a fortnight.

**Vaeroth would not write that either** — but ordered the **grain query**: a
request through the eastern command for the loss schedules off the relief
claims, sent as a boring administrative question about food. *"If what they took
is food, somebody is feeding something, and I would like to know how many
mouths."*

### Maevis and the man with the writ

- **Vennory**, college scholar, could not read the rubbing, could not place it,
  did not believe it descended from anything he knows. Maevis rates him honest
  and said so.
- The man who took it three days later **was not college**: fifties, plain good
  clothes, no robe, no seal, **a crown writ** she was not permitted to read
  properly.
- **The annex porter** — ninety-odd, friendly — says he had seen that same
  gentleman **two days before**, possibly three. **Before Vennory asked to keep
  anything.** Maevis asked twice and got a different number the second time, and
  said so before anyone else could.

**Vaeroth's read:** a crown writ for an unreadable thing off a border mountain
is not sinister, it is correct. What he cannot dispose of is *before* — and the
ordinary roads it could have come down are all his own (forty-two people went
home to families; his own first letter; the Ashwell requisition; a surveyor of
the Office of Works in the city).

> *"I have spent three weeks worrying that nobody above me is listening. It had
> not occurred to me to worry that somebody is."*

**He kept her memory-notebook overnight and copied it by hand**, badly, on
purpose — *"so there will be two bad copies in Faelyn, in two buildings, and
neither of us has to be the single point at which it stops existing."*

### The levy — RESOLVED

Vaeroth: Thale was right about the clause, wrong about the order of it, and
**signing the petition anyway**, because the alternative is punishing a man for
finding the only lever in the county and then letting forty-one people fall off
a roll in March to make the point.

**Signed and lodged.** Boundary needed inside the month; Reyl left for Ashwell
on day twenty with a theodolite.

### Mya's name in the letter

Vaeroth told her to her face, in a room with witnesses, that **page one names
her** and that the man reading it is her father — not asking permission, which
he has not got the authority to ask, but refusing to let her find out
afterwards.

**Her counter, which he adopted:** *"Name everyone, sir. Every name, in order,
same weight, no rank attached to any of them... If I'm the only name he
recognises, it doesn't matter what you wrote. It'll read as my letter."*

### Knight — two days in a field

**Sharii Survival 15 (9+6)** to find Maevis by scent; **Ignis Animal Handling 16
(13+3)** on the hunting lesson.

- **Knight has never been taught to hunt.** Orphaned at roughly eight weeks when
  the Ashwell fighting scattered the pack. The nose is extraordinary; he has no
  patience, no concept of running at where a thing *will be*, and he makes a
  noise like a door the moment it becomes a chase.
- **On every approach he looks back at Sharii**, and that half-second is the
  half-second in which the hare leaves. Told to stop, he did not refuse — **he
  could not parse the request.** *But then I wouldn't know.*
- **He waited** on the fourth attempt — twenty seconds, flattened, still. Caught
  nothing and was delighted.

**The mock-attack drill.** He was never fooled by wolf-Ignis, on any pass. He
does not threaten and does not pursue; **he positions**, broadside across
Sharii's shins, every time, and **will not leave the position** even to meet a
threat at twenty feet. One move, already perfect.

**The cost, and the finding:** on a hard seventh pass something in him answered
before he did, and he made a sound nobody has heard from him. **The low whistle
took three attempts.** He would not take food from Ignis's hand that evening and
slept on Sharii's boots. Fine by morning.

> **A wolf that cannot disengage is a wolf that dies in place.**

**Hide-and-seek.** Stealth 17 vs Perception 21 (advantage, scent) — fourteen
seconds. Three hundred yards, then six hundred, all by **ground trail, nose
down, never casting for wind**. Then Sharii crossed a stream and **Knight
stopped dead and could not continue** — four attempts into the water, no search
of the far bank, visibly panicking inside a minute.

> **The limit is not distance. It is a BREAK in the trail.** Water, scoured
> rock, a gap he cannot get his nose into. This retroactively explains the
> twelve-foot crawl, refusing to go east (road and carts, no ground trail), and
> the sealed seam and the ledge. *"So if I want him somewhere, I have to have
> walked there first."*

### Knight — the conversation

Ignis cast **Speak with Animals before shifting** (self-target, ten minutes, no
concentration; ongoing spell benefits carry through Wild Shape — ruled at the
table).

- *I was pretending* had nothing to land on: **he was never deceived, on any
  pass, since the first night.**
- *Training*, *later*, *be the best you can be* — **did not translate at all.**
  No resistance; nothing on the other side to attach to.
- What landed was the smallest version: ***I want him not to be hurt. You want
  that too. We are the same about that.*** He leaned on her and had evidently
  been braced about it for days.
- **The seventh pass:** not anger, not fear of her. The nearest rendering is
  ***you came the way the thing comes.*** His body answered before he did, a
  noise came out of him that he did not choose, **and he knows it took three
  whistles.** That is what he was carrying.
- **The bone:** a bone, deer, good one, grass, cold. No shape of intention
  anywhere in it. **Either he does not know why, or the why does not live
  anywhere a spell can reach. Still unexplained. Still leaving it.**

**And then he raised something himself, unprompted — the first question he has
ever asked anyone.**

> Sometimes Sharii **goes somewhere without moving.** Knight knows the instant
> it happens — he knew it across a drill yard with his back turned — and **he
> does not know it by smell.** His entire world is a map built of smell and this
> one thing is not on it, and he has no comparison for it, in a head that has
> never needed one.
>
> ***"Is he coming back?"***
>
> **The ten minutes ran out in the middle of it.** She could not answer him.

*(DM note: this is a datum about Knight and about what Unbidden is like from
outside. It confirms and denies nothing about whether Sharii is a Magni. Keep it
that way.)*

### Maevis prices the staircase

**Ignis Persuasion 24 (19+5).** She said no three times, then asked who was
asking, and spent two hours on it complaining throughout.

- **Depth:** 200–400 ft from the ledge to the floor, off lamp size and angle.
  *"That's a shrug with a figure attached."*
- **Stone:** dense, fine-grained, no easy bedding planes. *"It's good stone.
  Which means it's slow stone."*
- **Rate:** Office of Works figures — **one to three treads a day per pair**, in
  daylight, on an eight-hour day, with spoil cleared.
- **Two holes she cannot close:** they may not sleep (factor of two or three),
  and **she does not know how much is already cut** — *"somebody could close
  that in an afternoon by going and looking."*

> **ESTIMATE: two months to a year. Most likely spring.**
> *"That's not a figure. That's a season. And a season is a worthless thing to
> plan around, and anybody who treats it as a date is a fool."*

**What she IS confident of — the stonework, her reliable domain:**

> Shallow rise, deep tread, a **cathedral pitch**, and **four or five people
> abreast.** *"Nobody cuts a cathedral stair four abreast for one slow old man.
> That's a stair for a **column**."*

### January and spring

Mya worked it in front of Ignis and it is the session's worst single fact:

> **The draw shuts in January. The stair finishes in spring.** Faelyn is shut out
> of that mountain for exactly the length of time it takes them to finish, and
> walks back up the draw the week after it is done.

Taken to **Thale**, with Mya's name deliberately kept off it. Thale noticed the
omission inside a minute and let it stand: *"You've left a name out and you
haven't put a different one in, and those are not the same crime. Leaving one
out is a kindness."* He went straight to Vaeroth.

**Ruling (day 21):** the hill post stands until the last safe rotation, then
winters **in Ashwell**, watching the draw mouth rather than the seam, meadow
shelf in clear weather only. Maevis objected that this is no observation at all;
Vaeroth agreed **in writing** and did it anyway.

### The second letter

Went day twenty-two. Mountain on page one, east on page two, **names in the
order Mya asked for**, Tarn's estimate enclosed with every caveat copied out.
Vaeroth read *a season is not a date* aloud twice and included it verbatim.

### Wick's map

Dalla Sarn walked the first report down a day and a half early. **Nothing had
happened** — twelve days, nobody in or out, no light, no movement, no sign of
awareness.

Then Wick's second page: **eleven patches of bare, warm, green ground** where
snow falls and does not stay, running east and a little north along the
shoulder, the furthest the better part of two miles out.

> *"You could not see these in autumn. There was nothing to see them against."*
> *"That hill is breathing out of eleven places. We found one of them and
> thought we'd found the thing."*

### The orders (day twenty-three)

**Six north with the rotation. Surface only. No entry, nothing introduced. Plot
every vent. Tarn for the stone. No contact. Thale commands from Ashwell.**

**And — new — conditions written into every line**, which Vaeroth attributed
explicitly to Coll Fenn:

- Daylight and clear weather only; if it closes, stop and come down.
- *Eleven is a number, not a target.* Nine is an answer if nine is what you get.
- Anything wider than a man's shoulders: **ten paces, mark it, move on.**
- **The order expires in fourteen days whatever is left undone.**

> *"I have about twenty people who do exactly what they're told. I have been
> writing orders for forty years without once putting an end on one of them."*

**Composition problem, raised and not resolved in the office:** the forward six
are Sharii, Alinar, Ignis, Wick, Mya and Maevis — which is exactly the *Pass
Without Trace* cap. **Knight is seven.**

### The vent survey — days 28 to 31

**Four clear days. Eleven of eleven walked. Order carried out as written, with
three days still on it.**

Universal to every live vent:

- Bare ground with a clean edge, living moss in deep winter, warm air.
- **Nothing has ever walked on any of them.** Alinar found it in thirty seconds
  at the first and it held at all ten. In a mountainside stripped of game, the
  single most attractive object for miles, untouched.
- **The spall that should choke the fissure has been cleared out and set down a
  yard to the left, in a low tidy heap. Every time.** The same shape as the
  stones stacked beside the twelve-foot crawl in session 2.

> Maevis: *"Somebody comes out here and cleans it. Eleven times. Along two miles.
> And they come up from underneath, because there's no track on this snow but
> ours."*

**Vent 4 — the sound.** A fissure a foot at the lip, strong airflow. Ear to the
rock: **tools. Stone being struck, a very long way down, absolutely regular.**

Maevis flat on the moss for a long time, then: **three pairs working. Possibly
four; she would not swear to the fourth.** Three separate rhythms that will not
resolve into one, which is how you know it is three and not an echo. **Counted
again on a later day at vent six: three.**

> *"That's the first thing anybody has measured about these people. I can come
> back in a fortnight and tell you whether it's gone up."*

**Vent 4 — Unbidden.** Fired unprompted. **Down**, a long way, and it is **the
same thing he felt at the end of the raw tunnel** — the vast cold structured
thing, something being worked on — **and it is not weaker.** Same intensity,
two-thirds of a mile away on a different face. He lost speech for about fifteen
seconds and then reported it flat. Mya's response was the one that worked in the
hall: *"Which direction?"*

> *"Then either it's enormous, or we're walking along the top of it."*

**Vent 7 — DEAD.** Snow lying on it, moss grey and a season dead, fissure packed
solid with spall nobody removed, no warm air. Fell in naturally. **And there is
an old cleared heap beside it, overgrown and settling, from the last time
anybody did it.** *"They maintained this one, and then they stopped. A year ago.
And they've maintained the other ten since."*

**Vent 9 — two feet at the lip**, comfortably wider than a man's shoulders. The
order was read aloud and obeyed: **ten paces, marked, bearing taken from
distance, walked away from.** Wick was unhappy for about four hundred yards.

**Vents 10 and 11** are much further out than Wick's pacing suggested — eleven
is nearly two and a half miles from the seam, and still has the moss, the warm
air, and the little heap set down a yard to the left.

### Dovin's plan — the arc

Eleven points fixed by bearing and distance. **They are not in a line.** A rule
laid along them touches three and misses eight. They lie on a long shallow
consistent **curve**, bending north as it runs east.

Dovin constructed the centre off three well-spaced points, checked it with a
second trio, and got agreement within a few hundred yards.

> **The centre falls north, deep under the high Tempest Peaks**, well past the
> seam, the draw, and anywhere any Wood Elf has walked in living memory. The
> radius is enormous: **the eleven vents are a fragment of an arc**, not a
> circle. A full circle would be miles across.

**Sharii:** *"The lamps were laid in arcs."*

**Maevis dismantled it herself, unprompted:**

> *"Vents follow the rock. Cracks run along bedding planes and faults, and a
> fault in this formation will curve over two miles exactly like that, for
> reasons that have nothing to do with anybody's building. And that point is the
> centre of a circle drawn through three holes by a man with a rule in a
> farmhouse kitchen. It is not a place. It is arithmetic."*
>
> *"All of which is true, and I'd say every word of it to the captain. And I'd
> also tell him that a natural fault doesn't have somebody walking two and a
> half miles of it with a shovel, stacking the spoil on the left-hand side every
> single time."*

### Locate Object — seventeen castings, one answer

*(Ruled in retroactively at the player's request; see Mechanics below.)*

- **At the seam: nothing.** **At vent one: nothing.** The tablet is not within a
  thousand feet of the mouth of that place.
- Ignis then treated it as a **method** and cast at every vent she had a slot
  for across three days. **Nothing at 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, 11.**
- **Vent 4 answered. Down. North. NOT MOVING.**

She volunteered the limits before being asked: no distance (a thousand feet is
the spell's edge, not a measurement), no surroundings, no identity. And the one
inference she would offer: **it is still a wax tablet.** Melted, scraped flat or
broken up, she does not believe it answers. It answered.

**Dovin plotted the campaign's first below-ground point**, as a broken line off
a surveyed vent — running north, into the same quarter as his constructed
centre. He said twice that the two agreeing does not make either right.

### "A great deal more of you"

Vaeroth's answer to Ignis's question, in full and unflattering:

- What he has: a militia built for poachers, sergeants who have never seen a
  battle, no engineers of his own, one healer who nearly went on the wrong roll.
- What he has in these three: the only people in Faelyn who have been
  underground there, the only ones who have been within thirty feet of one and
  come away, and — *"the part that actually matters"* — **four reports in five
  weeks, every one containing the thing that weakened it.** *"Men write what
  makes them look right. You three keep handing me the knife."*
- **What it means:** *"When a thing needs doing that I cannot give to twenty
  ordinary soldiers, I am going to give it to you. Every time. Not because it's
  fair — you've earned the opposite. You have made yourselves the instrument I
  reach for, and a man reaches for the same instrument until it breaks."*
- **And:** somebody will have to be first back up that mountain after the thaw,
  into a hall with eleven vents and a flight of steps four abreast that somebody
  spent a winter finishing. *"I am not going to pretend I don't know who."*

**And then, to Ignis specifically, in front of witnesses:** the twins are
Faelyn and have a kingdom with a claim on them; she is not, has no oath on her
that he would lean on, and **the door has been open behind her the whole time.**

> *"So I am telling you, once, while I am sober and there is nothing on fire:
> **you can go.** Any week of this winter. And I am telling you because the next
> time I ask you for something, I want to be certain you're there by choice, and
> I want you to be certain I knew the difference."*

### The morning — the three asks

Alinar: *why wait for the thaw?* Vaeroth split it into three and made them pick.

1. **Go back to the junction and the ledge and look at the staircase.** Cheapest
   and most useful; closes Tarn's one hole.
2. **Go down the stair.** Not observation. *"We have stood on a ledge over
   somebody's work and every time they have let us. Walking down their steps
   into the middle of it is a different act."*
3. **Go after the tablet.** Reachable by no route anyone knows.

**The party asked for three**, via **vent 9**, with the stair as fallback.

**Maevis found the hole in under a minute: nine is not four.** The tablet
answered at four; nine is better than a mile beyond it. *"That is not* go in at
nine and get the tablet. *That is* go in at nine and start exploring."

**Vaeroth found the second and it was his own:** nobody knows what nine does
past the lip, **because his own condition kept six obedient people ten paces
back from the one measurement that would tell him whether the plan is
possible.** *"That is a thing I want somebody to write down, because I would like
to be better at this by spring."*

**Thale killed the fallback, and it was made a ruling:** *"A fallback is an
instruction with no condition on it, agreed in a warm room, carried out by tired
people at the end of a long day when they've already failed at the first thing
and don't want to walk home with nothing. The stair is its own question with its
own order and its own date. They don't get to fall into it."*

**Mya reframed the whole thing:**

> *"We left them a thing. On purpose. And they came and got it and nothing bad
> happened. That's the only exchange anyone in this kingdom has ever had with
> them. And now we're proposing to go into their house in the dark and take it
> back. Tarn objected on the record that taking a thing and leaving nothing is a
> sentence in their grammar. I'd like somebody to tell me what sentence THIS
> is."*
>
> *"I'm not arguing against it. I'd go. But in fifteen weeks they have never once
> raised a hand to us, and if that ends I want us to be able to say honestly who
> ended it. Because at the minute, on the evidence, it would be us."*

**Ignis's clarification — they do not intend to take it, only to see where it
went** — moved Mya from theft to trespass. *"That's better. That's not nothing."*
And: *"the one of us who walked up to one of them alone and took what it put
down is the one proposing it. If anybody's earned the benefit of the doubt about
intent down there, it's her."*

## THE ORDER AS WRITTEN (day 37) — carries into session 6

1. **The party goes to vent nine. All of them.** Nobody waits at the seam; a
   healer's escort that cannot reach her is a decoration.
2. **The party holds at ten paces.** Only the scout goes nearer.
3. **The scout may enter.** One person, one form.
4. **⚠ THE CONDITION:** ***go in no further than you can still see daylight
   behind you. The moment you cannot see the opening, you turn round.*** Not a
   judgement — a thing the eyes report.
5. **Tarn counts the hour aloud from outside and calls it at FORTY, not sixty**,
   because the walk out is the same length as the walk in.
6. **Nothing taken, nothing left, nothing touched** — inside as well as out.
7. If nine pinches shut in six feet, **that is a good answer** and nobody is
   disappointed in anybody.
8. **NO FALLBACK. The stair is not on this order.**
9. **Expires in twelve days** whatever is left undone. Weather beats the order.

Maevis, Wick and Mya were each asked to their face whether they were content,
and each said so. **Mya's addition, written in:** *if she comes out and says she
couldn't see anything, we believe her the first time and we don't send her back
in.*
