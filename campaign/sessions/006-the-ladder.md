## Summary so far

**No combat. No damage. No deaths.** Six sessions, five without an attack roll.

Three things happened that change the campaign permanently:

1. **A wolf gave testimony.** A displaced pack on the road said nobody drove
   them off the mountain — *"we did not leave, we stopped being able to stay"* —
   and, unprompted, that the thing in the ground **did not grow, it STEPPED**,
   several times, each step shedding more animals. Also: the silent ones
   **are not it**, and there are **fewer giants** up there than there used to be.
2. **Vent nine is not a vent.** It is the top of a cut passage with a **worn
   staircase-shaft below it**, leading to a worked room containing **a stone
   cube, a flameless light, and an unfinished inscription circle**. The eleven
   vents are the tops of ladders.
3. **FIRST CONTACT, by touch.** Ignis broke the order, touched the cube, and was
   met by one of the quiet ones, which **looked directly at her**, **held out an
   open hand**, **took hers**, and **guided her hand onto the cube** — then
   looked at the way out and went back to work.

The session also settled a four-month-old ambiguity: **Ashwell lost four people
in session 1.** See `001-ashwell.md` → Correction Note.

## Scenes Played

### Day 38 — the gate yard, Caldalus

- **The seventh-body question, resolved by the players:** Knight comes, and
  **Ignis does not cover him** with *Pass Without Trace*. The reasoning offered
  was that his natural stealth and being indigenous wildlife would not rouse

### Days 38–40 — the road, and the wolves

**Encounter check hit on day three of the march.** Nine adult wolves on a fresh
red deer kill, eighty yards off the road, nine miles short of Ashwell.

**The in-fiction cause is four sessions old:** the pulse emptied the mountain of
game in the autumn, the game flooded Dorn's Ford's winter corn, and the
predators came down after it.

**Knight's approach.** He gave his silent shoulder-press warning signal — *and
then went anyway*, because the whistle has never been able to stop him. **Ignis
cast *Speak with Animals*, shifted to a white wolf, and ran to stand beside
him**, and **he stopped** — not for an order, but because another wolf arrived.

> **Operationally important, and Sharii now knows it: the whistle does not stop
> Knight. Ignis does.**

**Sharii cast *Speak with Animals* as well.** Rolls: **Ignis Persuasion 18
(13+5)**, **Sharii Survival 21 (15+6)**, **Sharii Insight 15 (9+6)**,
**Sharii Animal Handling 18 (12+6)** to call the leader back.

**What Sharii read off the field:**
- **They are NOT all female.** At least three read male. The player's hypothesis
  was wrong; the question was right.
- **There are no young.** Not one animal under three years old, in the third
  week of winter, when a healthy pack carries that spring's pups.
- **Two broken packs holding together.** The torn-eared female leads because she
  is the one who got them down, not because they are her litter.
- **They are fat.** Not desperate. Posture, not hunger — and therefore nothing
  to trade them. The only currency is distance.
- **No den, no settlement.** Still moving.
- **Cannibalism ruled out on evidence:** wolves only eat their young under
  starvation, these animals are in better condition than Faelyn's cattle, and
  there are no second bones at the kill.

**The torn-eared female's testimony, verbatim in substance:**
- *"Nothing came for us. I would know a thing that came for us."*
- ***"We did not leave. We stopped being able to stay."***
- It is in the ground, not the air. Not a smell, not a sound. You cannot get
  away from it sideways, only downward.
- Began before the leaves went.
- ***"It did not grow. It stepped."*** More times than she can count. Each step,
  more animals left — which is how two broken packs became one group.
- Worst **high, and north, and deep.**
- *"Some did not come down. I stopped smelling them. I do not know what that is.
  I did not see it."*
- The ones that smell of under: tall, thin, two-legged, soundless, *"cold, like
  the inside of a stone."* She has walked past one close enough to touch and it
  did not turn round. ***"They are not it."*** They were there before it got bad.
  They have never hunted anything.
- **Giants: "They have always been up there. They are fewer."**
- **Asked directly about anything rotten, dead-that-moves, or wrong-alive:
  a flat NO**, and she understood the question. *(Clean negative on Olum.)*

**And she asked them a question nobody in Faelyn has asked:**

> ***"The feeling. Is it coming down?"***

Neither Ignis nor Sharii had an answer.

**To Knight, who walked at nine strange wolves thinking they would be friendly:**
*"That one is with nothing."* Knight answered before anybody could: ***"I am with
him."*** She did not argue and did not accept it; she flicked an ear and left.

**Sharii's unfinished thought, left unresolved:** nine adults off that mountain
with all their young gone — and a pup orphaned at eight weeks at Ashwell's
treeline whose mother moved the den downhill at night, frightened of something
that was not a giant.

### Day 40 — the debrief on the march

Delivered in Vaeroth's two-column discipline without being asked to be.
**Maevis:** *"Somebody has been doing my job for me since the summer and she has
four legs and she cannot count past her own feet, and she has still given me
more steps than I have measurements."* Then she demolished her own inference
(weather steps, cold steps, memory steps) and wrote it down anyway.

**Mya:** *"If it steps, and each step reaches further, then at some point a step
reaches Ashwell."*

**Thale's ruling:** left column goes to the captain whole, including that the
animal asked them something. Right column does not. The precedent that makes it
land was set in the autumn — *"he took a four-month-old pup as a witness on your
brother's word."*

**Alinar said nothing at all** about *there are fewer giants*, which is the
third independent brick in a hypothesis he has never once pushed.

### Day 40, evening — Ashwell, and Aldric's kitchen

Arrived at dusk, **half a day up on the order.** Dovin Reyl is in the village
surveying the boundary with Merrick Dorn, knows nothing of Caldalus since day 20,
and **still has not been asked which office cuts a crown seal.**

**Sharii cast *Speak with Animals* (a slot — rangers have no Ritual Casting) and
explained the wolves to Knight**, gently and without the word *wrong* in it,
including that Ignis very likely saved his life. Knight accepted mortal danger
with no machinery for fearing a thing that did not happen, and then explained
himself:

> ***"I thought they were more of her."***

He has met exactly one other wolf in his life, and she plays with him and brings
him meat by hand. That is his entire sample of what another wolf is.

**Ignis's proposal, and the way she made it.** She said she wants to go back in
and *talk* to them properly, or failing that find a giant and ask why the others
have left — **and then left the room so they could say no without her in it.**

> **Mya:** *"She's done that so you can say no without her in the room. That is
> not what the last person who called a meeting about something did."*

**The responses:**
- **Thale:** not on the order, neither half. Each objective gets its own order
  and its own date. **The giants are a flat no, with his name on it.**
- **Maevis:** the giants burned the barn they were standing next to.
- **Mya:** talking is the only idea in four months that isn't surveying, and
  *"go in and speak is a third thing and I don't know what it is, and neither
  does she, and neither do you."*
- **Wick:** *"She's got one spell that makes six people quiet and there's seven
  of us, and she's the one going in the hole."*

**Then Alinar said he would follow his sergeant, with visible impatience**, and
**Sharii put a harder sentence in his mouth than the one he said**: *"What my
brother means to say is: let's speed this up before more people die."*

**This is the exchange that forced the session-1 correction.** Thale did not ask
*who* — he was there. He named them: **Marrin Sowle, Hesk Varran, the two
Arrowood brothers**, who went into the grain store after the seed corn that
burned anyway, and that Alinar later prised out of a Caldalus clerk over eight
days.

> **Thale:** *"We were late because nobody knew — not because anybody dithered.
> Four months later there's eleven of them on a surveyed plan... None of that
> came from moving fast. Which doesn't make you wrong. It means we're both right
> and only one of us gets to be right in time."*

> **Mya:** *"We were late to Ashwell because we were looking for the wrong thing.
> We're not late to this one. We're EARLY. That's the only advantage anybody in
> this kingdom has got, and spending it to arrive somewhere quicker is how you
> stop having it."*

> **Aldric**, from his own doorway, having lost nobody of his own: *"It'd be US,
> and it'd be me counting them again in the morning... It's my village. I'd like
> a vote in the sentence."*

**THALE'S CONCESSION, and it stands:** he writes to Vaeroth **in the morning**
and puts the contact question up **now**, while the party walks north, rather
than waiting nine days for them to come back down and start a fresh round of
letters. If the captain says yes, the order is cut and sitting on his desk
before the draw closes.

**Alinar to Sharii afterward, without heat:** *"That wasn't what I meant. I
didn't correct you in there and I'm not going to. But that's twice now you've
told a room what I meant, and the first time you were wrong about a druid and
this time you were wrong about me."* Then offered him the last of the preserve.

## WHAT IS INSIDE VENT NINE

### The passage (within sight of daylight)

- **Four feet of natural crack**, narrowing to about **fourteen inches** at the
  tightest point — then it **stops being natural.** Beyond it the walls are
  **cut**: tooled, squared, lightly domed, walkable by a stooped person.
  Somebody met the back of a natural fissure from inside and opened it exactly
  enough, leaving the outside looking broken.
- **The floor slopes down and is WORN SMOOTH** in a band about eighteen inches
  wide. Feet. A great many, over a very long time. **The first physical
  evidence of traffic found anywhere in this campaign.**
- **The walls are blank.** No inscription at all. This passage is not part of
  the thing that has writing on it.

### What the badger's nose got

- Warm wet stone, living moss, strong outward draught. **No smoke, no lamp oil,
  no tallow, no pitch — nothing has ever burned in there.**
- **People.** Faint, even, continuous, soaked into the stone — skin, breath, old
  worn cloth. The smell of a place that is *used*.
- **The same smell as the river stone** from the junction. She is certain.
- **NO food. No cooking. No waste. No latrine. Nothing rotten.**
- **Nothing has ever denned there.** No fox, rat, bat, bird or insect, in the
  warmest driest safest hole on the mountainside. *(Same fact as the untouched
  moss, found from the inside.)*
- **The tools, felt through the floor** — much louder than at the surface.

### The bend, and the decision

About thirty feet in the passage bends right, and **pale steady flameless light
lies on the wall of the bend.** The source is round the corner. **Going round it
means losing sight of the opening**, which is the one line of the order Vaeroth
actually cared about.

**She went round it. Deliberately, having decided to before she entered.**

### The room

- Fifteen feet across, **domed, hand-cut**, toolmarks legible on the ceiling.
- **A three-foot stone cube.** At a hand's breadth it is **covered edge to edge
  in the same script, cut so fine it reads as texture from two feet away** —
  more inscription on that one object than on the entire 350-foot corridor.
- A **flameless light** standing a hand's width clear of the top face, touching
  nothing, illuminating nothing.
- **An inscription circle cut into the floor around it, about two-thirds
  finished, stopping mid-character.**
- **Chips swept into a tidy heap A HAND'S SPAN TO THE LEFT.** Same habit as the
  spall outside all eleven vents, and the stones beside the session-2 crawl.
- **Tools laid out in a row in descending order of size**, in the order you
  would pick them up. Maevis, later: *"That's not tidiness. That's a trade."*
- **A SHAFT in the far floor**, square-cut, past the limit of sight, with
  **hand- and foot-holds chiselled into opposing walls and worn smooth.**
  Warm air rising.

> **THE VENTS ARE THE TOPS OF LADDERS. This is how they get to the surface.**

### The first touch — as the badger

Put a forepaw on the cube. **It is body-warm, and it is not stone — it gives by
a hair, like a drumhead.** The pulse came up the leg, undiluted.

**Constitution save DC 13: 7 + 2 = FAILURE.** The badger's body locked rigid for
about six seconds with the paw still on the face, and something in its chest
answered before she did — *the same shape as what happened in Knight on the
seventh pass.* No damage. Nothing taken.

**While she was locked, two things happened:**
1. **THE LIGHT FLICKERED. Once.** These lights do not flicker — that is the one
   thing established about them since session 3.
2. **Somebody started climbing the shaft.** Unhurried, perfectly even, the pace
   of a person on a ladder they have used ten thousand times.

**Line 6 of the order — *nothing touched* — is broken and cannot be un-broken.**

### She reverted to Tiefling and waited

Reverting is free. **AC 15, HP 31, darkvision 60 (twice the badger's), all slots
intact, one Wild Shape left.** She chose a spell and did not cast it.

### THE CONTACT

**The first time anyone has seen one of them in light, unveiled, at eight feet.**
Six foot four, translucent white skin with the vessels visible in the throat,
white hair tied back, long swept ears, **entirely black eyes that take light and
keep it**, four breaths a minute, no weapon, no armour, clothes worn soft and
clean, and the hands of a lifetime stonecutter.

In order:
1. **Three seconds of complete stillness** on arriving — same as the nine on the
   ledge when Maevis slipped.
2. **Walked past her within four feet without looking at her**, and stepped over
   the cut line of the circle without looking down.
3. **Put its own hand flat on the exact face she had touched — AND NOTHING
   HAPPENED TO IT.**
4. Looked at the light. Moved one tool very slightly.
5. **TURNED AND LOOKED DIRECTLY AT HER, AND HELD IT.** Unprecedented. The
   session-3 head-turn is the only comparable event and nobody could agree what
   it was looking at.
6. **Held out one open hand, palm up, empty, at chest height. And waited.**
7. **She showed it the river stone. IT TOOK ONE BREATH OUT OF RHYTHM** — half a
   beat early and deeper, then straight back to four a minute. **This is the
   only emotional or involuntary reaction any of them has ever shown**, through
   a prisoner, a sealed door, a working ledge and a five-hundred-foot pursuit.
8. **It did not want the stone.** The hand stayed open.
9. **She laid her palm in it.** Cool as a cellar wall, dry, split nails, heavy
   callus, old scars. **AND THE PULSE IS NOT IN IT** — the one thing in that
   room the pulse is absent from. *Independent confirmation of the wolf's "they
   are not it," obtained two days later by touch.*
10. **It turned her hand palm-up and moved it toward the cube — slowly, with
    every opportunity to refuse — and stopped four inches off, and waited,
    looking at her face rather than at her hand.**
11. **She consented and went the last four inches herself.**

### The second touch — as herself, hand-in-hand

**No save. That is a fact about the creature, not a reprieve.** Its hand never
left hers, and the pulse **went through her instead of seizing her**, which let
her perceive the shape of it for the first time:

- **The cube is not the source. It is a place the thing PASSES THROUGH.**
- It reaches **north, and down, and much further than she has any instrument
  for.**
- **There are others. Many.** Not a number — the certainty of hearing a choir
  and knowing it is not one voice.
- **Some are not doing it yet.** There is an **absence in it with a shape.**
  *(Sharii, outside, later: "One arc of them was never lit.")*
- **IT IS NOT SENDING ANYTHING.** Not a horn, a drum, a signal or a warning.

> **It feels like STRAIN. Like a rope under load, like standing with a hand flat
> on a dam. Something very large is HOLDING something, very carefully, and has
> been for a long time — and what bleeds off the edge of it is what emptied the
> mountain.**

> Nothing in it is in a hurry. Nothing in it is angry.

### And then it dismissed her

Lifted its hand off hers. Looked at her for two seconds. **Looked at the
passage. Looked back.** *Go.* No threat in it — the gesture you make to somebody
standing in a doorway you need.

Then it knelt, took the third tool from the row, and **started cutting, four
feet from her, without looking up again.**

**And every tool in the shaft started again at once**, settling straight back
into the three rhythms Maevis counted from a mountainside, *as though something
had been waiting to be told it could.*

### The exit

**She did not spend her second Wild Shape.** She pushed shield and pack through
ahead of her and went out through the fourteen-inch crack as a Tiefling, on her
side, one shoulder at a time. **Athletics 13 (14 − 1).** Four minutes, skin off
her forearm, a long tear in her coat.

**Out at twenty-nine minutes on a forty-minute clock.**

**Knight would not come near her** and stood behind Sharii's leg for the whole
report.

### The report

She gave the complete account standing in the snow, **including both order
breaches, unprompted** — round the corner, deliberately; hand on the stone,
twice. Third session running that she has volunteered the thing that weakens her
own account.

- **Wick:** *"Eleven holes. Eleven ladders. Eleven rooms. Eleven boxes. And the
  seventh one's been shut a year."*
- **Maevis:** *"A fault doesn't have STEPS CUT IN IT. I said a natural fissure
  would curve over two miles and I was right and it does not matter."* Also, on
  the chips: *"Eleven holes, two and a half miles, spoil on the left every time,
  and inside it's the same hand doing the same thing with a brush."* And on the
  tool row: *"Forty years I've laid them out like that. You do it so you can
  find the next one without looking, in the dark."* **She asked for the account
  written in Ignis's words and will sign as witness to the account and not to
  the act.**
- **Mya**, on the breath: *"That's an answer. In nineteen weeks everything
  they've done has been an arrangement. That's the first time anything any of us
  has done has got a REACTION out of one of them. And it wasn't the wax, and it
  wasn't the chisel, and it wasn't six people on a ledge. It was you holding up
  a rock."* And: *"It had you by the hand, thirty feet inside a mountain, and
  nobody would ever have found you. And it put you on the stone and then it
  pointed at the door. I asked three weeks ago what sentence we were writing. I
  still don't know. But that one isn't ours any more — THEY ANSWERED."*
