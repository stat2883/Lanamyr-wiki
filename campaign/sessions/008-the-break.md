# Session 008 — The Break

**In-world date:** 387 AW, 3rd Epoch. Deep winter. **Days one hundred and three
(continued) and one hundred and four.**
**Party level:** 5 (unchanged).
**Location:** the ledge → the stair (two stations) → the seam → the ledge → the
stair to its end → **the ledge north**, a mile and more, off the end of the map.

**⚠ STOPPED MID-SCENE AND MID-SPELL. See the status file.**

---

## Summary

**No combat. No attack roll. No damage. No deaths. Eight sessions, seven without
an attack roll.** And the campaign's largest session by content.

Six things:

1. **THE CONTROL WAS ALREADY IN THE SATCHEL.** Maevis talked herself out of her
   own twelve-board programme: a blank board with Mya's stroke, left where nobody
   met anybody, has been run **twice** and returned the same nine lines both
   times. One board does the work of three. **Board nine was cut by Ignis and
   left in the ledge niche**, and collected **within two and a half hours** by one
   coming **up the stair from the work.**
2. **THE SURVEY.** Maevis got two stations — the ledge and two hundred feet down
   the stair. **Fifty-eight lit, one continuous line with gaps in it, all at one
   height (somebody ran a level), curving away on both hands: we are on the
   inside and it closes.** Radius a mile and a quarter to a mile and a half.
   **Her centre and Dovin Reyl's land in the same few hundred yards. Three
   constructions. She refused to call it a convergence.**
3. **THE STAIR HAS NO BOTTOM.** Four hundred feet of it, and then a half-shaped
   tread and raw rock and air. It is unfinished and it goes nowhere. **The warm
   air of the entire circuit comes up past its last tread.**
4. **THE LEDGE IS THE ROAD**, worn to a polished band, with gallery mouths at
   intervals. Trunk, branches, ladders. It is why there has never been a track on
   that snow and why they have never used the seam.
5. **STONE, MARK, HAND.** One of a file of eleven crossed the road to stand in
   front of **Ignis specifically**, took a breath out of rhythm at the river stone
   for the second time ever, declined it, **touched the stone, then touched the
   exact place on her palm where the stroke was drawn thirty-five days ago**, and
   held its hand open. Then it led her north.
6. **THE BREAK, AND THE ARCH.** A hundred feet of wall **broken, not cut** —
   struck from the open side — with the smashed cube swept left in a heap the
   size of a cart. **The missing cubes are not missing. They are installed and
   failed.** Through the fracture, Ignis found the two cut ends of the line and
   they are **carrying more.**

> **Maevis: *That's an arch. Take a span out and the two beside it carry what it
> was carrying — and then one of them goes. It doesn't fail steadily. It fails in
> steps, and the steps come faster.***
>
> **Which answers the wolf's question, and Aldric's. *Is it coming down?*
> Yes. And faster each time.**

### Day 103 — SURVEY, STATION ONE (the ledge, one hour eleven minutes)

Maevis realised she had stood on that ledge four times this winter and never once
surveyed it, and was audibly angry about it.

- **Fifty-eight lit.** Exact. *(Previously "fifty-odd.")*
- **Evenly spaced to the limit of reading, and all at ONE HEIGHT.** *"Somebody ran
  a level out there. That is a surveyor's job. **They aren't only stonecutters.**"*
  — and the fixed interval matches the gallery shelves under vent nine, which is
  two independent observations of the same rule.
- **⚠ IT IS NOT THREE ARCS. It is one continuous line with gaps in it**, three
  resolvable from that station. **Which independently reproduces Wick's
  "they open new ground."**
- **Forty to sixty missing against fifty-eight standing** — two-thirds to
  three-quarters. **She refused to convert it to a date** and refused to call it a
  convergence with her own autumn staircase estimate.
- **⚠ ONE LAMP SITS BELOW THE LINE.** Far out, east end of the furthest resolvable
  span. **No conclusion written.** *(Third refusal. Fourth came the next day.)*
- **No distance and no centre** — one station, no baseline. She asked for a second
  station two hundred feet down the stair.

### Day 103 — SURVEY, STATION TWO (200 ft down the stair, ~50 minutes)

- **Nearest lamp four hundred yards. Furthest resolvable better than a mile and a

- **The line curves away on both hands: WE ARE ON THE INSIDE, AND IT CLOSES.**
- **Radius one and a quarter to one and a half miles**, large error, offered

- **Depth: the lamp line sits roughly three hundred and forty feet below the

- **⚠ HER CENTRE AND REYL'S LAND INSIDE THE SAME FEW HUNDRED YARDS.** Three

  wrong the same way. Three bad methods agreeing is three. **And I have never in

- **⚠ THE LOW LAMP: the shelf it stands on is cut low too.** Not a slip. *"Somebody

- **OFFERED AND NOT TAKEN:** a traverse carried from the seam to that lamp's

- **⚠ THE LAMPS ARE THE CUBES.** On cut shelves in the wall, **the stair runs past

- **⚠ THE STAIR HAS NO BOTTOM.** Finished flights, then rough, then raw, then a

### Day 104 — the ledge, and the road

- **⚠ THE LEDGE IS WORN.** An eighteen-inch polished band down the middle, like
  the crawls and the corridor. **The stair has never been walked on; the road has
  been walked for longer than anybody can measure.**
- **Four gallery mouths in the first mile**, cut, square, worn, running back east
  and south into the rock.
- **⚠ TRUNK, BRANCHES, LADDERS.** The ledge is the road; the galleries are the
  branches; the vents are the ladders to the surface. **This is how fourteen-plus
  pairs of hands move two and a half miles without a track on the snow — and why
  they have never used the seam this militia opened.**
- **Maevis's warning, accepted:** the ledge follows the curve. **It goes round the
  centre, never toward it.**

### Day 104 — ⚠ THE FILE OF ELEVEN

**First time any of them have been seen in transit, and the largest group seen
outside the gallery.** Baskets, a coil of rope, two sealed water jars — **and one
carrying nothing at all.**

The ledge is about nine feet wide with a void on one side. **They passed at two
feet in lantern light and not one turned a head, slowed, or shielded its eyes.**

**The eleventh — the one carrying nothing — stopped**, crossed to the wall side,
and stood in front of **Ignis**. The other ten walked on and were gone inside
twenty seconds.

- **The river stone, third showing: ONE BREATH OUT OF RHYTHM.** Half a beat early
  and deeper, then back to four a minute. **Replicates session 6. Session 7's
  nothing is now the odd result, not the breath.**
- **It did not take the stone.** It touched the stone in her palm with one finger
  — then moved two inches and **touched the exact place where the stroke was drawn
  on the sixty-ninth day.** **There has been nothing there for thirty-five days.**
- **Then it held its own hand open and waited.**

> **STONE. MARK. HAND.** Three things in a sequence with a request at the end of
> it — **the longest thing any of them has ever said to anybody, and not one bit
> of it on wax.**
>
> **⚠ IT WENT TO THE PLACE.** Either it is the same individual, or **they can
> convey which hand and where, about a named person, thirty-five days later,
> without speaking and without looking at one another.**
>
> **Maevis: *"They have never once treated us as a party."***

- **Ignis put the stone away and offered her empty hand** — **the first time in
  thirty-one weeks a person has initiated anything with one of them, and it was
  accepted.**
- **The hand:** cool, dry, split nails, old scars, callus like saddle leather, and
  **no pulse in it.** **⚠ The callus pattern differs from the vent-nine one** —
  heaviest across the middle joints rather than the outside edge of the palm.
  Different grip, different tool, or a different person. **She will not claim to
  be sure.**
- **It led her north.** Four hundred paces, Maevis counting the whole way.

### Day 104 — ⚠ THE BREAK

- **About a hundred feet of frontage. Not cut — BROKEN.** The dressed facing gone,

- **A heap of smashed cube the size of a cart**, three or four characters to a

- **⚠ MAEVIS GAVE A CONCLUSION**, which she almost never does, because it is stone

- **⚠ MYA: *"Then we're not outside it... That wall is between that thing and

### Day 104 — ⚠ THE ARCH

**Ignis took the guide's hand a second time, of her own initiative.** It turned her
hand palm-down, moved it toward the fracture, **stopped four inches short and
waited**, and she went the last four inches herself — the same procedure as the
cube under vent nine, so **the thing went through her rather than taking hold of
her.**

- **There is no load in the broken stone at all.** The dam is not there.
- **She found the two cut ends either side of the gap — and they are HEAVIER.**
  *"It's the same rope and it's pulled harder at the ends."*

> **Maevis, sitting down on the ledge for the first time this winter:**
> ***"That's an arch. You take a span out of an arch and the two beside it carry
> what it was carrying. And they hold, for a while, because they're good work.
> And then one of them goes. And then the two beside that one carry it. It doesn't
> fail steadily. IT FAILS IN STEPS, AND THE STEPS COME FASTER."***
>
> **Ignis: *"It did not grow. It stepped."*** — the torn-eared female, session 6,
> *more times than I can count, every step shedding animals, and it began before
> the leaves went.* **Every step is a span going.**
>
> **⚠ IS IT COMING DOWN — ASKED BY A WOLF AND BY ALDRIC AND NEVER ANSWERED.
> Maevis: *"Yes. And faster each time."***

- **⚠ THE SHARD.** The guide crouched at the heap, selected one piece — flat, hand-
  sized, **three characters and the beginning of a fourth cut so fine they read as
  texture** — put it in Ignis's hand and **closed her fingers over it with its
  own.**

> **The first thing anybody in Faelyn has ever been given that was cut in stone.**
> Not wax, not a rubbing, not a bad copy by candlelight. **And it came out of a
> heap of something that failed.**
> **Maevis is carrying it wrapped in her instrument cloth.**

### Day 104 — across the gap

**Wild Shape 1 of 2, Tiny spider.** *(Spider Climb — no ability check to cross.)*
Alinar named the numbers before she went: **forty out loud, then fifty-five, then
nothing**, and *"we cannot come and get you."* Mya took the counting watch and
said out loud that it was her turn to do the thing she had abolished that morning.

- **A slab the size of a door let go under her mid-crossing.** Five people heard
  it start to fall. **Nobody heard it land.**
- **FAR SIDE: the ledge resumes and there is NO WORN BAND.** Nobody walks that
  side.
- **The lamps resume lit about forty feet along.** **The gap is one break, a
  hundred feet wide, with a working line either side of it.**
- **⚠ THE FIRST CUBE PAST THE BREAK IS CRACKED CORNER TO CORNER AND STILL LIT** —
  braced with **at least five complete concentric circles**, the outermost running
  past the edge of thirty feet of spider darkvision. **Not a failure recut. A
  survivor, shored up, repeatedly, by people who came back to it.**
- **⚠ THE GUIDE WATCHED HER THE WHOLE CROSSING AND HAS NOT STOPPED.** Sustained
  eye-tracking. In thirty-one weeks the only comparable event is one second on a
  ledge in the autumn that three people still disagree about.

### Day 104 — ⚠ THE SECOND BREAK (where we stopped)

She went on north alone as a spider. **No worn band the whole way. Thirteen lamps,
about a third of a mile.** Then no more lamps ahead.

- **A second gap, much wider — she cannot see the far end of it.**
- **⚠ MORE OF THEM THAN HAVE EVER BEEN SEEN IN ONE PLACE.** Ranks of them, strung
  across broken ground in total darkness. Uncountable from four inches off a floor
  with thirty feet of sight. *The first thing in this mountain that has ever been
  loud.*
- **They are not cutting. They are CLEARING.** Stone hand to hand, baskets, shards
  carried back and stacked.
- **⚠ THERE IS DUST IN THE AIR**, in a structure where every floor anybody has ever
  found was swept. **The heap is started and not finished.**

> **THIS BREAK IS FRESH.**

- **⚠ AND PAST IT, THE FIRST THREE SHELVES CARRY CUBES AND THEY ARE DARK.**
  She cannot see past the third. **The failure propagated.**

**She is fourteen minutes into a sixty-minute Wild Shape. STOPPED HERE.**

## Mechanics and Rulings

- **No combat, no attack roll, no damage, no deaths.** Eight sessions, seven
  without an attack roll.
- **No spell slots spent across both days.** *Druidcraft* is a cantrip. **Ignis
  is at full slots and 38/38 HP. Wild Shape 1 of 2 spent.**
- **RULING — spell preparation.** *Speak with Plants* was proposed, ruled legal
  (moss is a plant; the limiting factor is *within the past day*, against a vent
  eleven heap cleared *inside the week*), and then **withdrawn before the long
  rest**. ***Plant Growth* → *Protection from Energy*** is the change that
  landed. **She now has no area-control or offensive option above *Thorn Whip*,
  one *Hellish Rebuke* and one *Darkness*.**
- **RULING — *Protection from Energy*:** touch, one creature, **concentration**
  (competes with *Pass Without Trace*), type chosen at casting, **fire is wasted
  on herself** (Hellish Resistance does not stack), **cold resistance is not
  warmth.**
- **RULING — Spider Climb crossings need no ability check.** The hazard on the
  fracture face is loose rock, not grip.
- **RULING — reverting is free; the second Wild Shape is the ticket home.**
  Restated and now load-bearing: she is across an impassable gap on her first use.
- **RULING — carried items meld.** The shard was handed to Maevis before the
  crossing on exactly that basis.
- **Maevis's survey is DM-side arithmetic, not a die roll.** All figures were
  given with their error and she volunteered the error every time.
- **No rolls were called all session.**
