# Session 009 — Unlettered

**In-world date:** 387 AW, 3rd Epoch. Deep winter. **Days one hundred and four
(continued) to one hundred and fifteen — 12 to 23 January.**
**Party level:** 5 (unchanged).
**Location:** the ledge, nine hundred feet under it, and back → the seam →
Ashwell → five days of road → **Caldalus**.

**⚠ A PLAYER-FACING CALENDAR WAS ADOPTED THIS SESSION. See the status file.**

---

## Summary

**No combat. No attack roll. No damage. No deaths. Nine sessions, eight without
an attack roll.** Two dice were rolled all session and both were Ignis's.

Six things:

1. **THE STRUCTURE IS A BAND ON A BELL, AND THERE ARE THREE RINGS.** Ignis went
   off the road, down the outside of it, and found that the finished work is a
   **thirty-foot belt** on a wall that undercuts. **Second ring at −85 ft:
   finished, cubes seated, every one dark, no circle cut round any of them, no
   dust, never walked.** **Third band at −190 ft: half-built**, six empty
   shelves, scored outlines, dressed face stopping mid-course, chips swept left.
   **The work goes downward.**
2. **BELOW THAT THE WALL IS GONE.** A torn margin at roughly −300 ft running out
   of sight in both directions, and nothing past it. **The lower part of the
   bell has been taken off the mountain.** Which finishes the arch: the top ring
   is not failing because something is hitting the top ring. **It is the only
   one left holding.**
3. **SHE WENT INSIDE IT.** Six hundred feet down a dragline into open air, where
   the rising air **rotates** and comes in **surges four times a minute** — the
   rate they breathe at. And at ~900 ft she crossed a threshold and the strain
   stopped being *below* her. **Inside it there is no strain at all, no
   attention, no mind, and nothing confined. It is not trying to get out.**
   Which sits directly across Maevis's signed fracture conclusion. Both halves
   on the paper.
4. **BOARD NINE CAME BACK NINE LINES — AND WITH THE CORNER STROKE.** Third
   identical reply; the form is confirmed place- *and* hand-independent. But the
   stroke appeared where **nobody met anybody**, which breaks the rule built the
   day before. **Four boards, four data points, no rule. Maevis will not make
   one up.**
5. **ORIS CAUL.** A royal-household warrant, not entered in any register, held
   **nine renewals** by the current king and **older than him** — one word in
   the book: ***correspondence***. He takes only writing **nobody can identify**,
   never anything inked. **Eleven-plus years at the college annex. Thirty-one
   years at the dead counter. The collection is 231 years old, three kings,
   three holders.** He cannot read a word of it. **He has nineteen pieces of the
   mountain hand, and four of them are the same nine lines.**
6. **ALINAR READ UKURI OUT LOUD.** Off a lead strip from the eastern road, dead
   stock, eight months old, and he disclosed that he can read and write a
   language he has never seen on a page before he translated a word of it.

> **THE LEAD STRIP, in full:**
> ***Two thousand four hundred. Half measure. Thirty days.***
> ***Eighth road. They are not told.***
>
> Alinar's own caveat, volunteered: the numbers and the measure are certain;
> **the last four words he is not sure of** and the grammar is doing something
> he can hear and cannot name.
>
> **This answers, from the far end, the most boring question Vaeroth could think
> of in October — *how many mouths* — which has gone unanswered for three
> months.**

## Scenes Played

### Day 104 — north of the second break, alone

- **Carried.** One of them — **clean, carrying nothing, no dust on him anywhere
  while every other hand on that heap was grey** — stood directly beneath her
  for the whole span crossing, then **laid his open hand flat against the rock
  at her height**, four feet up a broken wall. Not at his chest. **At hers.**
  She got on it. He carried her **nine hundred feet north in three minutes**,
  straight through the ranks, nobody looking up.
- **The ranks are not repairing.** Stone goes south hand to hand and **nothing
  comes north** — no fill, no timber, no new-cut block. They are only moving
  what fell.
- **⚠ PAST THE SECOND BREAK THE ROAD IS WORN AGAIN.** The unwalked stretch is
  the piece **between the two breaks.** North of there the road is in daily use,
  **and it is not being used by anybody coming up from Faelyn.**
- **Eleven dark cubes in a row**, all correct, all seated, **none ringed.**
- **⚠ SHE TOUCHED A LIT ONE.** Two legs on the twelfth.
  *(Con save d20(7)+0 = 7 vs DC 13, failed — six seconds rigid, as under vent
  nine.)* The strain is **the same rope pulled past anything she has a word
  for**, and underneath it, distinct: **it is going.** Slipping, continuously.
  **The cube flickered** — and these lights do not flicker — **and he put his own
  hand flat on it beside her**, and the flicker stopped and her legs came free
  in the same instant, **and there is no way to tell which caused which.**

### Day 112 — ⚠ SERRY VANE AND THE RULE SHE DIDN'T KNOW SHE HAD

The dead counter takes in what arrives with no direction and nobody can place.
It sits a year and is burned. **A line, a date and a road are entered for every
item.**

- Caul comes two or three days after quarter-day, has missed perhaps four in
  thirty-one years. Looks for twenty minutes. **Usually takes nothing** — six or
  seven times in all those years.
- **⚠ HE HAS NEVER ONCE TAKEN A THING THAT WAS WRITTEN WITH A PEN.** Everything
  he takes is **cut, pressed or scratched**: a wax tablet off a river boat, a
  lead tag, a bit of slate, a strip of birch bark.
- **What he takes came in from:** Kuldor caravan ×2, the river south ×2, a
  southern carter's lot ×1, **and the EAST ×2 — the most recent nineteen months
  old, the birch bark, out of a returned soldier's kit from Fardar way.**
- **He has been coming MORE in the last two or three years**, between quarters,
  and asks one question: ***"has anything unlettered come in."***

**⚠ THE REJECTS — Maevis derived the real rule in ten minutes.** Four cut items
currently on the shelf that he has picked up and put down: a foundry tag, a
notched tally, a potter's token, a wax shopping list in plain Aiwiya. **Every
one is in a script somebody in that building can NAME.**

> ***He does not want cut. He wants UNIDENTIFIABLE.*** Cut is the side effect —
> an unknown hand on soft material is exactly the sort of thing that loses its
> direction and ends up there. **Everything Oris Caul has taken in nine years of
> warrant and eleven of college annex is writing nobody in Faelyn could name.**

### Day 112 — ⚠ THE LEAD STRIP

The fifth cut item, and it breaks the pattern: **cut, on soft metal, exactly
what he takes — and he has looked at it twice and left it both times.**

Hammered lead, hand-length, folded twice and flattened, **two short lines
pressed in.** Eastern road, **14 May 387**, no direction, returned soldier's
effects.

- **Maevis, with her own notebook open beside it:** *"It is NOT ours. Different
  stroke, different rhythm, **it has word-breaks and the mountain hand doesn't**,
  and the characters repeat in a way ours never does. It's a real script, and I
  can't read a mark of it."*
- **⚠ ALINAR CAN.** And he put the fence up first, in front of Maevis, Ignis,
  Sharii and a civilian clerk: *"One, I can read it. Two, I have never seen a
  page of Ukuri in my life and I do not know how, and I found out eight days ago
  that I could write it and I did not tell any of you, **and that was mine to
  tell and I didn't.** Three, Serry Vane is standing here and about to hear a
  militia officer read Dark Elf, and she did not ask for that."*

> ***Two thousand four hundred. Half measure. Thirty days.***
> ***Eighth road. They are not told.***

- **His caveat, volunteered before anyone asked:** numbers, measure and *thirty
  days* are certain. **The last four words are not.** *they are not told* /
  *they are not to be told* / *they have not been told yet* — *"the grammar is
  doing something I can hear and can't name, and I'd be guessing."*
- **Ignis:** *"Twenty-four hundred at half for thirty days is not a raiding
  party and it isn't a garrison. **That's a population.** And you don't write
  *half measure* unless the full measure is what you'd normally give them."*
- **Sharii, one sentence:** *"Somebody is feeding twenty-four hundred people,
  and not telling them something."*
- **Maevis wrote the translation down and named its standing herself:** the
  least verifiable object in her possession — an unreadable script rendered by a
  man who cannot explain how he knows it, **with no second reader in the
  kingdom.**

**Both items signed out of the crown ledger** — Alinar took the lead strip (in
full, with rank, which Serry noticed), Ignis took the hazel tally.

**⚠ THE TALLY:** hazel, seven inches, **split lengthwise — one half of a pair,
and the grain will match only its twin.** Broad and narrow notches, a V at the
head, **no owner's mark**. Count: **forty-one.** *Maevis killed the Ashwell
resonance on the spot and had the words "coincidence, flagged by me" written
after it in her own book.* Southern carter's lot, 2 Aug 387.

### Day 112 evening — ⚠ EVERYTHING TO VAEROTH

Two hours standing, breaches first. Mountain, rings, bell, arch, the thing that
is not trying to get out, the palace, the front matter, Caul, the lead strip.

**Four questions and not a fifth:** *Who outside this room knows the boy can
read it?* (Serry Vane, nobody else.) *Is Ashwell's answer still* we don't know*?*
(No — **yes, and faster.**) *Did the king say* page*?* (He said page.) *And
which of you decided not to tell me yesterday?* (Nobody answered. He let it be.)

**His rulings:**
- **On the breach — nothing.** *"I have nothing left to take off you and we both
  know it. It goes in the left column, unsoftened, with the march of a captain's
  doctrine down the page beside it. Somebody in forty years will read this file
  and understand exactly how a company gets to where you are standing, and I
  would like that to be legible."*
- **On Ashwell — inside five minutes.** Charter, levy and boundary all go up **as
  grain** again, reason in no letter. **And a line to Thale by the fastest rider
  before the draw shuts: the answer to Aldric's question has changed to YES, he
  is to be told in person by Thale, and told that it came from us and not from a
  rumour.**
- **On the twenty-four hundred** he stood up. *"I asked how many mouths in
  October because it was the most boring question I could think of. Something
  has just told me, from the other end, on a strip of lead, and the number is
  twenty-four hundred and they are on half."*
- **⚠ ON ALINAR.** *"I am not going to write* this officer reads Ukuri and cannot
  account for it *in a letter, because in a war with Dark Elves that sentence
  does not survive contact with one clever man in a warm office four hundred
  miles away. It will not matter that it's true. It will matter that it's
  useful."* And then: ***"Covering you and using you are the same act. I said
  that to your brother in the autumn. It is now the two of you."*** He will take
  translations and never write where they came from, and one day somebody will
  ask him, and he will have to answer it.
- **⚠ HE REFUSED THE STAKEOUT.** *"Six soldiers lying in wait at a crown counter
  for an elderly man with the king's warrant in his pocket. If I sanction that I
  am doing the thing I refused to do on Tuesday, only worse, and in a cellar."*

**Maevis proposed the alternative and then killed half of it herself:** put an
unreadable thing where he looks and see whether he takes it — *"we have been
doing exactly this to a mountain since October"* — **and the cost is that if he
takes it, the crown household has the script, from us, on purpose.** And:
**she will not make a doctored one.** *"I'll cut an honest copy and tell you
it's a copy, or I'll cut nothing."* *(Second refusal to forge; the first was
session 7.)*

**Alinar then found the hole in the elegant version:** a bait test tells you he
is interested, **not one word of what is on the tablets.** Vaeroth credited it
out loud: ***"You do not question a man who has spent eleven years looking for
something. You show him."***

  two streets of workshops, down into an unfashionable quarter. **A narrow
  three-storey house between a cooper's and a boarded frontage**, tannery three
  doors along. **He unlocks it himself, key on a cord round his neck.**

**⚠ WHAT A NOSE GOT THAT A MAN WOULD NOT:** no cooking and a **stone-cold
chimney on the hardest morning of the winter**; **no second person's scent at
all**, ever; an enormous quantity of **dry paper, dust, old wax and lamp-smoke**
coming out under the door; **the upper two floors shuttered and weathered
shut.** And — **somebody has stood in a doorway at the end of that lane, more
than once, over days.** *A dog can only report that. Vaeroth: write it down, do
not build on it, and do not go and look.*

*(Wild Shape 1 of 2. Just under an hour of two used.)*

### Day 114 — ⚠ SERRY TELLS HIM

Alinar went back to confirm and got it without having to ask.

> **Caul noticed the two gaps on the shelf.** *"There was a lead strip here.
> Folded. Two lines."* **He asked who it was released to. She showed him the
> crown ledger, as she had no grounds to refuse. He read Alinar's name, rank and
> date, stood there about ten seconds saying nothing, and went.**
>
> *"He's never once asked me about a thing he didn't take. Thirty-one years."*

**By quarter to ten on the twenty-second, Oris Caul had Alinar's name.**

Vaeroth's reading: *"It makes going **better**, not worse. A man who is
wondering about you is dangerous to you. A man who has met you is a man. Go as
yourself, rank and name, first sentence. Civil — his king asked for that and his
king was not wrong."*

### Day 115 (23 Jan) — ⚠ THE HOUSE

**He opened the door before they knocked**, having been at the one unshuttered
window very likely since first light.

*"You'll be the one who took the lead strip. Come in before the whole lane sees
you, there's a man at the cooper's who talks."*

One long room: a table, two chairs, a cot, a lamp, **a cold grate with week-old
ash** — and paper, boxes and shelving floor to ceiling down both walls and
across the back, with more stacked up the staircase into the dark. **No order
anybody could use. A room in which one man has been putting things for a very
long time.**

*"I have had eleven years of people not coming here, and then yesterday a clerk
showed me a militia officer's name in a crown ledger, and I did not sleep, and I
would be grateful if you would tell me quickly whether I am in trouble, because
at my age the waiting is the part that does the damage."*

*"I have done nothing I was not warranted to do. Every item I have ever taken is
entered. Every one. **And I have never in my life been asked a single question
about any of it by anybody, including the three men who signed the warrant.**"*

**⚠ ALINAR TOLD HIM EVERYTHING.** The whole mountain, in confidence, leaving no
detail out, and then presented both copies and asked whether he could read them
or knew who could.

*(This goes well beyond what Vaeroth sanctioned — private persons, a copy, and
*we won't say of what.* It is the largest disclosure of the campaign and it was
made to a crown warrant-holder. Nobody has yet called it a breach.)*

Caul did not interrupt once. Sat down on the cot at the nine cutting a stair in
the dark. Put a hand over his mouth at the arch and left it there.

**Then: *"May I see them."***

- **"Yes. I know this hand."** And immediately, before anybody's face could
  finish changing: **"I CANNOT READ IT. Not one character. Neither could the man
  who had this before me, and neither could his. I will not be the thing you
  hoped I was."**
- **The box: NINETEEN items in that hand.** Lead, wax, pale stone, fired clay,
  birch bark, bone. All cut, pressed or scratched. **None inked.** Out of
  perhaps four hundred unidentifiable things on those shelves.
- **The oldest is not his.** It came with the warrant and the box. **231 years,
  three kings, three holders. He is the third.** *"I have been quietly certain
  for sixty years that this hand is what the warrant is actually for, and nobody
  has ever confirmed it to me."*
- **⚠ FOUR OF THE NINETEEN ARE IDENTICAL TO ONE ANOTHER.** A lead strip, a wax
  board, a clay tile, a thin slate — four materials, four separate centuries of
  entries, **the same marks in the same order with the same breaks. NINE LINES.**
- **Maevis's copy of the form matches them.** Character for character.
- **Ignis told him what it is:** *"It isn't a letter. It's a form. It's what they
  send back when you leave them a blank board with a mason's test-stroke in the
  corner. We've run it three times."*

> **⚠ CAUL: *"Then somebody was leaving them blank boards. In Kuldor. And off
> the southern river. And out of the east. FOR TWO HUNDRED YEARS. And I have
> entered every one of them in a ledger and sent the ledger up and never once
> been asked a question about it by anybody."***

- **He is not frightened any more.** He is a man who has just found out that
  sixty years of careful work meant something and he was never told.
- **"I will help you."**
- **His three standing instructions, kept fifty-nine years:** ***You collect. You
  enter. You tell no one.***
- **⚠ AND THE FOURTH THING, which was not an instruction, said once in a corridor
  by the steward who handed him the box, and never repeated to a living soul
  until this minute:**

> ### ***"Don't try to read it."***

**SESSION ENDS HERE.** He has just said it. Nobody has responded.
