Overview

The last surviving Drasu — taller than any present-day race, bronze-skinned, lean and powerful in his true form, which he rarely shows. Galstaff Quint is one of the rare few to have seen it. His body doesn't age, frozen at the physical state it held when Ah'Tabai granted him immortality in 1334, at the Battle of Atral.

Immortality mechanics

Adomorn's physical body can be killed, but it fades and eventually rematerializes at Atral — the precise location where the gift was granted — after some undecided period. He can be removed from a situation temporarily but never permanently by conventional or magical means. Only two things can permanently kill him: the Abaculus, and an Onwu Irridae. Diquyk doesn't yet know the specifics of how his immortality works.

The search for the Abaculus

Has been searching for the Abaculus since 2165 by tracking Olum movement — every lead is legitimate, but each one turns up only natural nesting behavior. The Burnt Plains, a lifeless dead zone, is the one place his methodology gives him no reason to visit, which is precisely why he's never found it there. He's aware of Relics of Creation from the Drasian Vault, but wouldn't share that knowledge unless absolutely necessary. He is the only living being capable of safely moving the Abaculus at all — Irridae and Drasu magic are the only known forces that can move it without physical contact, bypassing its corruption.

He knew many Kings and Queens of Harrad personally, including Morgan, and was present at the Battle of Rohl. He's also searching for a way to free Karma from Greyhawk's curse, without success so far.

The Var Harma and the Monowi Inn

Has contact only with the highest ranks of the Var Harma — likely because he either witnessed their discovery of the Drasian Vault or guided them to it, though which is undecided. Lower-ranking members like Maeglin Leralonde likely don't know of his involvement, or possibly who he even is. His rematerialization point being Atral, which the High Elves were actively excavating at the time, is the likely explanation for how the relationship began in the first place.

Has passed through the Monowi Inn at some point; Elsie Varis treated him like any other traveler, unaware of who he was.

The Elf War

Contacted by the Wood Elves after Mya Li's first crushing defeat against Diquyk's forces. Based on her description of Diquyk's abilities, he suspected a Magni — an identification that shaped his tactical planning — but didn't anticipate how far Diquyk's powers had actually developed, nor his ability to intercept and redirect magic. That gap in his intelligence contributed directly to the cost of the final battle. He fought alongside Mya and the Marauders and remains close to Mya to this day, seeing her on occasion. Does not know Diquyk has returned in 432 AW, and does not know the Abaculus sits beneath the Burnt Plains.

Tentative: it's considered — but not confirmed — that Adomorn suffered a physical death immediately after banishing Diquyk in 392 AW, spending everything he had in the final confrontation and succumbing to his wounds shortly after. His body would have faded and eventually rematerialized at Atral. Mya Li, who knew him only as a powerful legendary wizard and was unaware of his immortality, would have believed him dead.
