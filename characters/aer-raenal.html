Overview

Father of Mya Li and Phelloden — 103 years old at Mya's birth (347 AW) and 113 at Phelloden's, both well within a Wood Elf's reproductive years (50–125). Gifted Mya the twin blades Crimson and Sear. At the time of Mya's youth, before 387 AW, he was roughly 140 — solidly in his prime, with decades still ahead, which removed any competitive pressure between his children over the succession.

Dispatched Mya to help with Tarik's overthrow after being personally told firsthand stories of Tarik's atrocities by Grimmon Herringer and a Plains Elf emissary. After Mya's first crushing defeat against Diquyk's forces, urged her to seek out Adomorn — recognizing the threat was beyond anything the Wood Elves could handle alone.
