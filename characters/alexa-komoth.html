Alexa Komoth

Human, Andar Alive, 432 AW Undeveloped

Overview

A red-haired human barbarian from Andar. Was aboard Wilward Cobbersplint's airship, alongside Skitterskink and Maeglin Leralonde, when it landed in Adenburry in 430 AW and they fought together to exterminate the subterranean Olum nest that breached the town. Made her way to Broldendul afterward — the specific circumstances undeveloped — where she now leads the Four Crowns gang.
