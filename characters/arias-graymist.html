Arias Graymist

Hillfolk human, Felgard 2nd Epoch
Born
2059, Felgard, Utoa
Died
2140, age 81
Sons
Cyrus, Mako, Talon

Overview

A Magni from Felgard, on Utoa — considered exceptional among Magni, likely eclipsed only by Diquyk Lockwood himself. Created the Harbinger Set (a Cloak, Staff, and Amulet, each able to summon a powerful creature on its own) in 2111. Died in 2140 at age 81, and his three sons each inherited one component of the set.
