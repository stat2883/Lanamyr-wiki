Arrama Herringer

Iron Dwarf 2nd Epoch Undeveloped
Role
Matron of the original Herringer family
Died
Killed by bandits, sometime after 1561

Overview

Matron of an Iron Dwarf family of great wealth. Used the family's resources to organize resistance against King Bourbac's enslavement of the Iron Dwarves beginning in 1529, under the political protection of Lord Yllyndel the Ancient. The Herringers are believed to have funded Evander "Freeman"'s assassination of Bourbac in 1561. Once the Iron Dwarves were freed, her political protection vanished, and she was killed by bandits near the Ash Mountains foothills. Grimmon Herringer, who rekindled the family name generations later, is said by some to be Arrama reborn.
