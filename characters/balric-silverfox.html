Overview

Two years older than Gralvin Sutton, from a different village. Enslaved by Tarik Bolden as a manual laborer, not a gladiator — the two met within the slave system under circumstances not yet developed. They escaped together in 408 AW and joined Valeria's network for several years, and Balric was present for Tarik's overthrow in 413 AW.

After Valeria's death he remained close to Gralvin in the early years of his reign. At some point he became Mya Li's traveling companion and love interest — the circumstances undecided. In 432 AW it's just the two of them, adventuring together. No physical description or personality details have been established yet.
