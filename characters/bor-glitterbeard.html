Overview

Cousin of Qenwick Orcsbane — their fathers are brothers. Carries the deep cultural weight of two major periods of Iron Dwarf enslavement (under Bourbac, then Tarik), which has left a lingering distrust of humans among his people.

Grimmon Herringer recognized Bor as a kindred spirit and personally shared the map to the lost kingdom of Nusul with him, along with his knowledge of the Tarantar Mountains. After Grimmon's death in 429 AW, Bor obtained the map itself from his belongings — it had passed through several generations of Herringers before Grimmon, its ultimate origin undeveloped. Bor holds the map; Maeglin has access to it but doesn't possess it, and it only gives an approximate location and hints at a hidden entrance. Bor is aware of multiple legends about the lost kingdom's downfall — Dark Elf involvement is considered plausible — but doesn't yet know Creatures of Olum were the true cause.

Was traveling overland from Fardar into Kimora with Qenwick when they were present at the Adenburry sinkhole event in 430 AW, fighting alongside the others to exterminate the Olum nest. Afterward traveled with Maeglin, Skitterskink, and Qenwick to inform Herringer members across Kimora of Grimmon's death. Travels with Maeglin and Skitterskink in 432 AW.
