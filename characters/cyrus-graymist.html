Cyrus Graymist

Human, Felgard 2nd Epoch
Born
2104
Died
2190, age 86
Inherited
The Staff, one piece of the Harbinger Set

Overview

Son of Arias Graymist. Stayed in Felgard rather than traveling abroad like his brothers, and brokered the peace treaty with the Cobalt Dragonborne that ended their raids on the kingdom by 2114. Died in 2190 at age 86; his tomb sits near Jorvik, and the Staff is believed to remain there with him.
