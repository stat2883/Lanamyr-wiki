Degeric Cent

Hillfolk human Deceased, 381 AW
Born
333 AW
Died
381 AW, age 48, natural causes
Role
Paladin of the Church of Angellia

Overview

A genuinely kind and compassionate man, well liked and respected in his community. His faith in Angellia — Goddess of Love, Empathy, Compassion, and Sacrifice — was real and practical, not abstract: he helped the less fortunate and protected the vulnerable as a matter of course. A capable fighter and a basic magic user, he'd fought alongside his town's guards against Creatures of Olum in the past.

Taking in Diquyk (366 AW)

Degeric was among those who came running when the disturbance began, and witnessed the aftermath firsthand — the charred remains of Diquyk Lockwood's parents and the guards, and Diquyk himself, sobbing and uncomprehending. He didn't weigh the decision; he stepped in, offered comfort, and took the boy in out of the same compassion that defined everything else he did, stepping past the fact that some of the dead guards were men he'd fought beside.

Over roughly ten years he helped Diquyk process his grief, took him along on church duties, and treated him as a father figure without trying to replace what was lost. As Diquyk's magical ability outgrew what Degeric could teach, Degeric shifted from instructor to mentor — urging him to use his power to protect people, which Diquyk took to heart.

Departure and death

Around 376 AW, both of them arrived at the same understanding — Diquyk had outgrown his small town — and the parting was warm and loving. Degeric pointed him toward a region of rumored Olum activity as a direction for what came next. Diquyk visited one last time in 379–381 AW, still pre-corruption; Degeric died in 381 AW at 48, never learning what his student became.
