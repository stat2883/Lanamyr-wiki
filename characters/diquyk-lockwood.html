Diquyk Lockwood "Die Quick"

Hillfolk human Corrupted Alive, 432 AW
Born
352 AW, central Alios
Age (432 AW)
80 by Lanamyr reckoning; physically ~46
Race
Hillfolk human, possibly the most powerful Magni ever born
Appearance (post-corruption)
Pale blue skin, featureless glowing white orb eyes, worn layered clothing

Overview

Frail from birth. At 14 (366 AW), accidentally killed a town guard and both parents with an uncontrolled burst of blue flame — the same power that would define his magic for the rest of his life. Taken in and mentored for roughly ten years by Degeric Cent, a paladin of Angellia who happened to be among those who came running at the disturbance.

Departure and corruption

Around 376 AW, Diquyk parted from Degeric in a mutual, warm recognition that his potential had outgrown his small town, and began creating the Jaunt Gates. He visited Degeric one final time in 379–381 AW, before Degeric's death in 381 AW at 48 — Degeric never learned what his student became.

In 383 AW, curiosity drew Diquyk to the Burnt Plains — a patch of dead earth that received normal rainfall yet supported no life. Investigating, he was guided by the Abaculus (sensing his power) into Greyhawk's old chamber beneath the battlefield debris, and corrupted on contact. He left the Burnt Plains immediately after.

The Elf War (387–392 AW)

Between his corruption and the war's start, Diquyk separately recruited the Stone Giants of the Tempest Peaks (with a demonstration of power and the promise of Faelyn's riches) and the Dark Elves of The Deep (valued for their obscurity and lack of outside alliances), equipping the latter with enchanted weapons. He directed the early war years from between the two fronts via Jaunt Gates, rarely present on the front lines himself.

In 392 AW he made his first major push, inflicting Mya Li's most crushing defeat and killing Kharis Ailwin among others. The same year, in the final battle inside The Deep, he sensed Adomorn's Jaunt Gate transit and foiled the ambush it was meant to spring — killing several of the ten Marauders by intercepting and redirecting Adomorn's own spells as blue flame. He was ultimately banished at physical age 40, once Mya and Adomorn cut off his magical fuel supply by forcing physical combat.

Banishment and return

Diquyk was banished to a separate, parallel physical plane where time moves far more slowly than in Lanamyr — he experienced roughly one subjective year there while 35 years passed at home. He returned in 427 AW through a gate of his own making, back to the Nexus on Myst, physically aged to about 41 despite being 75 by Lanamyr's calendar. Finding his old life abandoned or gone stirred a brief flicker of genuine hurt and fear before the Abaculus redirected it. By 432 AW, his physical age is approximately 46.

Present day

Diquyk's ultimate goal is to ascend to godhood by wielding the Abaculus against the Irridae — not knowing the Abaculus would turn on him in turn. He is currently working to resurrect Greyhawk into Karma, unaware that Karma sits directly above the Abaculus, or that he himself was corrupted at that same location. A significant personal driver is revenge against Adomorn, rooted in authentic hurt and fear the Abaculus has twisted into grievance and a need to dominate — which makes it more dangerous, and more tragic, than pure manipulation would be. Adomorn does not know Diquyk has returned.
