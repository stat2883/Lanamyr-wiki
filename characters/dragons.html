Dragons

3rd Epoch — 432 AW

Aendrontiag (Greyhawk)

A black dragon corrupted by the Abaculus in the 2nd Epoch after finding it in the Tarantar Mountains. Directed the Great War, deliberately relocating the Abaculus to beneath the grasslands near Rohl to draw Creatures of Olum to the city while he built his forces. Killed at the Battle of Rohl in 2165. His dying curse, directed by the Abaculus in its final instant, transformed Karma and bound him to the Burnt Plains as the intended vessel for Greyhawk's eventual resurrection.

Karmarukyntin (Karma)

A gold dragon who fought alongside Adomorn in the Great War, transformed by Greyhawk's dying curse into a golden-skinned Kobold bound to the Burnt Plains — see Kobolds for his present-day form. An amulet contains his life force; if he ventures beyond the plains, he dies. He has been bound there for over 400 years with no knowledge of the Abaculus beneath him or its plans for his body.
