Dwarves

3rd Epoch — 432 AW

Bor Glitterbeard

Iron Dwarf from Fardar, born 399 AW. Battlerager fighting style. Cousin of Qenwick Orcsbane. Holds the map to the lost kingdom of Nusul, obtained from Grimmon Herringer's belongings after his death. Travels with Maeglin Leralonde and Skitterskink in 432 AW.

Qenwick Orcsbane

Iron Dwarf paladin from Fardar, cousin of Bor Glitterbeard. Present at the Adenburry sinkhole event in 430 AW alongside Bor. Returned to Fardar afterward, where he remains as of 432 AW.

Grimmon Herringer

Iron Dwarf, born 372 AW, died 429 AW — killed by Orc raiders. Rebuilt the Herringer name, personally organized the coordinated overthrow of Tarik the Ravager, and founded Herrington. Ambition: a new Dwarven kingdom in the Tarantar Mountains, informed by the legend of the lost kingdom of Nusul.
