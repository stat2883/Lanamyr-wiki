Elsie Varis

Hillfolk human Alive, 432 AW
Age (432 AW)
53
Lives
The Monowi Inn, Monowi Hills
Daughter
Alita Varis

Overview

Plump, always happy and hospitable. Her husband died of sickness some years ago, leaving her to raise Alita alone. Unable to manage the farm by herself afterward, she began offering freshly cooked meals to travelers on the road — over time, kind souls helped her build a small inn next to the farmhouse, which grew into the Monowi Inn, one of the most frequently visited stops on the road between Fardar and Faelyn.

Many merchants and travelers know her by name. She has hosted numerous notable people without ever knowing who they were — including Mya Li and Adomorn the Immortal — and treated every one of them like royalty regardless.
