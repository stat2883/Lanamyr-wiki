Elves

3rd Epoch — 432 AW

Mya Li

Wood Elf princess of Faelyn, born 347 AW. Led the Marauders during the Elf War. See her full entry for details.

Aer'Raenal Liaphyra

Wood Elf, born 244 AW. King of the Wood Elves, ruling from Caldalus. Father of Mya Li and Phelloden. Gifted Mya the twin blades Crimson and Sear.

Phelloden Liaphyra

Wood Elf, born 357 AW. Younger brother of Mya Li by ten years and heir to the throne of Faelyn — a deliberate choice by Mya, not circumstance. More enthusiastic about courtly studies than his sister ever was.

Kharis Ailwin

Wood Elf, born 347 AW. Mya Li's first love interest, killed in the first major encounter with Diquyk Lockwood's forces in 392 AW. A survivor of a Stone Giant attack on his home village, which killed his family and drove him to join the militia.

Maeglin Leralonde

High Elf, born 354 AW, from a noble family in T'sarra. A renowned assassin within the Var Harma. Good friends with Bor Glitterbeard since their first meeting over a ceremonial gold hammer. Travels with Bor and Skitterskink in 432 AW.

Valindra & Cyran

Plains Elf lovers who drowned together in 116 AW when their boat failed far from shore. Cyran did not remain — his absence is the entire tragedy. Valindra's spirit never passed; she still walks the shore of the lake that now bears her name, Valindra's Torment.

Shedyak 2nd Epoch

Dark Elf, Diquyk Lockwood's top lieutenant during the Elf War. Killed by Mya Li in the final battle inside Dark Elf territory.

Lord Yllyndel the Ancient 2nd Epoch

Plains Elf lord who gave Arrama Herringer political protection while she organized resistance against King Bourbac. Died sometime during the Great War.
