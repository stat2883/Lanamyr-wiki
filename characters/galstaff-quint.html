Galstaff Quint

Human Alive, 432 AW
Born
369 AW
Age (432 AW)
63
Occupation
Sage, skilled magic user, historian

Overview

Author of The Journals of Galstaff Quint, the source of all Galstaff quotes throughout the wider world bible. No interest in combat. Began traveling at age 20 and discovered the Nexus on Myst, where he found and studied Diquyk Lockwood's abandoned records well enough to learn safe use of the Jaunt Gates. Has since traveled most of Lanamyr, and met Adomorn personally — one of the rare few to have seen his true Drasu form.

Encountered Skitterskink at some point and sensed remarkable, unexplained power radiating from the acorn in his vial. Travels alone.

Open question: may have discovered Estefania's hidden Harrad lineage — not yet official.
