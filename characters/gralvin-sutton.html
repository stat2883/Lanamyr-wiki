Overview

His village was destroyed by Tarik Bolden when Gralvin was seven (394 AW), and he was taken as a gladiatorial slave — spending fourteen years in the arena. He escaped in 408 AW alongside Balric Silverfox, whom he'd met within the slave system under circumstances not yet developed. The two were directed to Valeria shortly after.

Gralvin and Valeria became friends quickly, then lovers — he saw in her a genuinely pure heart. He and Balric chose to stay and help run her escape network for several years, gathering worsening firsthand accounts of Tarik's cruelty. When Mya Li arrived, pointed to Valeria by the same network, everything crystallized into the coordinated overthrow. Valeria died in the final battle; Gralvin took the throne, reluctantly, at age 26.

Present day

Good friends with Mya Li. Works to mend strained Kimora–Fardar relations following Tarik's enslavement of the Iron Dwarves. Tarik's former followers remain a lingering threat, and Valeria's presence occasionally manifests supernaturally in his life — the nature and degree of it undecided.
