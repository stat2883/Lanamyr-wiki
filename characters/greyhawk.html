Aendrontiag "Greyhawk"

Black dragon Deceased, 2165
Born
658, 2nd Epoch
Died
2165, age ~1,507, Battle of Rohl
Size
~100 feet nose to tail, ~120 foot wingspan

Overview

Black scales with a glossy, sunlit sheen. His two hind legs propelled him skyward for takeoff, while his front claws — despite walking on all fours — could grasp like arms. An immensely powerful physical force by size alone, and also a highly adept magic user and charismatic leader who spoke many languages by choice.

Found the Abaculus in 2136 at roughly 1,478 years old, still in his prime, and was corrupted on contact. He went on to direct the entire Great War, recruiting a number of dragons to his cause willingly while under the Abaculus's influence; those he couldn't persuade were killed or forcibly corrupted at Olum nests, becoming extraordinarily powerful Creatures of Olum in the process. He deliberately relocated the Abaculus from the Tarantar Mountains to beneath the grasslands near Rohl, knowing it would become the war's most pivotal battleground, letting the Abaculus draw Olum to the city while he built his forces there.

Death and legacy

Killed at the Battle of Rohl in 2165. When the battle turned against him, he fled toward the Abaculus, hoping to use it as a weapon against Karma and Adomorn — the same fatal miscalculation Ah'Tabai had made in the 1st Epoch. They killed him before he reached it; Adomorn never learned that was his intention, and so never realized the Abaculus was nearby. In his final instant, the Abaculus acted through his dying curse, transforming Karma into a golden-skinned Kobold and binding him to the Burnt Plains as the intended vessel for Greyhawk's own eventual resurrection — which Diquyk Lockwood is now working to complete.
