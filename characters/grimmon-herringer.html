Grimmon Herringer

Iron Dwarf Deceased, 429 AW
Born
372 AW
Died
429 AW, age 57, killed by Orc raiders
Appearance
Dark brown hair, blue eyes

Overview

Rebuilt the Herringer name during Tarik's reign, said by some to be Arrama Herringer reborn. In his mid-to-late 30s, he personally traveled to Ellendale to draw up simultaneous strike plans with the Plains Elves, then traveled with a Plains Elf emissary to Faelyn to tell King Aer'Raenal and Mya Li firsthand stories of Tarik's atrocities — moving Mya enough that she volunteered to help. He was 41 and present for the culmination of the coordinated overthrow in 413 AW, and went on to found Herrington.

His ambition — a new Dwarven kingdom in the Tarantar Mountains, informed by the legend of the lost kingdom of Nusul and a map passed through generations of Herringers before him — remained unrealized. He recognized Bor Glitterbeard as a kindred spirit and personally shared the map and his knowledge with him. Killed by Orc raiders in 429 AW — believed to be instruments of a framing operation against Kimora orchestrated by Tarik's former followers.
