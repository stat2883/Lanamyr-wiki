Humans

3rd Epoch — 432 AW

Diquyk Lockwood

Hillfolk human born 352 AW, and possibly the most powerful Magni ever born. Corrupted by the Abaculus in 383 AW. Waged the Elf War, was banished in 392 AW, and has now returned to pursue Greyhawk's resurrection.

Gralvin Sutton

The Reluctant King of Kimora, born 387 AW. A former gladiatorial slave under Tarik the Ravager, escaped alongside Balric Silverfox in 408 AW. Took the throne at age 26 after Tarik's overthrow.

Valeria

Ran the escape network that freed Gralvin Sutton and Balric Silverfox. Died in the final battle against Tarik in 413 AW.

Balric Silverfox

Escaped alongside Gralvin Sutton in 408 AW. Now Mya Li's traveling companion.

Tarik Bolden (Tarik the Ravager)

Took the throne of Kimora in 386 AW at age 31 and enslaved both Hillfolk humans and Iron Dwarves from Fardar. Overthrown and killed in 413 AW.

Galstaff Quint

Human sage, skilled magic user, and historian, born 369 AW. Discovered the Nexus on Myst and learned to safely use the Jaunt Gates. Author of The Journals of Galstaff Quint. Travels alone.

Elsie Varis

Hillfolk human, runs the Monowi Inn with her daughter Alita. See the full Monowi Inn entry.

Alita Varis

Hillfolk human, Elsie's daughter. Lives and works at the Monowi Inn, playing violin live several nights each week.

Degeric Cent

Hillfolk human paladin of the Church of Angellia, born 333 AW, died 381 AW. Took in a young Diquyk Lockwood after the incident that killed his parents, mentoring him for roughly ten years.

Estefania

The last surviving, unknowing descendant of the Harrad bloodline, age 23 in 432 AW. Lives somewhere on Alios.
