All Characters

3rd Epoch — 432 AW

Individual character pages will be built out over time — Mya Li is the first. Below is a broad map of who's who.

The immortal and the ancient

Adomorn the Immortal — true name Divinos, the last surviving Drasu, granted immortality by the dying god Ah'Tabai. Appears as an old, wise human. Searching for the Abaculus since 2165.

The corrupted

Diquyk Lockwood — a Hillfolk human and possibly the most powerful Magni ever born, corrupted by the Abaculus in 383 AW. Waged the Elf War, was banished in 392 AW, and has now returned to pursue Greyhawk's resurrection. Aendrontiag (Greyhawk) — a black dragon corrupted in the 2nd Epoch, directed the Great War, killed at the Battle of Rohl. Karmarukyntin (Karma) — a gold dragon transformed into a golden-skinned Kobold by Greyhawk's dying curse, bound above the Abaculus ever since.

The Wood Elves of Faelyn

Mya Li — Wood Elf princess, led the Marauders in the Elf War. Aer'Raenal Liaphyra — King of the Wood Elves, her father. Phelloden Liaphyra — her younger brother and heir to Faelyn. Kharis Ailwin — Mya's first love, killed in the Elf War.

Kimora and the overthrow of Tarik

Gralvin Sutton — the Reluctant King of Kimora, a former gladiatorial slave. Valeria — ran the escape network that freed him, died in the final battle against Tarik. Balric Silverfox — escaped alongside Gralvin, now Mya Li's traveling companion. Tarik Bolden — the tyrant who enslaved Kimora and Fardar, overthrown in 413 AW.

Wanderers and adventurers

Galstaff Quint — sage and historian, discovered the Nexus and safe use of the Jaunt Gates. Maeglin Leralonde — High Elf assassin of the Var Harma. Bor Glitterbeard — Iron Dwarf battlerager, holds the map to the lost kingdom of Nusul. Skitterskink (Skitter) — Mystic Kobold cleric, unknowingly carries a Relic of Creation. Qenwick Orcsbane — Iron Dwarf paladin, Bor's cousin.

The Monowi Inn

Elsie Varis and her daughter Alita Varis run one of the most visited stops on the road between Fardar and Faelyn — unknowingly having hosted both Mya Li and Adomorn as ordinary travelers.

Those who came before

Degeric Cent — the paladin who raised Diquyk after the incident that killed his parents. Grimmon Herringer — rebuilt the Herringer name and organized Tarik's overthrow. Valindra and Cyran — young Plains Elf lovers who drowned in 116 AW; Valindra's spirit still walks the lake that bears her name.

Threads still unresolved

Estefania — the last, unknowing descendant of the Harrad bloodline.
