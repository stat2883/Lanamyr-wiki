Overview

A gold dragon who fought alongside Adomorn in the Great War. When Greyhawk was killed at the Battle of Rohl in 2165, his dying curse — directed by the Abaculus in its final instant — transformed Karma into a golden-skinned Kobold and bound him to the Burnt Plains, directly above the Abaculus itself. An amulet contains his life force; if he ventures beyond the plains, he dies, and his own survival instinct keeps him exactly where the Abaculus needs him without any active maintenance required.

Bound there for over 400 years, living directly above the Abaculus with no knowledge of either its existence or its plans for him — he's the intended vessel for Greyhawk's eventual resurrection. The Abaculus has spent those centuries subtly influencing his mind, discouraging certain thoughts and nudging his attention at key moments, including ensuring he never reported Diquyk's visit in 383 AW to Adomorn. Karma may not realize any deliberate choice was ever made. Adomorn is actively searching for a way to free him from the curse, but hasn't yet found one.

Open question: the specific mechanism of the curse, and the ritual Diquyk needs for Greyhawk's resurrection, are both undeveloped.
