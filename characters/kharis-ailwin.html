Kharis Ailwin

Wood Elf Deceased, 392 AW
Born
347 AW — the same age as Mya Li
Died
392 AW, age 45, first major encounter with Diquyk's forces
Appearance
5'7", light brown hair, brown eyes

Overview

Mya Li's first love interest. Joined the Faelyn militia at essentially the same time as Mya — among the survivors of a Stone Giant attack on a small Wood Elf village that killed his family, which became his primary motivation for joining. Both showed great promise from the start.

Over their first year together they built deep trust on missions; by their second year they'd become serious, though never openly — their roles demanded professionalism, and only their closest friends knew. He was killed alongside several others under Mya's command in the first major encounter with Diquyk Lockwood's forces in 392 AW, young by Wood Elf standards, barely past young adulthood. Because the relationship was never publicly acknowledged, Mya couldn't grieve him openly — she absorbed the loss and channeled it into forming the Marauders that same year.
