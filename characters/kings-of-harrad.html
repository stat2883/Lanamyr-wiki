Kings of Harrad

2nd Epoch, 1207–2165 Confirmed lore

The royal line of Harrad, the dominant human alliance of the 2nd Epoch. Formed in 1207 under Eisengol during the Dragon Wars — the first-born child assumed the throne regardless of gender, which is why the line includes both Kings and Queens. Dissolved peacefully after the Great War in 2165; its territory became Kimora, Ellendale, and Fardar. The bloodline was believed to have ended with King Morgan's death at the Battle of Rohl, but survived through a secondary branch — see Estefania, its last known descendant, unaware of her own heritage.

This page holds the full succession as one place for now, rather than splitting individual monarchs into their own pages — a couple of the more consequential names (Bourbac, Evander) may eventually get fuller treatment of their own.

Full succession

  • Eisengol (King) — Born 1171, reigned 1207–1238, died 1238 of natural causes. First King of Harrad, named at age 36 during the Dragon Wars.
  • Thyman (King) — Born 1198, reigned 1238–1272, died 1272 of natural causes. First-born son of Eisengol, took the throne at 40.
  • Silban (King) — Born 1231, reigned 1272–1316, died 1348 of natural causes at age 117. A skilled magic user. Stepped down at 85 to live among the Plains Elves; his steward Groehl ruled in his absence. His brother Thengol (born 1236) died at 34, cause undecided.
  • Groehl (Steward) — Served 1272–1316. Not of the Harrad bloodline.
  • William (King) — Born 1276, reigned 1316–1329, killed in an attack in 1329 at age 40. Took his fraternal twins Rhein and Rhea to visit Silban when the party was attacked; William and Rhein were killed, only Rhea survived.
  • Erlin (Steward) — Served 1329–1331. Grandson of Groehl, not of the bloodline. Returned governance to Rhea upon her arrival in Rohl in 1331.
  • Rhea (Queen) — Born 1309, reigned 1331–1360, died 1360 of illness. Harrad's first queen. Survived the attack that killed her father and twin at age 20. Met and married Dane during her time away, though he never held the throne.
  • Allana (Queen) — Born 1336, reigned 1360–1402, died 1402 of natural causes. First-born daughter of Rhea and Dane, took the throne at 24.
  • Ravin (King) — Born 1366, reigned 1402–1437, died 1437 of natural causes. First-born son of Allana, took the throne at 36.
  • Elise (Queen) — Born 1389, reigned 1437–1452, died 1458 of natural causes. Took the throne at 48, stepped down at 63.
  • Callan (King) — Born 1423, reigned 1452–1487, died 1497 of natural causes. Took the throne at 29, stepped down at 64.
  • Chandler (King) — Born 1455, reigned 1487–1529, died 1529 on the throne. Recognized Bourbac's undesirable qualities and held the throne as long as he could to keep him from it.
  • Bourbac (King) — Born 1490, reigned 1529–1561, assassinated 1561. A cruel tyrant who enslaved Iron Dwarves on a large scale, prompting the Herringers to organize resistance. Assassinated alongside his son Quinn (age 30) by his nephew Evander, with significant Herringer support.
  • Evander "Freeman" (King) — Born 1528, reigned 1561–1599, death year undecided. Bourbac's nephew. Freed the enslaved Iron Dwarves, earning the title "Freeman," and is regarded as the king who restored Harrad.
  • Holm (King) — Born 1558, reigned 1599–1607, died 1607 of illness. First-born son of Evander. Died without an heir; the throne passed to his sister.
  • Katherine (Queen) — Born 1565, reigned 1607–1637, died 1646 of natural causes. Younger sister of Holm, took the throne at 42, stepped down at 72.
  • Elrich (King) — Born 1595, reigned 1637–1667, died 1670 of natural causes. Took the throne at 42, stepped down at 72.
  • Mercer (King) — Born 1621, reigned 1667–1685, died 1685 on the throne. Took the throne at 46.
  • Sofia (Queen) — Born 1650, reigned 1685–1718, died 1729 of natural causes. Took the throne at 35, stepped down at 68.
  • Zoe (Queen) — Born 1678, reigned 1718–1741, died 1762 of natural causes. Took the throne at 40, stepped down at 63.
  • Ellis (King) — Born 1704, reigned 1741–1778, died 1794 of natural causes at the remarkable age of 90. Took the throne at 37, stepped down at 74.
  • Viktor (King) — Born 1736, reigned 1778–1789, died 1789 of illness on the throne after only 11 years. Took the throne at 42.
  • Isa (Queen) — Born 1774, reigned 1789–1849, died 1857 of natural causes. Known always as Isa, never Isabella. Harrad's youngest ruler, taking the throne at 15, and reigned 60 prosperous years before stepping down at 75. Had fraternal twins Daria and Lara in 1802.
  • Daria (Consort, not a ruler) — Born 1802, death undecided. Born roughly 15 minutes before her twin Lara and graciously deferred the throne to her, holding the title Consort of Queen Lara instead. Had children of her own — a possible tie to Estefania's lineage, not yet confirmed.
  • Lara (Queen) — Born 1802, reigned 1849–1855, death year undecided. Younger twin by roughly 15 minutes. Took the throne at 47, stepped down after only 6 years.
  • Nicolas (King) — Born 1826, reigned 1855–1889, died 1897 of natural causes. Took the throne at 29, stepped down at 63.
  • Patrick (King) — Born 1860, reigned 1889–1925, died 1929 of natural causes. Took the throne at 29, stepped down at 65.
  • Corinna (Queen) — Born 1893, reigned 1925–1963, died 1969 of natural causes. Took the throne at 32, stepped down at 70.
  • Celina (Queen) — Born 1924, reigned 1963–1987, died 1995 of natural causes. Took the throne at 39, stepped down at 63.
  • Camilla (Queen) — Born 1953, reigned 1987–2019, died 2022 of natural causes. Took the throne at 34, stepped down at 66.
  • Laila (Queen) — Born 1980, reigned 2019–2037, died 2048 of natural causes. Took the throne at 39, stepped down at 57.
  • Dolan (King) — Born 2011, reigned 2037–2072, died 2081 of natural causes. Took the throne at 26, stepped down at 61.
  • Adriana (Queen) — Born 2045, reigned 2072–2100, died 2113 of natural causes. Took the throne at 27, stepped down at 55.
  • Rosamund (Queen) — Born 2071, reigned 2100–2136, died 2149 of natural causes at 78 — before the Battle of Rohl. Took the throne at 29, stepped down at 65.
  • Morgan (King) — Born 2102, reigned 2136–2165, killed at the Battle of Rohl in 2165 at age 63. The last King of Harrad — his entire 29-year reign was consumed by the Great War. Adomorn knew him personally. Killed alongside his daughter Victoria (born 2130, age 35) and grandson Nathan (born 2157, age 8). The bloodline survived through a secondary branch, down to Estefania in the present day.
