Kobolds

3rd Epoch — 432 AW

Skitterskink (Skitter)

Mystic Kobold cleric — one of the rare individuals of his race with magical ability. Unknowingly carries a Relic of Creation, an acorn found unaffected by fire in the charred wreckage of a farm his tribe raided. Travels with Bor Glitterbeard and Maeglin Leralonde in 432 AW.

Karmarukyntin (Karma)

Though a golden-skinned Kobold in his present-day form, Karma is by nature a gold dragon, transformed by Greyhawk's dying curse and bound to the Burnt Plains — see his full entry for his true nature.
