Lord Yllyndel the Ancient

Plains Elf 2nd Epoch Undeveloped
Died
Sometime during the Great War (2148 or later)

Overview

A Plains Elf lord who provided political protection to Arrama Herringer as she organized resistance to King Bourbac's enslavement of the Iron Dwarves, beginning in 1529. That protection ended with his death sometime during the Great War, decades after the resistance he'd shielded had already succeeded.
