Overview

From a noble T'sarra family; his father was a caretaker of elven artifacts, which led to Maeglin's own induction into the Var Harma. Obsessed with knowledge, fair and open-minded, but lets emotions cloud his judgement around aloof people — he's grown sterner since becoming a renowned assassin for the organization. Likely doesn't know of Adomorn's true involvement with the Var Harma, or who he really is.

On his first solo expedition, he retrieved a ceremonial gold hammer from a ruined Dwarven temple to Dey near the Troll's Breath Mountains, where he first met Bor Glitterbeard. He traded Bor the hammer for access to Bor's map hinting at a hidden entrance to a lost dwarven kingdom — a partnership of convenience that grew into genuine friendship.

Was aboard Wilward Cobbersplint's airship, alongside Skitterskink and Alexa Komoth, when it landed in Adenburry in 430 AW and fought to exterminate the subterranean Olum nest that breached the town. Travels with Bor and Skitterskink in 432 AW.
