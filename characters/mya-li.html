Overview

Wood Elf princess, daughter of King Aer'Raenal Liaphyra. Originally intended to become queen of Faelyn, Mya grew restless with courtly and scholarly life and deliberately stepped aside from the throne in favor of her younger brother, Phelloden — a choice made openly between them, not one forced by circumstance.

The Elf War

Joined the Faelyn militia at age 40 as Stone Giant attacks began on the kingdom's outskirts. Suffered a crushing first defeat against Diquyk Lockwood's forces in 392 AW, in which her first love interest, Kharis Ailwin, was killed. She went on to form the Marauders — ten Wood Elves assembled for the final incursion into Dark Elf territory — and led the final battle that ended in Diquyk's banishment. All ten Marauders were lost.

Present day

By 432 AW, Mya has found stability in her chosen path. She travels with Balric Silverfox, maintains a close bond with Adomorn, and is good friends with King Gralvin Sutton of Kimora.
