All Organizations

Confirmed lore

The groups, orders, and factions that recur across Lanamyr's history and stories, distinct from individually named people, who are organized by race across the Characters & Organizations section of the sidebar.

Guardians of the Tree

An ancient hereditary Wood Elf secret order from Faelyn, founded early in the 2nd Epoch by Adomorn, who personally chose fewer than 10 founding families. They protect Heaven's Oak and the Abaculus containment chamber within it, and answer only to Adomorn — operating outside the normal structure of Wood Elf society. Members are aware of the Abaculus and the chamber's three purposes. Guardians may take non-Guardian pure Wood Elves as mates; no mixing with other races is allowed. Still vigilant against a renewed Elf War threat, they remain unaware that the Abaculus's strategy has shifted toward resurrecting Greyhawk instead. Internal structure, ritual, and founding family names are undeveloped.

The Marauders

An elite ten-strong Wood Elf unit assembled by Mya Li specifically for the final incursion into Dark Elf territory during the Elf War — formed only after her first crushing defeat against Diquyk Lockwood's forces, and distinct from the militia unit present in that earlier encounter. For the final battle the Marauders split: eight accompanied Mya as a deliberate decoy force, while two accompanied Adomorn through a Jaunt Gate to flank from behind. Diquyk sensed the gate transit and the ambush was foiled; all ten Marauders were dead by the battle's end. Individual member names and backgrounds beyond Mya Li herself are undeveloped.

The Var Harma (the Curators)

A secretive High Elf society from T'sarra, formed to preserve and retrieve artifacts vital to Elven advancement. It operates on a strict need-to-know basis internally — lower-ranking members aren't privy to the organization's deepest knowledge or connections. Its origin traces to the excavation of the ruins of Atral: as more of the city was uncovered, the High Elves formed the Var Harma to protect their findings, and the organization grew beyond that original protective role. Rumored to be headquartered in the ruins of Atral itself, directly above the Drasian Vault. Adomorn has contact only with its highest ranks — likely dating to when he first rematerialized at Atral during the excavations, though this is undecided. Maeglin Leralonde, a renowned assassin within the organization, is a known member who likely doesn't know of Adomorn's involvement or true identity.

The Herringers

Defined by common cause rather than blood. Originally an Iron Dwarf family of great wealth, the Herringers first organized resistance when Matron Arrama Herringer used the family's resources to resist Bourbac's enslavement of the Iron Dwarves, under the political protection of Lord Yllyndel the Ancient. They're believed to have funded Evander "Freeman"'s assassination of Bourbac; once the Dwarves were freed, that protection vanished and Arrama was killed by bandits. The name was rekindled generations later as Tarik's reign took hold, when Grimmon Herringer rebuilt it, personally organized the coordinated overthrow of Tarik, and founded Herrington. His ambition — a new Dwarven kingdom in the Tarantar Mountains, informed by the legend of the lost kingdom of Nusul — remains on hold following his death to Orc raiders in 429 AW. Leadership and the family's future are undecided.

The Four Crowns

A gang based in Broldendul, led by Alexa Komoth. Further details are undeveloped.
