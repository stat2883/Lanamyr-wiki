3rd Epoch — 432 AW

Named characters whose race doesn't fall under Elves, Dwarves, Gnomes, Humans, Dragons, Dragonborne, or Kobolds.

Adomorn the Immortal

True name Divinos. The last surviving Drasu, granted immortality by the dying god Ah'Tabai in the final moments before Ah'Tabai was fully claimed by the Abaculus. Chooses to appear as an old, wise human, though his true form is Drasu — taller than any present-day race, bronze-skinned, lean and powerful. Has been searching for the Abaculus since 2165.
