Phelloden Liaphyra

Wood Elf Alive, 432 AW
Born
357 AW
Age (432 AW)
75 — just entering his prime (62–150)
Family
Son of Aer'Raenal, younger brother of Mya Li by 10 years

Overview

More enthusiastic about his studies than his sister ever was — maps, treaties, and the history of Faelyn's relationships with neighboring kingdoms. Heir to the throne of Faelyn, a deliberate choice Mya made rather than one forced by circumstance. The two share a warm relationship with genuine mutual respect and no rivalry: Phelloden recognizes Mya's potential and would have had no issue if she'd chosen the throne instead, and easy humor and teasing exist naturally between them.
