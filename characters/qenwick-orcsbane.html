Qenwick Orcsbane

Iron Dwarf Alive, 432 AW
Role
Paladin, Fardar
Family
Cousin of Bor Glitterbeard — their fathers are brothers

Overview

Was traveling overland from Fardar into Kimora with Bor when they were present at the Adenburry sinkhole event in 430 AW, fighting alongside the others to exterminate the subterranean Olum nest that breached the town. Afterward traveled with Maeglin, Bor, and Skitterskink to inform Herringer members across Kimora of Grimmon Herringer's death. Returned to Fardar once the news was delivered, and remains there as of 432 AW.
