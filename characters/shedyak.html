Shedyak

Dark Elf Deceased, 392 AW Undeveloped

Overview

Diquyk Lockwood's top lieutenant during the Elf War, commanding the Dark Elf forces recruited from The Deep. Killed by Mya Li in the final battle of the war, inside Dark Elf territory — she acquired the Bow of the Dales from him afterward, a weapon Diquyk had originally created for him.
