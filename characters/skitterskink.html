Skitterskink "Skitter"

Mystic Kobold Alive, 432 AW
Race
Mystic Kobold — one of the rare individuals of his race with magical ability
Carries
An unknowing Relic of Creation

Overview

Cleric to his tribe. During a raid on a small farm that burned several buildings, Skitter found a small acorn completely unaffected by the fire in the charred wreckage. Unable to break, smash, or burn it himself, he placed it in a glass vial he now wears around his neck — with no idea it's a Relic of Creation. Galstaff Quint encountered him at some point and sensed remarkable, unexplained power radiating from the vial.

Prior connections through travel include Wilward Cobbersplint, Alexa Komoth, and Qenwick Orcsbane — shared through the Adenburry sinkhole event in 430 AW, when he was aboard Wilward's airship alongside Alexa and Maeglin and fought to exterminate the subterranean Olum nest. Afterward traveled with Maeglin, Bor, and Qenwick to inform Herringer members across Kimora of Grimmon Herringer's death. Current companions in 432 AW: Bor Glitterbeard and Maeglin Leralonde.

Open question: which Irridae Skitter worships, and exactly when and how Galstaff encountered him, are both undecided.
