Tarik Bolden Tarik the Ravager

Human Deceased, 413 AW
Born
355 AW
Took the throne
386 AW, age 31
Died
413 AW, age 58, overthrown and killed

Overview

Spent years building support before taking the throne of Kimora in 386 AW at age 31. Immediately began raiding villages and enslaving populations — both Hillfolk humans and Iron Dwarves from Fardar. Enslaved humans were used as gladiators and laborers; enslaved Iron Dwarves built the Palace of Shatazaar. Those too injured to work were executed.

Overthrown and killed in 413 AW by a coordinated strike from Gralvin Sutton's escape network, the Plains Elves, and the Herringers. His former followers have had nearly two decades to organize since, and are suspected of orchestrating a later Orc framing operation against Kimora.
