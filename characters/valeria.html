Valeria

Human Deceased, 413 AW
Died
413 AW, final battle of Tarik's overthrow
Appearance
Long blonde hair, slender yet powerful build, striking blue-gray eyes, a soft deep voice

Overview

Mischievous and warm, quick with a friendly barb or teasing remark, with no love for authority — a principled defiance she wore lightly. Ran the informal escape network that freed enslaved people from Tarik Bolden's Kimora for years, alongside Gralvin Sutton and Balric Silverfox, who joined the network after their own escape. Gralvin and Valeria became lovers.

When Mya Li arrived, pointed to the network by the same channels, Valeria recognized immediately what it could mean for Tarik's overthrow. She died in the final battle.

Present day

Her presence occasionally manifests supernaturally in Gralvin's life in 432 AW — the degree and nature of it undecided.
