Valindra & Cyran

Plains Elves Deceased, 116 AW
Died
116 AW, drowned together, exact ages undeveloped
Location
A lake near their nomadic settlement in Ellendale, now called Valindra's Torment

Overview

Young Plains Elf lovers from a nomadic settlement near a small lake in Ellendale. Their boat failed far from shore, and neither was a strong swimmer — both drowned. The settlement moved on shortly after, as nomadic settlements do.

Cyran

Did not remain. His absence is the entire tragedy.

Valindra

Her spirit never passed on. She inhabits the lake and walks its shoreline, waiting for Cyran to return. Centuries have decayed her appearance — pale gray skin, dark hair hanging in an unkempt mass partially covering her face, features grown increasingly indistinct with time. She does not speak and does not threaten; she mourns. Those who come close to the lake feel her torment as though it were their own, grief bleeding outward from the water's edge — the sobbing and wailing heard at a distance are hers. Stories of an evil or vengeful spirit guarding the lake are exaggeration born of fear and distance. She is still waiting.
