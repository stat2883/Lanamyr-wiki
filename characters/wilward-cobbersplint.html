Wilward Cobbersplint

Sky Gnome Alive, 432 AW
Occupation
Airship owner and operator
Lives
Adenburry, Kimora

Overview

Was carrying Skitterskink, Alexa Komoth, and Maeglin Leralonde aboard his airship when they landed in Adenburry in 430 AW. During the sinkhole collapse that followed, fires spread from nearby buildings to the airship, which caught fire and tore in half as the ground gave way beneath it. Chose to stay in Adenburry afterward to help the town rebuild and to reconstruct his airship, and remains there as of 432 AW.
