Continents

Confirmed lore

Lanamyr has three major continents. Alios and Edura are joined by land and form one large landmass; Utoa is the only major continent entirely surrounded by ocean. All other landmasses are islands of varying size. All borders below are approximations defined by natural geographic features, not political lines.

Alios

The largest continent, dominating the northern and central portion of the world. Central Alios is predominantly Deciduous and Mixed Forest, transitioning to Steppes and Cool Desert in the east, and warming toward Tropical Seasonal Forest in the south. The north is cold and harsh, home to the Tarantar Mountains — the tallest and coldest range in Lanamyr. Alios is home to the kingdoms of Kimora, Fardar, Ellendale, Faelyn, Hellar, and Andar.

Edura

Considerably smaller than Alios, connected to it via the Blackwing Hills land corridor. Northern Edura is Tropical Rain Forest, home to T'sarra and the ruins of Atral. Central Edura holds the Void Sands, a hot desert. Southern Edura cools progressively toward tundra at its southern tip.

Utoa

The only continent isolated entirely by ocean. Dominated by the Desolate Tops, a very high east-west mountain range. Northwestern Utoa is Steppes and Cool Desert, home to Felgard; the south reaches Polar Ice and Polar Desert at its extreme tip.

Major islands

Each significant island or archipelago is controlled entirely by a single race and kingdom: Icefall (Ice Dwarves), Jokul (Silver Dragonborne), the Malulo Archipelago (Sea Elves, mostly underwater), Myst (Sky Gnomes, home of the Nexus), and a still-unnamed island east of Utoa (Snow Elves). Remote western islands include the Gleaming Spires (Polar Ice, self-luminous peaks) and the volcanic Firethorn Peaks.
