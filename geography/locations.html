Key Locations

Confirmed lore

Individual location pages will be built out over time — the Monowi Inn is the first. Below is a broad reference to the other notable sites of Lanamyr.

The Burnt Plains

A vast swath of scorched, lifeless earth near the ruins of Rohl, within Kimora. Beneath it lies the Abaculus. Rainfall is normal — the same as the fertile land around it — yet nothing grows, the result of Greyhawk's corruption leaking into the ground centuries ago. Karma, bound there by Greyhawk's dying curse, sits at its center.

Heaven's Oak

A landmark in Faelyn containing a containment chamber built under Adomorn's instruction early in the 2nd Epoch, intended to physically contain the Abaculus, mask it from Creatures of Olum, and prevent it acting as a beacon to the Irridae. Intact, but has never held the Abaculus. Protected by the Guardians of the Tree.

The Nexus

A deep cavern on Myst, and the central hub for all Jaunt Gates. Diquyk's records of the gates' creation remain here, discovered by Galstaff Quint.

Atral (Ruins)

The first and greatest Drasian city, built on Edura along the Waywin River. Destroyed in the Sundering. Excavated for centuries by the High Elves of T'sarra and the secretive Var Harma, who are rumored to have their headquarters here — directly above the Drasian Vault.

Rohl (Ruins)

The former capital of Harrad, within present-day Kimora. Its palace was destroyed during the Great War; locals avoid the site.

The Monowi Inn

One of the most frequently visited stops on the road between Fardar and Faelyn, run by Elsie and Alita Varis. See the full Monowi Inn entry.

Valindra's Torment

A small lake in the plains of Ellendale, named for the Plains Elf who drowned there in 116 AW and whose grieving spirit still walks its shore.

Other notable sites

Adenburry — a Kimoran border town, site of the 430 AW Olum sinkhole event. Herrington — an Ash Mountains mining town founded by Grimmon Herringer. Broldendul — home of the Four Crowns gang. Tower of Grannel — Adomorn's base of operations. Drasian Vault — a hidden repository of Irridae records beneath Atral.
