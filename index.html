Lanamyr

Lanamyr is a world created by the N'du Irridae — with the participation of some Itule Irridae — to hide the Abaculus, a sentient, indestructible crystal that craves the destruction of all existence.

The present day sits in the 3rd Epoch, year 432 AW (After War). Two prior ages shaped the world: the 1st Epoch of the Drasu and Draak, ended by the Battle of Atral and the Sundering; and the 2nd Epoch of kingdoms, ended by the Battle of Rohl and the fall of Harrad.
