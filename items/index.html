Items & Artifacts

Confirmed lore

The Abaculus

A sentient, indestructible crystal, black and close to obsidian, with faint swirls of green, blue, and red moving across its surface. It cannot be destroyed by any known means. On contact it corrupts whoever touches it; in close proximity it can subtly influence thought and behavior. It can destroy an Irridae outright, and can consume a Relic of Creation to permanently erase whatever it represents from existence. It wants to be found, and its pattern across the ages has been the same: identify an exceptionally powerful being, corrupt them, and use them as a weapon — Greyhawk in the 2nd Epoch, Diquyk Lockwood in the 3rd. Currently beneath the Burnt Plains in Kimora, in active suppression, unknown to Adomorn.

Relics of Creation

Physical byproducts of the Irridae's act of creation — each one a discrete object representing something created, whether physical (a species, a mountain range) or abstract (love, grief, courage). All are virtually indestructible by conventional means; only the Abaculus can destroy one, permanently erasing what it represents from all of reality. The only confirmed Relic in the present day is an acorn, carried unknowingly by the Kobold Skitterskink in a glass vial around his neck.

Jaunt Gates

A network of magical portals connected through the Nexus on Myst, created by Diquyk Lockwood before his corruption. They pass travelers through a featureless void — one of the planes stripped of energy during Lanamyr's own creation. Conscious passage causes madness and death; safe transit requires being unconscious. Galstaff Quint discovered Diquyk's records at the Nexus and learned to use them safely.

The Harbinger Set

A Cloak, Staff, and Amulet created by the Magni Arias Graymist in 2111 (2nd Epoch). Each piece can summon a powerful creature on its own; combined, their effect scales dramatically. The three components passed to his sons Cyrus, Mako, and Talon, and are believed to remain in their respective tombs.

Weapons of note

  • Crimson and Sear — twin blades gifted to Mya Li by her father Aer'Raenal
  • Bow of the Dales — created by Diquyk for his lieutenant Shedyak, taken by Mya Li after killing him in the final battle of the Elf War; fires purple-pink bolts of ambient energy
  • Karma's Amulet — contains Karma's life force, sustaining him within the Burnt Plains

The Journals of Galstaff Quint

The written travel records of the sage Galstaff Quint — the source of all Galstaff quotes throughout the wider world bible.
