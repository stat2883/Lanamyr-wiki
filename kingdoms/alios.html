Kingdoms of Alios

3rd Epoch — 432 AW

Alios is the largest continent and home to more kingdoms than anywhere else in Lanamyr. See Continents for its geography.

  • Kimora — Hillfolk humans, capital Shatazaar, ruled by King Gralvin Sutton
  • Fardar — Iron Dwarves, one of the richest kingdoms in Lanamyr
  • Ellendale — Plains Elves
  • Faelyn — Wood Elves, capital Caldalus, home of the Guardians of the Tree
  • Hellar — Dark Dwarves, an underground city in the Forbidden Peaks
  • Andar — Northmen humans, the great northern peninsula

Lost to history

Two former Alios kingdoms no longer exist:

  • Harrad — the dominant 2nd Epoch human alliance, dissolved after the Great War; its territory became Kimora, Ellendale, and Fardar
  • Nusul — a lost Ice Dwarf kingdom in the Tarantar Mountains, overrun by Creatures of Olum in year 268 of the 2nd Epoch and never found again
