Kingdoms of Edura

3rd Epoch — 432 AW

Edura is considerably smaller than Alios and holds only one confirmed kingdom in the present day. See Continents for its geography.

  • T'sarra — High Elves, fairly reclusive, home of the secretive Var Harma. Tropical Rain Forest biome — lush and warm. Ruler and internal politics undeveloped.

1st Epoch — the Drasian Empire

Edura was also the heartland of the Drasu civilization before the Sundering. The Drasian Empire, centered on the city of Atral along the Waywin River, was the first and greatest Drasian city. Its ruins are now excavated by the High Elves and the Var Harma, rumored to sit directly above the Drasian Vault.
