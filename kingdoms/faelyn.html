Overview

Faelyn spans the eastern and lower western arm of Alios and is home to the Guardians of the Tree and the Heaven's Oak chamber — a containment structure built by Adomorn early in the 2nd Epoch to guard against the Abaculus.

The Elf War (387–392 AW)

Faelyn was the target of a sustained two-front assault by Dark Elves and Stone Giants under Diquyk Lockwood's command, directed at breaking the Guardians and compromising the Heaven's Oak chamber. The war culminated in a final battle inside The Deep in 392 AW, ending in Diquyk's banishment at the cost of all ten Marauders.
