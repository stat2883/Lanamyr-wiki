All Kingdoms

3rd Epoch — 432 AW

The political map of present-day Lanamyr. Individual kingdom pages will be built out over time — Faelyn is the first.

Alios

Kimora — Hillfolk humans, capital Shatazaar, ruled by King Gralvin Sutton. Fardar — Iron Dwarves, one of the richest kingdoms in Lanamyr. Ellendale — Plains Elves. Faelyn — Wood Elves, capital Caldalus, home of the Guardians of the Tree. Hellar — Dark Dwarves, an underground city in the Forbidden Peaks. Andar — Northmen humans, the great northern peninsula.

Edura

T'sarra — High Elves, fairly reclusive, home of the secretive Var Harma.

Utoa

Felgard — Hillfolk humans, capital Jorvik, long allied with the Cobalt Dragonborne.

Island kingdoms

Icefall — Ice Dwarves, home of Ice Forging. Jokul — Silver Dragonborne. Malulo — Sea Elves, mostly underwater. Myst — Sky Gnomes, home of the Nexus. A still-unnamed Snow Elf kingdom sits on an island east of Utoa.

Lost to history

Harrad — the dominant 2nd Epoch human alliance, dissolved after the Great War; its territory became Kimora, Ellendale, and Fardar. Nusul — a lost Ice Dwarf kingdom in the Tarantar Mountains, overrun by Creatures of Olum in year 268 of the 2nd Epoch and never found again.
