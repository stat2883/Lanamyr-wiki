Island Kingdoms

3rd Epoch — 432 AW

Each significant island or archipelago outside the three major continents is controlled entirely by a single race and kingdom.

  • Icefall — Ice Dwarves, home of Ice Forging
  • Jokul — Silver Dragonborne
  • Malulo — Sea Elves, mostly underwater
  • Myst — Sky Gnomes, home of the Nexus
  • Snow Elves — kingdom name undecided, an island east of Utoa
