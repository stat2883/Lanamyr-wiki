Kingdoms of Utoa

3rd Epoch — 432 AW

Utoa is the only major continent entirely surrounded by ocean, and holds only one confirmed kingdom in the present day. See Continents for its geography.

  • Felgard — Hillfolk humans, capital Jorvik, confirmed surviving into 432 AW. Allied with the Cobalt Dragonborne for over 260 years. Steppes and Cool Desert biome.

The Magni Arias Graymist was born in Felgard in 2059 (2nd Epoch) and created the Harbinger Set here. His son Cyrus Graymist remained in Felgard, brokering the peace treaty with the Cobalt Dragonborne that ended their raids by 2114; his tomb sits near Jorvik.
