The Monowi Inn

Monowi Hills Kimora–Ellendale border
Location
Monowi Hills, near the Kimora–Ellendale border
Run by
Elsie Varis and her daughter, Alita Varis
Also known as
"Elsie's place"

Overview

A small inn built next to Elsie Varis's farmhouse in the Monowi Hills, on the natural border line between Faelyn and Kimora. It is one of the most well known and frequently visited stops along the road between Fardar and Faelyn.

Elsie has hosted many notable travelers without knowing who they were — including Mya Li and Adomorn the Immortal — and treated each of them like any other guest passing through.
