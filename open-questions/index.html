Open Questions

Undecided lore

Not everything in Lanamyr is settled. This page tracks the major undecided threads at a broad level — items move here when raised and out again once resolved into the appropriate page. It will grow more granular over time.

The Abaculus & the road to a second Great War

The specific mechanism of Greyhawk's curse and the ritual Diquyk needs to resurrect him are undeveloped, as is whether Karma would survive that resurrection. Diquyk's path to godhood is entirely uncharted. Whether Adomorn suffered a physical death immediately after banishing Diquyk in 392 AW is tentative and unconfirmed.

Organizations & politics

The internal structure and current leadership of the Guardians of the Tree and the Var Harma are undeveloped, as is whether the Var Harma have found — and understood — the Drasian Vault. Leadership of the Herringers after Grimmon's death is undecided, as are the internal politics of Felgard and T'sarra.

Races & geography still taking shape

The Snow Elf island and kingdom have no name yet, nor does the Marsh Goblin island. The ancient Draak settlement in the Black Mountains of Fardar has no name and its remains are undeveloped. Several Giantkin and Troll subtypes still lack confirmed origins.

Character threads

Estefania's awareness of her own Harrad lineage is undecided, as is her narrative function going forward. The circumstances that brought Balric Silverfox to Mya Li as a companion are undecided. The nature of Valeria's supernatural presence in Gralvin's life is left open.

See the full working bible for the complete, granular list of undecided items — this page surfaces only the broad threads.
