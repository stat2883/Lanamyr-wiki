Cosmology & Magic

Confirmed lore

Lanamyr is a created world — built by the N'du Irridae, with the participation of some Itule Irridae, for a single purpose: to hide the Abaculus, a sentient, indestructible crystal that craves the destruction of all existence. Every living thing on Lanamyr, and the ambient magical energy that fills the world, exists in service of that concealment, whether or not anyone alive knows it.

The Irridae — the gods

The Irridae are higher-dimensional beings of pure energy, divided into three factions: the N'du (Good/Life), the Itule (Neutral/Balance), and the Onwu (Evil/Death). To meaningfully act within a single plane of existence like Lanamyr, an Irridae must descend and bind themselves to it — a process that sharply limits their power. All Irridae who bound themselves to Lanamyr were destroyed at the Battle of Atral; none remain in the world today.

Virtually everyone in Lanamyr — including devout clergy — understands the gods only through faith, doctrine, and tradition. The true nature of the Irridae, the three-faction conflict, and the existence of the Abaculus are unknown to essentially all living people. Genuine faith is entirely possible, and meaningful, without any of that knowledge.

Ambient energy and the magic system

All magic users draw on an ambient energy that permeates everything in Lanamyr — harvested by the Irridae from other, lifeless planes of existence during the world's creation. Magni — an exceedingly rare fluke of birth that can occur in any race — tap into this energy more easily, without spells or rituals.

The ambient energy generated by all living things doubles as a vast masking field, hiding the Abaculus's location from detection. This is the reason Creatures of Olum exist at all: their purpose is to destroy life and thin that field.

The Abaculus

A sentient, indestructible crystal of unknown origin, possibly as old as the universe itself. It cannot be destroyed by any known means, can consume Relics of Creation to erase what they represent from existence, and corrupts anyone who touches it. It wants to be found — its pattern is to identify an exceptionally powerful being, corrupt them, and use them as a weapon. See the full Abaculus entry for details on its capabilities, history, and current status.
