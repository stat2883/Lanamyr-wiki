1st Epoch Timeline

The Drasu and Draak Year 0 – 1334

The age of the Drasu and Draak, the two civilizations created directly by the Irridae. Ends at the Battle of Atral and the Sundering. See The Three Epochs for the broader overview.

Year 0
Creation of Lanamyr. The N'du Irridae are the primary architects, with some Itule participating, built in secret from the Onwu. All life on Lanamyr — from the Drasu and Draak down to the smallest creature — is created. The Drasu and Draak civilizations are established.
Year 70
The N'du depart. The Abaculus is hidden in the Black Mountains of Fardar, heart of Draak Territory, as a temporary measure. The Draak agree to actively monitor and protect it. Adomorn leads the operation. The Onwu, having discovered Lanamyr, bind themselves to the plane and begin searching for the Abaculus.
Year 245
The city of Atral is founded on Edura. The Drasian Vault is built underground at its center.
Year 1181
Onwu Irridae arrive at Atral. Olum creates the first Creatures of Olum.
Year 1334
Ah'Tabai returns to Lanamyr to prepare for the Onwu threat and retrieves the Abaculus from the Black Mountains, intending to weaponize it. He is corrupted on contact but, fighting the corruption with the full force of his cosmic nature, grants Adomorn immortality before being fully claimed. The Abaculus then uses him to destroy every Irridae present. The catastrophic destruction of the Irridae sends a resonant shockwave through their creation — the Sundering — shattering the Drasu and Draak into every modern race, simultaneously, across all of Lanamyr. End of the 1st Epoch.
