2nd Epoch Timeline

The age of kingdoms Year 7 – 2190

The age in which the modern kingdoms of Lanamyr took shape, dominated for most of its length by the human alliance of Harrad. Ends at the Battle of Rohl. See The Three Epochs for the broader overview.

Year 7
Adomorn moves the Abaculus beneath the Tarantar Mountains. Creatures of Olum begin expanding outward from that location over the following centuries. Adomorn begins building the Heaven's Oak chamber with the Wood Elves, choosing fewer than 10 founding families as the Guardians of the Tree.
Year 221
First Ice Dwarf expeditions depart Icefall in multiple directions. One fleet finds the Tempest Peaks and Darkened Slopes and establishes trade routes; another discovers the Tarantar Mountains and their mineral wealth.
Year 239
Ice Dwarves establish Nusul in the Tarantar Mountains. Regular supply runs and labor forces begin.
Year 253
First major Olum encounter at Nusul. The dwarves fight them off and continue working; attacks grow more frequent as the population grows.
Year 261
Major Olum defeat at Nusul. Part of the mines are closed off; reinforcements and permanent guards are sent from Icefall.
Year 268
A mine at Nusul punches through into a subterranean cavern swarming with Olum. The kingdom is overrun and all inhabitants killed. The Ice Dwarves never return.
1206–1214
The Dragon Wars. Harrad is formed under King Eisengol.
Year 1529
Bourbac takes the throne of Harrad. Iron Dwarf enslavement begins; Herringer resistance begins under Arrama Herringer.
Year 1561
Bourbac is assassinated. Evander "Freeman" takes the throne and frees the enslaved Iron Dwarves.
Year 2059
Arias Graymist born in Felgard, Utoa.
Year 2111
The Harbinger Set is created by Arias Graymist. Cobalt Dragonborne raids on Felgard cease by 2114.
Year 2136
Greyhawk finds the Abaculus in the Tarantar Mountains and is corrupted on contact. He relocates it to beneath the grasslands near Rohl and begins building his forces for the Great War.
Year 2140
Arias Graymist dies (age 81). His three sons — Cyrus, Mako, and Talon — inherit the Harbinger Set.
2140–2148
The Felgard–Dragonborne peace treaty is brokered by Cyrus Graymist.
Year 2148
The Great War begins.
Year 2165
Battle of Rohl. Greyhawk is killed; his dying curse, directed by the Abaculus, transforms Karma and binds him to the Burnt Plains. King Morgan, his daughter Victoria, and grandson Nathan are killed. Harrad dissolves and the Common Calendar resets to year 0. End of the 2nd Epoch.
Year 2190
Cyrus Graymist dies (age 86). Entombed near Jorvik.
