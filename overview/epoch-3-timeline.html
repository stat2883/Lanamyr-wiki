3rd Epoch Timeline

After War (AW) 116 AW – 432 AW

The present age, following the fall of Harrad and the reset of the Common Calendar. Present day: 432 AW. See The Three Epochs for the broader overview.

116 AW
Valindra and Cyran, young Plains Elves from a nomadic settlement in Ellendale, drown when their boat fails far from shore. The lake becomes known as Valindra's Torment.
244 AW
Aer'Raenal Liaphyra born (Wood Elf).
333 AW
Degeric Cent born (Hillfolk human).
347 AW
Mya Li born in Caldalus, Faelyn.
352 AW
Diquyk Lockwood born, central Alios, Hillfolk.
354 AW
Maeglin Leralonde born (High Elf, T'sarra).
355 AW
Tarik Bolden born.
357 AW
Phelloden Liaphyra born.
366 AW
Diquyk kills his parents and town guards with blue flame at age 14. Taken in by Degeric Cent.
369 AW
Galstaff Quint born (human).
372 AW
Grimmon Herringer born (Iron Dwarf).
~376 AW
Diquyk parts from Degeric and begins creating Jaunt Gates.
379–381 AW
Diquyk visits Degeric one last time, still pre-corruption. Degeric dies in 381 AW (age 48), never knowing what his student became.
383 AW
Diquyk, drawn by curiosity to the Burnt Plains, is corrupted by the Abaculus on contact.
383–387 AW
Diquyk recruits the Stone Giants of the Tempest Peaks and the Dark Elves of The Deep to prosecute the coming Elf War.
385 AW
Balric Silverfox born (human).
386 AW
Tarik Bolden takes the throne of Kimora at age 31 and begins raiding villages and enslaving populations.
387 AW
Gralvin Sutton born. The Elf War begins with a two-front assault on Faelyn.
389 AW
Galstaff begins traveling at age 20 and discovers the Nexus on Myst.
392 AW
Diquyk's first major push against the Wood Elves; Kharis Ailwin is killed. The Marauders are formed and the final battle follows within the same year — all ten Marauders die, and Diquyk is banished at physical age 40.
394 AW
Gralvin's village is destroyed by Tarik at age 7; he is taken as a gladiatorial slave.
399 AW
Bor Glitterbeard born (Iron Dwarf, Fardar).
408 AW
Gralvin and Balric escape together and are directed to Valeria.
~408–412 AW
Grimmon Herringer travels to Ellendale and then Faelyn to rally support against Tarik.
413 AW
Tarik is overthrown and killed. Valeria dies in the final battle. Gralvin takes the throne of Kimora at age 26.
427 AW
Diquyk returns to Lanamyr through the Nexus on Myst, physically aged to approximately 41 despite 35 years having passed in Lanamyr.
429 AW
Grimmon Herringer is killed by Orc raiders. Bor obtains the map to the lost kingdom of Nusul from his belongings.
430 AW
The Adenburry sinkhole event: subterranean Creatures of Olum breach the town and are exterminated by a group of adventurers present at the time.
432 AW
Present day. Diquyk pursues Greyhawk's resurrection via Karma at the Burnt Plains.
