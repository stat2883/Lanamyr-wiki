The Three Epochs

Confirmed lore

Lanamyr's history is divided into three ages, each ended by a catastrophic battle that reshaped the world. The present day sits in the 3rd Epoch, year 432 AW.

1st Epoch
The age of the Drasu and Draak. Ended by the Battle of Atral, year 1334.
2nd Epoch
The age of kingdoms. Ended by the Battle of Rohl, year 2165. Calendar reset to year 0.
3rd Epoch
The present age (AW — After War). Present day: 432 AW.

1st Epoch — the Drasu and Draak

Two parallel civilizations, created directly by the Irridae. The Drasu — tall, bronze-skinned, and powerful magic users — built the Drasian Empire on Edura, centered on the city of Atral. The Draak — larger and more powerful ancestors of dragons — held a heartland in the Black Mountains of Fardar. Outside these two territories, the vast majority of Lanamyr was wild and untamed.

The epoch ended in year 1334 at the Battle of Atral, when Ah'Tabai — an N'du Irridae — retrieved the Abaculus intending to weaponize it against the invading Onwu, and was corrupted on contact. The Abaculus used him to destroy every Irridae present.

The Sundering

The catastrophic destruction of the Irridae at Atral sent a resonant shockwave through their creation. Every Drasu and Draak alive at that moment — even those far from Atral — fractured simultaneously into one individual of each descendant race. Every Drasu produced exactly one elf, one human, one dwarf, one gnome, one orc, one goblin, and so on; every Draak produced one of each dragon-descended type. The full moral spectrum of the Drasu and Draak was preserved through the fracture, which is why every modern race carries the capacity for both good and evil.

Newly formed individuals appeared as full physical adults, with no memory beyond the moment of their own creation, in the region that would become their homeland. See All Races for where each race first appeared.

2nd Epoch — the age of kingdoms

The age in which the modern kingdoms of Lanamyr took shape. Harrad, formed in year 1207 under King Eisengol, was the dominant human alliance for most of the epoch. It ended in the Great War (2148–2165), when the black dragon Greyhawk — corrupted by the Abaculus after finding it in the Tarantar Mountains — built an army of dragons, willing and forcibly corrupted, and marched on Harrad. King Morgan and his heirs were killed at the Battle of Rohl in 2165, Harrad dissolved, and the Common Calendar reset to year zero.

3rd Epoch — After War (present day)

The current age. Former Harrad territory is now the kingdoms of Kimora, Ellendale, and Fardar. The present day, 432 AW, follows the Elf War (387–392 AW) and the recent return of Diquyk Lockwood, who is working to resurrect Greyhawk from beneath the Burnt Plains.
