Draak & Descendent Races

1st Epoch Confirmed lore

The Draak

Most closely resembled present-day dragons, but larger and more powerful, and capable of flight. Intelligent and capable of magic, though not to the extent of the Drasu. Their central heartland was the Black Mountains of Fardar (Draak Territory), where they maintained a settlement in the river valley dividing its eastern and western halves — not a city in the Drasian sense, but a gathering place of natural caves and peaks modified to suit their size. The Draak were active, willing participants in guarding the Abaculus during its first hiding place in these same mountains, lending their strength, flight, and magic, until Ah'Tabai retrieved it prior to the Battle of Atral. Like the Drasu, the potential for both good and evil existed within them, and all were shattered in the Sundering that followed.

Dragons

Direct descendants of the Draak, divided into Chromatic and Metallic factions.

Dragonborne

  • Gold — eastern Alios
  • Cobalt — Desolate Tops, Utoa
  • Silver — Jokul
  • Obsidian — southwestern Alios swamps

Kobolds

  • Mystic — southern Alios, the most magically capable
  • Tunneler — Black Mountains of Fardar
  • Scavenger — southern Edura
  • Hardback — Evergreen Strand, Utoa
