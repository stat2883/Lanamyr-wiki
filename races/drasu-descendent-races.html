Drasu & Descendent Races

1st Epoch Confirmed lore

The Drasu

Humanoid, larger than any present-day race — tall, bronze-skinned, lean and powerful. Superior intelligence and magic users. The Drasu built the Drasian Empire on Edura and the Drasian Vault beneath the city of Atral. The potential for both good and evil existed within them; the Sundering revealed and amplified rather than created these qualities — see the linked section for the map of where each race first appeared. Adomorn is the last surviving Drasu.

Elves

  • Wood — Faelyn
  • High — T'sarra
  • Plains — Ellendale
  • Snow — unnamed island east of Utoa
  • Dark — The Deep
  • Sea — Malulo, mostly underwater

Dwarves

  • Iron — Fardar
  • Dark — Hellar
  • Sand — Void Sands

Gnomes

  • Sky — Myst
  • Cave — beneath the Tarantar Mountains

  • Woodland — southern Edura
  • Desert — central-south Alios

Humans

  • Hillfolk — Kimora, the baseline standard
  • Northmen — Andar
  • Shepherds — a distant eastern island, deep animal affinity
  • Water Whisperers — unmatched shipbuilders

Others

  • Orcs — eastern Alios
  • Goblins — three subtypes: Screecher, Marsh, Feral
  • Giantkin — Stone Giants confirmed; Frost, Hill, Swamp Giants and Ogres undeveloped
  • Trolls — several subtypes; Mountain Trolls confirmed
  • Magni — an exceedingly rare magical fluke that can occur in any race
