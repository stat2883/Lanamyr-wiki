All Races

Confirmed lore

Every race in Lanamyr today descends from either the Drasu or the Draak, torn apart into one individual of each descendant race during the Sundering. Individual race pages will be built out over time — Wood Elves is the first. Below is the full family tree.

Drasu-descended races

Elves

  • Wood — Faelyn
  • High — T'sarra
  • Plains — Ellendale
  • Snow — unnamed island east of Utoa
  • Dark — The Deep
  • Sea — Malulo, mostly underwater

Dwarves

  • Iron — Fardar
  • Dark — Hellar
  • Sand — Void Sands
  • Ice — Icefall, home of Ice Forging

Gnomes

  • Sky — Myst
  • Cave — beneath the Tarantar Mountains
  • Rock — Coldwind Mountains
  • Woodland — southern Edura
  • Desert — central-south Alios

Humans

  • Hillfolk — Kimora, the baseline standard
  • Northmen — Andar
  • Shepherds — a distant eastern island, deep animal affinity
  • Water Whisperers — unmatched shipbuilders

Others

  • Orcs — eastern Alios
  • Goblins — three subtypes: Screecher, Marsh, Feral
  • Giantkin — Stone Giants confirmed; Frost, Hill, Swamp Giants and Ogres undeveloped
  • Trolls — several subtypes; Mountain Trolls confirmed
  • Magni — an exceedingly rare magical fluke that can occur in any race

Draak-descended races

Dragons

Direct descendants of the Draak, divided into Chromatic and Metallic factions.

Dragonborne

  • Gold — eastern Alios
  • Cobalt — Desolate Tops, Utoa
  • Silver — Jokul
  • Obsidian — southwestern Alios swamps

Kobolds

  • Mystic — southern Alios, the most magically capable
  • Tunneler — Black Mountains of Fardar
  • Scavenger — southern Edura
  • Hardback — Evergreen Strand, Utoa
