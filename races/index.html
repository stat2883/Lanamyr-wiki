All Races

Confirmed lore

Every race in Lanamyr today descends from either the Drasu or the Draak, torn apart into one individual of each descendant race during the Sundering. Individual race pages will be built out over time — Wood Elves is the first. Below is the full family tree.

Drasu-descended races

Elves — Wood (Faelyn), High (T'sarra), Plains (Ellendale), Snow (unnamed island east of Utoa), Dark (The Deep), and Sea (Malulo, mostly underwater).

Dwarves — Iron (Fardar), Dark (Hellar), Sand (Void Sands), and Ice (Icefall, home of Ice Forging).

Gnomes — Sky (Myst), Cave (beneath the Tarantar Mountains), Rock (Coldwind Mountains), Woodland (southern Edura), and Desert (central-south Alios).

Humans — Hillfolk (Kimora, the baseline standard), Northmen (Andar), Shepherds (a distant eastern island, deep animal affinity), and Water Whisperers (unmatched shipbuilders).

Others — Orcs (eastern Alios), three Goblin subtypes (Screecher, Marsh, Feral), Giantkin (Stone Giants confirmed; Frost, Hill, Swamp Giants and Ogres undeveloped), several Troll subtypes (Mountain Trolls confirmed), and Magni — an exceedingly rare magical fluke that can occur in any race.

Draak-descended races

Dragons — direct descendants of the Draak, divided into Chromatic and Metallic factions.

Dragonborne — Gold (eastern Alios), Cobalt (Desolate Tops, Utoa), Silver (Jokul), and Obsidian (southwestern Alios swamps).

Kobolds — Mystic (southern Alios, the most magically capable), Tunneler (Black Mountains of Fardar), Scavenger (southern Edura), and Hardback (Evergreen Strand, Utoa).
