Irridae (Gods of Lanamyr)

Confirmed lore List partial

Beings of pure energy, each with a unique signature, divided into three factions. The Irridae departed Lanamyr early in the 1st Epoch to spread the Onwu thin across multiple worlds; only a small number were ever destroyed at the Battle of Atral, and the majority still exist elsewhere in an ongoing cosmic conflict. See Cosmology & Magic for how they created Lanamyr and why virtually no one in the world knows their true nature.

When an Irridae dies, their domain does not cease to exist — the qualities they governed were woven into the fabric of creation at the beginning and persist independently. Only the Abaculus consuming the corresponding Relic of Creation could permanently erase a quality from existence.

N'du (Good/Life)

  • Algesh — God of the Sun (Warmth, Fire, Rebirth, Hope)
  • Corryn — Goddess of Good (Healing, Mercy)
  • Dey — Goddess of Steel (Strength, Industry, Merchants)
  • Khell — God of Life (Life, Fertility, Healing)
  • Krynn — God of Light (Fate, Future, Truth)

  • Grom — God of the Mountains (Protection, Patience, Resilience)
  • Luna — Goddess of Truth (Knowledge, Scholars, Discipline)
  • Ordun — God of Magic (Magic, Sorcery, Will)
  • Sellier — Goddess of Nature (Balance, Survival, Animals)
  • Sharrdul — God of Chaos (Anarchy, Disaster, Conquerors)
  • Throdd — God of War (Battle, War, Aggression)
  • Tier — God of Death (Forgiveness, Release, Mortality)
  • Tyrn — God of Order (Law, Cleanliness, Kings)
  • Uhgallah — Goddess of the Dark (Blindness, Direction, Answers)

Onwu (Evil/Death)

  • Burrdolm — God of Deceit (Lies, Trickery, Crime)

  • Santos — God of Evil

  • Trillios — God of the Oceans (Anger, Vengeance, Fury)

This list of 20 is partial — remaining Irridae names and domains are undeveloped.

Other named Irridae

Ah'Tabai — N'du Irridae, God of Resurrection and Reincarnation. Returned to Lanamyr to prepare for the incoming Onwu and, in an act of poor judgement, retrieved the Abaculus to weaponize it against them. Corrupted on contact, he resisted long enough — by virtue of his cosmic nature — to grant Adomorn immortality before being fully claimed and used to destroy the other Irridae present at Atral. His death left Resurrection and Reincarnation as ungoverned domains, cosmic qualities that persist without a governing Irridae — possibly the mechanism by which Diquyk will eventually raise Greyhawk, though this is tentative and unconfirmed.
