Other Forms of Life

Confirmed lore Largely undeveloped

All life on Lanamyr — every race, every creature, every plant — was created by the N'du Irridae, with the Itule contributing in a lesser capacity, as part of the same act of creation that produced the Drasu and Draak. Ordinary wildlife, plants, and lesser creatures were made individually by a single Irridae with comparatively little effort, unlike the Drasu and Draak, which required many Irridae working together. This is precisely why the Sundering that shattered the Drasu and Draak left the rest of the natural world mostly unaffected — a rabbit, a tree, an ordinary fish simply didn't carry the collective creative weight needed to be torn apart the same way.

Creatures of Olum

Created by the god Olum by corrupting living creatures. Sickly gray-to-black with glowing red eyes, they are not truly living — manifestations of death rather than a race in their own right. Their primary directive is to destroy life, reducing the ambient magical energy that masks the Abaculus's location. Source creatures are drawn primarily from wildlife — bears, deer, boars, and similar animals are most common — though intelligent races can also be taken if they stray too close to a nest. They age into large, tentacled pack leaders that form birthing chambers, and dissolve to harmless black dust on death.

Undeveloped

Specific named wildlife, flora, and fauna across Lanamyr's many biomes — from the Tropical Rain Forests of T'sarra to the Polar Ice of Icefall — have not yet been detailed individually. This page will grow as that material is developed.
