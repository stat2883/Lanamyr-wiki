Overview

All elf subtypes share pointed ears and more delicate features than most other races, tending toward leaner, more slender builds than Hillfolk humans. Elves have finite lifespans — they are not immortal, and elderly elves exist in the world.

Wood Elves are native to Faelyn, spanning the eastern reach of Alios and the lower western arm. Their kingdom is home to the Heaven's Oak chamber and the secretive Guardians of the Tree, who protect it.

Notable Wood Elves

  • Mya Li — Wood Elf princess, leader of the Marauders during the Elf War
  • Aer'Raenal Liaphyra — King of the Wood Elves, father of Mya and Phelloden
  • Phelloden Liaphyra — Heir to the throne of Faelyn
