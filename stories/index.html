Story Outlines

In development

Lanamyr's narrative work is developed alongside its lore. This page tracks story concepts at a broad level; full chapter-by-chapter outlines will get their own pages over time.

Unlit (working title)

3rd Epoch, 366–392 AW. A parallel story following Mya Li and Diquyk Lockwood from their youth through the full course of the Elf War, told in alternating chapter blocks and ending from Mya's point of view. The reader is assumed to know nothing of Lanamyr going in — the world is revealed entirely through what the characters live through. Outline confirmed; several chapters substantially developed.

The Shadow Before the Storm

2nd Epoch, the lead-up to and early years of the Great War. An unnamed group of adventurers navigating a world beginning to darken — Olum in places they shouldn't be, trade routes going quiet. Concept only.

The Reluctant King

3rd Epoch, 387–413 AW. The story of Gralvin Sutton, from the destruction of his village at age seven through fourteen years in the gladiatorial arena to the overthrow of Tarik the Ravager — a crown he never wanted, and the person he loved most lost in taking it. Broad shape confirmed; scenes undeveloped.

Organizations & orders

Several organizations recur across these stories and the wider history of Lanamyr.

Guardians of the Tree

An ancient hereditary Wood Elf secret order from Faelyn, founded by Adomorn early in the 2nd Epoch to protect Heaven's Oak and the Abaculus containment chamber within it. Answers only to Adomorn.

The Var Harma (the Curators)

A secretive High Elf society from T'sarra, formed to protect what the excavation of Atral uncovered. Operates on a need-to-know basis; only its highest ranks have any contact with Adomorn.

The Marauders

An elite ten-strong Wood Elf unit assembled by Mya Li for the final incursion of the Elf War. All ten were lost in the final battle that banished Diquyk.

The Herringers

An Iron Dwarf family defined by cause rather than blood, twice organizing resistance against tyranny in Fardar and Kimora — first against Bourbac, later rebuilt by Grimmon Herringer against Tarik.
