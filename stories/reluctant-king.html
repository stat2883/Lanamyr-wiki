The Reluctant King

Untitled 3rd Epoch, 387–413 AW Concept only
Protagonist
Gralvin Sutton
Status
Broad shape confirmed; scenes undeveloped

Concept

The story of Gralvin Sutton from his earliest years to the overthrow of Tarik the Ravager. Beginning with the destruction of his village at age seven, through fourteen years in the arena, to his escape alongside Balric Silverfox, his discovery of Valeria and her network, the years of quietly helping escaped slaves while gathering worse and worse reports, and finally the arrival of Mya Li and the coordinated overthrow that cost him the person he loved most. The story ends where the legend begins — Gralvin, age 26, being handed a crown he never wanted.

Key unresolved questions

  • The specific circumstances that drew Gralvin and Balric together within the slave system
  • The names and faces of people Gralvin fought in the arena
  • The texture of Valeria's network and the world after escape
  • The full shape of the coordinated overthrow and the final battle in which Valeria died
