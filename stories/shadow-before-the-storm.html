The Shadow Before the Storm

Untitled 2nd Epoch Concept only

Set during the lead-up to and early years of the Great War. An unnamed group of adventurers navigates a world beginning to darken around them — Creatures of Olum appearing in places they shouldn't, trade routes going quiet, people disappearing. Adomorn and Karma may appear. A ground-level view of the Great War era, in contrast to Unlit's close focus on Diquyk and Mya Li in the 3rd Epoch.

Characters, specific plot, and scope are entirely undeveloped. This concept doesn't yet have its own sidebar entry alongside Unlit and The Reluctant King — reachable from those pages until it's developed further.
