Unlit

Working title 3rd Epoch, 366–392 AW In development
POV
Mya Li and Diquyk Lockwood, alternating chapter blocks
Ending
From Mya's point of view
Status
Outline confirmed; several chapters substantially developed

Concept

A parallel story following Mya Li and Diquyk Lockwood from their youth through the full course of the Elf War (387–392 AW), culminating in the final battle inside the Dark Elf kingdom and Diquyk's banishment. Told in alternating chapter blocks — not strictly one chapter per character, but groups of chapters before switching point of view. The reader is assumed to have no prior knowledge of Lanamyr; the world is revealed entirely through the characters' lived experience. Other titles considered: Two Flames, The Same War, Not Yet, Before the Collision.

Narrative structure

Diquyk and Mya each narrate their own chapters in first or close-third person. In the early portions, Diquyk's arc requires more runway — spanning roughly 26 years from age 14 through the Elf War — while Mya's arc spans approximately 5 years from joining the militia, so the grouped block structure lets each character's momentum build before switching rather than constantly interrupting. As the two timelines converge toward the final battle, the blocks shorten and shift more frequently to build pace. The two chapters covering direct encounters between Mya and Diquyk use an unnamed omniscient external narrator, signaling that something has shifted beyond either character's individual perspective.

Three-act shape

  • Act One — Both characters' early years: Diquyk from the incident in 366 AW through his corruption in 383 AW and recruitment of the Stone Giants and Dark Elves; Mya from her restless scholarly years in Caldalus through joining the militia in 387 AW
  • Act Two — The Elf War years, building to the first large encounter as its midpoint crisis — Mya's most crushing defeat, Kharis killed
  • Act Three — Reformation and convergence: Mya rebuilds, forms the Marauders, Adomorn enters the picture, and the final battle ends in Diquyk's banishment

Confirmed lore from this story

  • Mya's original path was to become queen — scholarly, restless, increasingly bored. Phelloden was more enthusiastic, and Mya stepping aside is a deliberate choice, not circumstance
  • Mya and Phelloden share a warm sibling relationship — mutual respect, no rivalry, genuine humor between them
  • Early war years (387–391 AW) were two-front probing attacks; Diquyk directed operations via Jaunt Gates and wasn't on the front lines until 392 AW
  • The first large encounter and the final battle both occur within 392 AW — Kharis's death, the Marauders' formation, and their loss all compressed into the same year
  • Mya knew Adomorn only as a legendary wizard, unaware of his true Drasu nature
  • The white-blue bursts of Diquyk's power in the opening chapter are the same blue flame that defines his magic for the rest of his life
  • The story ends from Mya's point of view — Diquyk's banishment is a threshold for his story, not this book's ending

Key unresolved questions

  • What is Mya thinking about when her mind wanders in her opening chapter?
  • Does Diquyk notice Mya specifically during the first encounter?
  • Who are the ten Marauders, beyond Mya herself? Names and backgrounds undeveloped
  • What did Diquyk experience during the early Elf War years (387–391 AW)?
  • Does the book end with Adomorn's apparent death and Mya standing alone? Tentative
